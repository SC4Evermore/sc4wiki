## About the SC4 Encyclopaedia

The SC4 Encyclopaedia is a free, open-content encyclopaedia for SimCity 4-related information. It aims to provide easy access to up-to-date information on every aspect of the game, with knowledge consolidated, distilled and checked for accuracy by anyone who wants to be involved.

Registration is required to edit.

## Current News

The Encyclopaedia is being migrated here from its old home on SC4Devotion. Articles will appear over the coming weeks. Until then, the [original wiki](https://www.sc4devotion.com/wiki/index.php?title=Main_Page) remains available.
