# SC4 Encyclopaedia

The SimCity 4 Encyclopaedia is a project to consolidate all factual information about SimCity 4 and its community in an easily searchable, impartial environment.

> **We're moving!** The Encyclopaedia is being migrated here from its old home on SC4Devotion. Articles, tutorials and documentation will appear over the coming weeks.
{.is-info}

## In the meantime

- The original wiki remains available at [SC4Devotion](https://www.sc4devotion.com/wiki/index.php?title=Main_Page).
- Visit [SC4Evermore](https://www.sc4evermore.com) for downloads and community news.
