The **Parthenon** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 00D** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Parthenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parthenon) in [Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athens), [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece) as it appeared in 2003.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Parthenon Teaches \#city# a Lesson in Proportion".
