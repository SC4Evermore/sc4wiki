**Natural Growth** features the city "Boston v2" by [CSGdesign](/people/csgdesign), and is captioned *"The study of a city developing under its own natural tendencies"*. The concept behind this journal is to explore how a city would develop if decisions about development were made after role playing the decision making process of the sims, rather than an omnipotent "overview" or "top down [God Mode](/god-mode)" development style. This results in sometimes inefficient decisions being made about development, as well as a relatively small amount of fore-thought in city planning, but results in an interesting and reasonably realistic region view.

Boston v2 was started in May [2008](/2008), being originally published on [Simtropolis](/simtropolis) before moving to [SC4Devotion](/sc4devotion) and finally to [CSGdescussions](/csgdescussions) and [CSGforums](/csgforums). Natural Growth inspired a [string of similar styled journals](/category/cs-natural-growth), although many of them were more self-funded growth rather than Natural Growth, having missed the role-playing element of the style and focused more on the organic development form rather than the reasons behind it.

Natural Growth is still published and [CSGdesign](/people/csgdesign) continues to add entries to this day.
