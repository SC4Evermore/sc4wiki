Argentum was [Tarkus'](/people/tarkus) short-lived first-attempt at entering the world of Mayors' Diaries, begun in April 2007, and closed after only a single full update one month later. It was mirrored both at SC4D and Simtropolis. Like his later MD work, it was focused heavily on road construction projects. The Argentum region was later incorporated as a major focus in Tarkus' second MD, [Tarkusian Cities](/tarkusian-cities), which is still active today.
