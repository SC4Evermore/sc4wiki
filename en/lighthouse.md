The **Lighthouse** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that increases your city's Dirty and Manufacturing Industrial capacities.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "#City# Seeks Lonesome Lighthouse Keeper".

## Unlock Requirements

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Seaport Usage** in excess of **100 Shipped Units**
- (Defacto) A **[Seaport](/seaport) has been constructed in the city**
