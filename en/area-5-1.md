**Area 5.1** (Referred to as the **UFO Base** in its exemplar and as **Area 5.1 \[Top Secret\]** in the game) is a [Business Deal](/business-deals) building that unleashes untold hell upon your city and its citizens.

It has multiple effects designed to drive away Residential and Commercial development, at least in the immediate area, and is capable of completely negating the effect of Police Stations in a Small City Tile. In the Vanilla game, you merely need to play badly on the Hard difficulty setting, however [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) modified the unlock properties so it can be a trophy for evil and vindictive players. Rush Hour also added several exotic U Drive It vehicles like the UFO and Metal Whale unlockable with this lot, as well as making it attainable on the lower difficulty settings through U-Drive It missions.

As with all Business Deals, demolishing the building is an incredibly expensive action.

### Gameplay

#### Easy Difficulty

Cannot be unlocked through gameplay.

#### Medium Difficulty

Cannot be unlocked through gameplay.

#### Hard Difficulty

- **Combined Residential, Commercial and Industrial Population** in excess of **15,500 Sims**
- **Available Cash less than §20,000**
- **Available Cash** now **less than** what it was **4 Months ago - §3,000**
- **Monthly Income less than Monthly Expenses**

- Icecream Truck Mission: Pure Evil Ice Cream
- Steam Train Mission: Sell Secret Rocket Formula
- Crop Dusting Mission: Spread Zombie Dust
