The **Park Effect** is an effect emitted by [lots](/lot) that increases nearby Residential desirability. Placing a lot that emits the Park Effect (such as a Park) will usually result in higher wealth and density Residential development nearby.

When defining the Park Effect for a lot, one specifies the radius over which the effect acts, and the intensity of the effect.

The Park Effect is similar to the [Landmark Effect](/landmark-effect), which acts on Commercial properties.

## Property Information

ID: 0x27812850

Type: Sint32

Reps: 2 (magnitude, radius in cells)
