**Crime** is a condition that is caused by not providing enough well-paid jobs or a quality [educational system](/education). One way to combat crime immediately is by building a strong police force. The crime [Data View](/data-view) shows where criminal clusters are, and can help you in the strategic placement of the [police stations](/police-station).
