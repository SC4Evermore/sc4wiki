## January

- 2nd: The wiki is plagued by an edit war, and is temporarily suspended. It was re-opened on January 4th.
- 14th: 24th [NAM](/nam) release, coinciding with the release of [RHW](/rhw) Version 3.0 and a fix to the [HSRP](/hsrp) mod, to mark the 6th anniversary of the release of [SC4](/sc4).

## February

- 1st: The [2008 Trixie Award Winners](/2008-trixie-award-winners) are announced at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis).
- 10th: [Battlecat](/people/battlecat) starts the [SC4D HoF](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame) MD [Adara](/adara).

## March

- 7th: 25th [NAM](/nam) release, coinciding with the release of [RAM](/ram) Single Track Rail, the [Rural Roads](/rural-roads) Plugin and [RHW](/rhw) Version 3.2.

## April

## May

- 14th: [Yan077](/people/yan077) starts the [SC4D HoF](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame) MD [Corsica](/corsica).

## June

- 21st: 26th [NAM](/nam) release, marks the introduction of the [Canal Addon Mod](/canal-addon-mod). Reintroduction of "update" files.

## July

- 18th: 27th [NAM](/nam) release, primarily a bugfixing release.

## August

- 30th: [io_bg](/people/io-bg) starts the [SC4D HoF](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame) MD [The Region of Pirgos](/the-region-of-pirgos).

## September

- [EBLT Team](/eblt-team) is founded.

## October

- 9th: [ITC Team](/itc-team) is moved from Other Team to Certified Team on [SC4D](/sc4d).
- 20th: [EBLT Team](/eblt-team) receives team board at [SC4D](/sc4d).

## November

- 6th: [SC4PIM](/sc4pim), also known as PIM-X or X-Tool, is finally released to the LEX.
- 9th: [Team Downunder](/team-downunder) is founded by [Joelyboy911](/people/joelyboy911), [adroman](/people/adroman) and [dobdriver](/people/dobdriver).

## December

- 25th: the 2009 [Trixies](/trixie) have been awarded at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis).
