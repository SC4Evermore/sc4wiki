The German **SimCityKurier** is the download area of [SimCityPlaza](/simcityplaza), a large German fansite dedicated to SimCity. It provides a large variety of downloads, which have been translated into German, checked, updated and tested by the [SFBT](/sfbt) Team before the upload, in order to make sure that they work properly and integrate into the game perfectly. Nearly all uploads are packaged with an installer, and everyone comes with a comprehensive ReadMe file, in order to ease the installation process. The files are sorted into several categories and subcategories, along with some special links like "new files", "top 15 files", and a full text search feature. As of September 2008, SimCityKurier offers about 1000 different files.
