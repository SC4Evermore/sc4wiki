The **Movie Studio** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that serves as an icon of good overall Residential management and growth in your city.

The Movie Studio is one of the cheapest rewards in SimCity 4 Rush Hour/Deluxe, being completely free, however constructing it should be considered akin to taking out a business deal: It uses a lot of land which may be required in a city of 110,000+ Residents, it has a reasonable drain on resources, little actual benefit and a massive demolition cost.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Stars Glitter, Sims Atwitter At New \#City Name# Movie Studio".

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **110,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **52 Points**
