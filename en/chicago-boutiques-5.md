**Chicago Boutiques 5** (Named in the game as just **[Boutiques](/boutiques)**) is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on only High Density Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic High-Wealth stores grouped under the Boutiques name.

[Hedman Planners](/hedman-planners) uses the same model sets and can be considered a Commercial Offices variant of this building.
