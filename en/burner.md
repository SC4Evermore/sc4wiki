[Burner](/burner) is a Dirty Industrial Machinery Building that grows on Medium and High density Industrial Zones[^1].

Machinery Buildings are grown by SimCity 4's Industrial Zones attached to an Anchor Lot and are designed to expand the base lot with major machinery installations. Machinery buildings are the second type of Industry building to be grown, preceded by [Anchor Lots](/category/i-ht-growables/anchors) and followed by [Outbuildings](/category/i-ht-growables/outbuildings).

## [Colossus Addon Mod](/colossus-addon-mod)

When the Colossus Addon Mod is installed, the lot is promoted to Stage 5 and the number of jobs is increased to 40. It is otherwise unchanged[^2].

**Burner** is a Dirty Industrial Machinery Building that grows on Medium and High density Industrial Zones[^3].

Machinery buildings are the second type of Industry building to be grown, preceded by [Anchor Lots](/category/i-ht-growables/anchors) and followed by [Outbuildings](/category/i-ht-growables/outbuildings).

## [Colossus Addon Mod](/colossus-addon-mod)
