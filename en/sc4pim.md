**SC4PIM**, also known as **PIM-X** or the **X-Tool** is a tool, created by [wouanagaine](/people/wouanagaine), that can be used as a replacement for the [Maxis](/maxis) [Plugin Manager](/plugin-manager) and [Lot Editor](/lot-editor) among some other applications. It has taken a long time to develop, but was eventually released on November 6, [2009](/2009), 3 days after the [PIM-X User Guide](http://sc4devotion.com/csxlex/lex_filedesc.php?lotGET=2257) was released by [BarbyW](/people/barbyw).
