The **Gateway Arch** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Gateway Arch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gateway_Arch) in [St Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Louis), [Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), completed in 1965.
