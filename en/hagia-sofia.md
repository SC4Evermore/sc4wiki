**Hagia Sofia** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Hagia Sophia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hagia_Sophia) in [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul), [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey), completed in 537.
