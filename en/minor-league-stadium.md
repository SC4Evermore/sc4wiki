The **Minor League Stadium** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Beautification and Recreation for your [Sims](/sims).

Parks provide a [Park Effect](/park-effect) over a fixed radius, in addition to usually cleaning up air and water pollution and increasing [CAPs](/cap). In addition, the **Minor League Stadium** provides an additional Landmark Effect over a small radius and large CAP relief. However, it carries a Park Effect penalty.

The Minor League Stadium is the third most expensive Park in SimCity 4 Rush Hour/Deluxe, behind the [City Zoo](/city-zoo) and above the [Softball Field](/softball-field) in Costs.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "New Minor League Stadium Provokes Major Sim Excitement".

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **22,500 Sims**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **20 Points**
- **At least 16 other [Park Department Buildings](/category/park-buildings) built**
