**Bank of America** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [555 California Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/555_California_Street) in [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California), [United States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America), completed in 1969.

The name of the building in real life does not match that of the game as the building was renamed from **Bank of America Center** to **555 California Street** when it was purchased by a private enterprise in 2005, two years after SimCity 4 was released.

## Trivia

It was quite possibly included as a jab at Transamerica for threatening legal action over SimCity 3000's free downloadable version of [Transamerica Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transamerica_Tower).
