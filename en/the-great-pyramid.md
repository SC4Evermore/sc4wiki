**The Great Pyramid** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Great Pyramid of Giza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Pyramid_of_Giza) in the [Giza Necropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giza_Necropolis), [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt), completed sometime around 2560BCE.
