**Chicago Boutiques 3** (Named in the game as just **[Boutiques](/boutiques)**) is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on Medium Density and High Density Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic High-Wealth stores grouped under the Boutiques name.

[Kearney LLC](/kearney-llc) uses the same model sets and can be considered a Commercial Offices variant of this building.

## Trivia

As exemplar the name for this building is ChiLuxuryStore, it's highly likely that this building was only ever meant to be available in the Chicago tileset.[^1]
