**Tarkusian Cities**, is, as the author puts it, an [MD](/md) that is *"always under construction"*. Begun on June 17, [2007](/2007), it is follows the travails of various transportation and land use authorities in several regions that [Tarkus](/people/tarkus) maintains, and numerous road construction projects, often complemented with frequent maps. Many of these road improvements also prominently feature as-of-yet unreleased transit modifications [Tarkus](/people/tarkus) is working for the [NAM Team](/nam-team), such as new [RHW](/rhw) and [NWM](/nwm) features.

To date, six total regions have been shown, Cascadia Gorge/Wakeman, Argentum (a carryover from Tarkus' previous MD/CJ attempt, [Argentum](/argentum)), Los Endos, and Lenox, Chemeketa, and New Argentum. Except for the more verdant Lenox and Chemeketa, these regions are situated in an arid or semi-arid climate, often featuring Tarkus' own <!-- unconverted template: plugin -->, which was developed specifically for use in the MD.

A few tutorials have been shown in Tarkusian Cities as well, including a tutorial on using the [NAM](/nam) [Road Curve Puzzle Pieces](/fractional-angle-project) (Update 3) and a tutorial showing how Tarkus makes his road maps (Update 21).

Despite being updated rather infrequently and its rather specialized approach, it remains one of the most viewed MDs at SC4D, reaching nearly 225,000 views since its opening. It has been [OSITM](/ositm) on four occasions, first in October [2007](/2007), and was inducted into the [MD Hall of Fame](/sc4d-mayor-and#39-s-diary-hall-of-fame) in December [2009](/2009). The less active ST edition of Tarkusian Cities won the 2011 [Trixie Award](/trixie-award) for "Best Roadgeek City Journal", with over 90,000 views.
