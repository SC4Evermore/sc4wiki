The **Large Police Station** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides [Police Protection](/crime) for your [Sims](/sims).

Police Stations reduce crime by a variable amount dependent on their funding over a variable radius which can be adjusted using the Vehicle Funding slider in the building's Query Panel up until there are no more unused jail cells.

The Large Police Station is the second most expensive and effective Crime-fighting solution in SimCity 4 Rush Hour/Deluxe (The most effective and expensive in the base game), with an increased monthly cost, more dispatchable cars, a stronger crime-deterring effect and a wider coverage area than the [Small Police Station](/small-police-station).

It is positioned above the [Small Police Station](/small-police-station) and below the [Deluxe Police Station](/deluxe-police-station) in Crime-fighting power and facilities. The Small Police Station is a direct downgrade and the Deluxe Police Station a direct upgrade.

## Bugs

It produces electricity. It probably should not do that.

## Trivia

The original name for this building was probably the **36-Car Local Precinct** before being changed to **Large Police Station** as its exemplar still contains this name. This possibly happened because it doesn't have 36 dispatchable cars.
