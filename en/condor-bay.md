### Region Overview

**Condor Bay** is a relatively small region (as large as 2x2 large cities), authored by [Masochist](/people/masochist), consisting of a valley surrounded by mountains and set amidst three major bodies of water: Condor Bay, Willow Bay and the Ace River. The entire region, when finished, will be comprised of one fairly large city: Fordville, the capital of the fictional state Willow.

The [City Showcase](/city-showcase) is known for its large updates - there are regularly 20+ pictures, each usually having at least a paragraph describing what is going on - It's also noted for its long periods between updates (usually about a month), as well as its progressive building style. The most well-known part of Condor Bay is perhaps Condor Beach, which remains its most aesthetically pleasing feature.

### History

"Welcome To Condor Bay" began on New Year's Day (Jan. 1st) of [2008](/2008) with its first post involving the author finding a fictional journal. Since then, the city has progressed to a mid-sized city, encompassing a large seaport and aforementioned Condor Beach (among other beaches). In Feb. '08, Condor Bay was named a [Mayor's Diary](/mayor-and#39-s-diary) "[Of Special Interest This Month](/of-special-interest-this-month)" on [SC4D](/sc4d).
