**Houston Boutiques 4** (Named in the game as just **[Boutiques](/boutiques)**) is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that only grows on High Density Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic High-Wealth stores grouped under the Boutiques name.

## Trivia

This is one of the CS§§§ buildings that feature product placement, usually for The Sims or for its third expansion pack, The Sims: Hot Date.
