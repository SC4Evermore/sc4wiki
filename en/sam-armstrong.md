**Sam Armstrong** is the [Public Safety](/public-safety) Advisor in [SimCity 4](/simcity-4). His role is to advise the player on the state of their [Police](/police) and [Fire](/fire) departments.

## Regional Differences

### Korea/Thailand/Taiwan/Hong Kong (SKU 3) and China (SKU 4)

It is understood that the Advisors were changed in these versions, but their names are not known and the only evidence is blurry screenshots.

### Japan (SKU 5)

**Sam Aaron** (セム アロン) replaces Sam Armstrong as the [Public Safety](/public-safety) Advisor in the Japanese version of SimCity 4.
