The **SC4D Thematic Competition** is a competition held at, as the name suggests, [SimCity 4 Devotion](/simcity-4-devotion). It was inspired by the Dorfwettbewerb at [SimForum](/simforum) and is meant to allow players to create the same theme with their own input. Unlike the [Picture Competition](/picture-competition), the contestant will be able to show more than one picture.
