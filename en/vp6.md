**VP6** or **VP60** is the video format that [SimCity (2013)](/simcity-2013) uses to store in-game video. It is named after the codec used to encode it, *Electronic Arts VP60 Video Codec*.

These files are used in a myriad of other EA Games like [Command and Conquer 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Command_and_Conquer_3) and [Dante's Inferno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dante%27s_Inferno).

Its [DBPF](/dbpf) 3.0 [Type ID](/type-id) is **376840D7**
