The **Magnificent Mayor's Statue** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that is very desirable to both residential and commercial sims.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Mayor \#Mayor name# Immortalized In Bronze".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03740000 Statue 3 - Male](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03740000-statue-3-male), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **60,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **60 Points**
