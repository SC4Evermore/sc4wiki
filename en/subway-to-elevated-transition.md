The **Subway-to-Elevated Transition** is a Transport building that allows [Subway](/subway) and [Elevated Rail](/elevated-rail) services to integrate and form one network. It was added in the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack.

It is designed to be placed on top of both a stretch of Subway Network as well on a stub of Elevated Rail, allowing your Sims to transfer from expensive Subways to cheaper Elevated Rail.
