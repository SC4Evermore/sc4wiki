The **TSC Orange Trophy** is a contest, organised by [Orange_o_](/people/orange-o), that is held simultaneously at [TSC](/tsc-website) and [SC4 Devotion](/sc4-devotion). It challenges [Lotters](/lot) to create a [lot](/lot) according to the specifications given by [Orange_o_](/people/orange-o). The winner of each contest can choose a building, which [Orange_o_](/people/orange-o) will then [BAT](/bat), as a reward.
