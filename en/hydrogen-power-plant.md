The **Hydrogen Power Plant** is a utility building that produces Power for your [Sims](/sims).

Power Plants plants are designed to be connected to zones and buildings with [Power Lines](/transit-network#powerlines), but do not have to be as they provide power to other buildings within a three-tile radius and those buildings may relay that power to other buildings in another three-tile radius and so on.

The Hydrogen Power Plant is the most expensive power solution in SimCity 4 initially, but its low cost per MWh/month makes it only slightly dearer than [Oil](/oil-power-plant) in the long run, however its lofty unlock requirements and high outright monthly cost place it out of reach for all but the largest of cities and conurbations. The Hydrogen Power Plant is best used to supply not one city, but a group of cities, single-handedly able to power the needs of over one million sims in the right conditions.

It is positioned above the [Nuclear Power Plant](/nuclear-power-plant) in individual power production with no other plant surpassing it. Its compact size, incredible output and reasonable long-term cost make it a no-brainer for any city or group of cities that has it available, can afford it and can use it to its fullest extent.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Jazz Fans Prefer Hydrogen Plant To Less "Hip" Forms of Power".
