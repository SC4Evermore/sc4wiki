The **Passenger Ferry Terminal** is a Transport building that provides Passenger Ferry services, added in the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) expansion pack.

Ferry Terminals are lots that provide ways for passengers and cars to cross bodies of water without a bridge, or across city borders. However, Sims boarding at a Passenger Ferry terminal can only alight at another Passenger Ferry terminal. Passenger Ferry and Car and Passenger Ferry traffic do not mix and exist as two separate virtual networks.

## Beta

![The remnants of the Beta Passenger Ferry Terminal left in the retail release of Rush Hour](/_files/images/beta-passenger-ferry-terminal.png) During the game's Beta phase, the Passenger Ferry Terminal may have had Parking facilities, meaning Sims may have been able to move from Cars to ferries. That was probably removed in favor of the Car and Passenger Ferry.
