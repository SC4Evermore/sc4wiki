**Custom Member Labels** (**CMLs**) are labels often given to members of sites within the community for various reasons, including substantial contributions made to the site or to the game. Each site has their own procedure for awarding a CML, and some sites do not use them at all.

## SimCity 4 Devotion

At [SimCity 4 Devotion](/simcity-4-devotion), CMLs are given out periodically - ranging from monthly to seasonally - to members of the community that have affected the community in a positive way. As of February [2009](/2009), there are 42 members of SC4D with CMLs. As of February 26, 2012, there are 64 members of SC4D with CMLs. A list of them can be found [here (Wiki)](/custom-member-label/sc4dlist) or [here (SC4D)](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=47.msg704#msg704).
