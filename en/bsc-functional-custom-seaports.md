The **BSC Functional Seaports** are a set of custom seaports that are functional, meaning they transport cargo, and provide [industrial CAP relief](/tutorial/demand-supply-and-caps#demand-satisfied). Most of the seaports also have several stages and will automatically upgrade based on demand. The seaports are compatible with the [AC Functional Custom Airports](/ac-functional-custom-airports) as well as third-party custom seaports.

A (nearly) complete guide to their use can be found [at SC4Devotion](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=14336.0) (login required).
