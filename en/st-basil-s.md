**Saint Basil's** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Saint Basil's Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Basil%27s_Cathedral) in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia), completed in 1561.
