## January

- 11th - [SC4 Devotion](/sc4-devotion) opens the Lot Exchange (LEX).
- 17th - [Badsim](/people/badsim) starts the [SC4D HoF](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame) MD [AntigOne](/antigone).
- 25th - [emilin](/people/emilin) starts the [SC4D HoF](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame) MD **Islas Gemas**, which would later become [Blackwater](/blackwater).

## February

- 27th - [rooker1](/people/rooker1) starts the [SC4D HoF](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame) MD [Mind Scape](/mind-scape).

## March

- 30th - [Shadow Assassin](/people/shadow-assassin) starts the SC4D edition of his MD **Ensenada**, which would later become [Whitefall, Iridar](/whitefall-iridar) and part of the [SC4D HoF](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame).

## April

- 14th - [RHW](/rhw) Version 1.3 is released by [Tarkus](/people/tarkus), fixing RHW/NAM incompatibility issues.
- 28th - [RHW](/rhw) Version 1.3a is released to fix a few small bugs with Version 1.3.

## May

- 22nd - [TheTeaCat](/people/theteacat) starts the [SC4D HoF](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame) MD [Tales at Tea Time](/tales-at-tea-time).

## June

- 1st - 21st [NAM](/nam) release, first "modular" NAM release, notable for the addition of Draggable [Ground Light Rail](/ground-light-rail) (GLR), the first [Override Network](/override-network). The [RHW](/rhw) officially becomes a NAM Team project, coinciding with the release of Version 1.3b.
- 17th - [Tarkus](/people/tarkus) starts the [SC4D HoF](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame) MD [Tarkusian Cities](/tarkusian-cities).
- 19th - [SimCity DS](/simcity-ds) is released in the US.

## July

- 31st - The [Colossus Addon Mod](/colossus-addon-mod) (CAM) is released.

## August

- 25th - [Ennedi](/people/ennedi) starts the [SC4D HoF](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame) MD [Ennedi plays Shosaloza](/ennedi-plays-shosaloza).

## September

- 15th - [Street Addon Mod](/street-addon-mod) (SAM) Version 1 is released by [jplumbley](/people/jplumbley) of the NAM Team.

## October

## November

- 4th - [SC4D's](/sc4d) first birthday.
- 13th - [SimCity Societies](/simcity-societies) is released.
- Polish-language site [SimCity Polska](/simcity-polska) opens.

## December

- 7th - [haljackey](/people/haljackey) starts the [Greater Terran Region](/greater-terran-region).
