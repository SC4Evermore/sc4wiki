**Wealth** is a concept used to describe how much taxes you can get from a specific [lot](/lot-editor). A widely-used symbol to show the wealth of a lot is the dollar symbol, but officially the section symbol, also known as the [Simoleon](/simoleon) symbol, should be used.

| Official Symbol | Common Symbol | Name          |
|-----------------|---------------|---------------|
| §               | \$            | Low Wealth    |
| §§              | \$\$          | Medium Wealth |
| §§§             | \$\$\$        | High Wealth   |
