The **Elementary School** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides [Education](/education) to your Young [Sims](/sims).

Education buildings increase the Education Quotient over a coverage radius. Some buildings have variable coverage radii which can be changed using a School Bus Funding slider, however the majority have a fixed radius that cannot be expanded or contracted without modding the game.

Schools are designed to greatly increase the Education Quotient of your Sims, however it only has an effect on a small age group. With Elementary Schools, the average household age must be at or below 30 Years old to have any real benefit.

In Elementary School rankings, it is second only to the larger [Large Elementary School](/large-elementary-school) added in [Rush Hour](/rush-hour).

## Trivia

The original name for this building was probably **Small Grades K-8 School** before being changed to **Elementary School** as its exemplar still contains this name. This also indicates that there was to be a wider range of Schools in the base game. A [Larger Elementary School](/large-elementary-school) was eventually added in [SimCity 4 Rush Hour and the Deluxe Edition](/rush-hour).
