**True3D** is the term given to models ([S3D](/s3d) files) that are actually 3D, not isometric.

True3D models are used for:

- Network Models (puzzle pieces, Elevated Networks + Ground Highway)
- Automata (Cars, trains, etc)
- Advisors
- Disasters (Tornado, Robot, etc)
