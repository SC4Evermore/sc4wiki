**The Canal Addon Mod** (**CAN-AM**) is another offspring of the NAM, it is a mod that provides pieces to create functional canals. The canals are able to take Passengers and Freight. [Daeley](/people/daeley) has been in charge of the main projects, and many other [NAM Members](/nam-team) and [BSC Members](/bsc/members) have helped or created stations for the project.

The CAN-AM is included in the NAM Core download beginning with the June 2009 release.
