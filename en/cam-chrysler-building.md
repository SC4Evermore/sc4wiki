The **Chrysler Building** is a High-Wealth Commercial Offices Building that grows on High Density Commercial zones, added as part of a [CAM](/cam) installation. [^1].

It is a recreation of the [Chrysler Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrysler_Building) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City) in the [United States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America), completed in 1930, as it appeared in 2003.
