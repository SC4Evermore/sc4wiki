**CN Tower** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [CN Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CN_Tower) in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto), [Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario), [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada), completed in 1976.
