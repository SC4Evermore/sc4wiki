The **House of Worship** are multiple [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Buildings that serve as icons of good Residential growth.

All four variants have identical lots and nearly identical statistics, however their unlock requirements vary.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Sims Show Spiritual Solidarity At New House Of Worship".

## Unlock Requirements

### Variant 1

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#031E0000 House of Worship 1](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#031e0000-house-of-worship-1), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

#### Gameplay

##### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **1,400 Sims**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **40 Points**

#### U Drive It Missions

- [Hearse](/cemetery) Mission: Get The Stiff To The Funeral
