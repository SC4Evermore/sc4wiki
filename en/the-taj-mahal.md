**The Taj Mahal** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Taj Mahal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taj_Mahal) in [Agra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agra), [Uttar Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttar_Pradesh), [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), completed in 1653.
