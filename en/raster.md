**RASTER** is a file format used by [Spore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spore)'s graphics engine and presumably used by [SimCity's](/simcity-2013) [RenderWare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RenderWare) graphics engine. Its use is unknown and it is undecoded.

Its [DBPF](/dbpf) 3.0 [Type ID](/type-id) is **2F4E681C**
