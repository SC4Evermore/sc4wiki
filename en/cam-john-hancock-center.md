The **John Hancock Center** is a High-Wealth Commercial Offices Building that grows on High Density Commercial zones, added as part of a [CAM](/cam) installation. [^1].

It is a recreation of [John Hancock Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hancock_Center) in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago), [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan), [United States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America), completed in 1969.
