**Hutt Enterprise Headquarters** is a High-Wealth Commercial Offices Building that grows on High-Density Commercial zones[^1], added in [SimCity 4's](/simcity-4) [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack.

By Occupant Height, it is the tallest Growable Building in SimCity 4, edging out [Pedriana Pharmaceuticals](/pedriana-pharmaceuticals) by only two metres.

It is designed to compete with [Pedriana Pharmaceuticals](/pedriana-pharmaceuticals) from the Chicago Tileset, [Cahalane Communications](/cahalane-communications) from the Houston Tileset and [Lind Entertainment](/lind-entertainment) from the New York Tileset.

## [Colossus Addon Mod](/colossus-addon-mod)

When the Colossus Addon Mod is installed, all lot variants are changed to Stage 10. It is otherwise unchanged.

## Reference Buildings

Hutt Enterprise Headquarters is clearly based on [Frankfurt's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt) [Commerzbank Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commerzbank_Tower), the second tallest building in Europe as of 2014.
