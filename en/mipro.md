The **Michigan Project**, better known as **mipro** (and formerly known as the **Barry Sanders Project**, or **BSP**) is devoted to recreating significant landmarks from the state of [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan). The group is comprised of independent custom content creators who work together to help bring [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan) to [SimCity 4](/simcity-4). The BSP was formed in April of [2004](/2004), though the team has been most productive from [2006](/2006)-present. The BSP generally uploads six high-quality recreations a year. [Click here](http://www.simtropolis.com/stex/index.cfm?page=1&keyword=BSP&type=all) to browse BSP uploads on the [STEX](/stex).
