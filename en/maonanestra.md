This [Mayor Diary](/mayor-diary) cannot be put into one category, because author experiments with different architectural styles and terrains.

- This city showcase is a fine example of the MD, where the good look and functionality go hand in hand with each other.

- Author makes teasers pretty often, and there are averagely 1,5 teaser for an update.
- Recently, the author has been updating with mosaic only, he makes 3 or 4 of them per update.
