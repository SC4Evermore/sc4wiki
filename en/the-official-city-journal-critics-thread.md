**The Official City Journal Critics Thread** is a thread were a team of designated [City Journal](/city-journal) reviewers post reviews about various [City Journals](/city-journals). A City Journal can be reviewed if the author sends a private message to a reviewer, or has been nominated by another member to be reviewed and approved by the author. The Official City Journal Critics Thread can be found [here](http://www.simtropolis.com/forum/messageview.cfm?catid=36&threadid=83113&enterthread=y).
