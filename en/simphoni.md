**Simphoni** was a [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) fansite that was founded on September 11, [2006](/2006), following the shutdown of [SimCity Central](/simcity-central) two days earlier. Simphoni was essentially a reboot of the old SimCity Central, with all of that site's old content, under a new name. As such, its forum hosted some of the earliest discussions of the game, dating back to 2002.

The site took off running with much of the old SimCity Central community, and quickly established itself as its own, new fansite. While there was talk of reunification when SimCity Central came back online later that year, the sites remained independent and both flourished for some time. The site provided coverage of many of the [SimCity](/simcity) franchise games, as well as related city-building games such as Cities XL, and other [Maxis](/maxis) games, such as The Sims and Spore.

Simphoni became a defunct site in late [2013](/2013). As of July [2016](/2016), its sister site, SimCity Central, is still operational, but has been largely inactive since 2013 as well.
