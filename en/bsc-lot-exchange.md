The **BSC Lot Exchange**, most commonly called the **LEX**, is the name of the exchange at [SC4Devotion](/sc4devotion). The exchange is not dedicated to the [BSC](/bsc) Team, but its members are among the main contributors. Registration to download from the LEX is separate from registration for the forums and the SC4 Encyclopaedia. Before you are able to upload to the LEX, the uploader must go through a candidacy process, known as "scrutineering", and if you have passed this test for a number of times, you will be granted uploading rights.
