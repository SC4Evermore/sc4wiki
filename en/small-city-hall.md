The **Small City Hall** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides a Landmark Effect and some Civil jobs to your [Sims](/sims).

Government Buildings are markers of your city-building prowess and usually supply Civil Service jobs and a Landmark Effect.

The **Small City Hall** is usually considered to be the marker for when your city reaches a medium-level population and upgraded to the [Medium City Hall](/medium-city-hall-unimplemented) when your city's population increased to a certain point.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Justice Served Up Piping Hot At New Courthouse".

## Unlock Requirements

### All Difficulties

- **Combined Population** in excess of **12,500 Sims**
- **Mayor Rating** above **20**

#### With MrTruesage's Fix

- **Combined Population** in excess of **15,500 Sims**
- **Mayor Rating** above **40**

## Bugs

Only the Small City Hall was ever given a reward script by Maxis. The Medium and Large city halls are both functionally complete. It appears that it was originally intended to automatically upgrade in the way that Airports and Seaports can. MrTruesage fixed this by implementing them as three separate lots at Simtropolis.

With custom props installed, a single badly-made tree on the lot can become any of of the custom props installed. This bug was fixed by Andreas Roth at Simtropolis.
