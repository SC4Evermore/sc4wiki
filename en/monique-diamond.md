**Monique Diamond** is the [Finance](/finance) Advisor in [SimCity 4](/simcity-4). Her role is to advise the player on how to better manage their city's finances, a task which brings [ire](/jonas-sparks#quotes) from the other Advisors.

## Regional Differences

### Korea/Thailand/Taiwan/Hong Kong (SKU 3) and China (SKU 4)

It is understood that the Advisors were changed in these versions, but their names are not known and the only evidence is blurry screenshots.

### Japan (SKU 5)

**Joan Sue** (ジョン スー) replaces Monique Diamond as the [Finance](/finance) Advisor in the Japanese version of SimCity 4.
