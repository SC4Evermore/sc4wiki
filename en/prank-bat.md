**Prank BATs** are creations meant to give custom content creators the chance to have a little fun. Often seen around April Fool's Day, these creations are meant, as their name implies, to be a joke. An example of this is the "Nuclear-Powered Starbucks Canary Wharf" by [TheQuiltedLlama](/people/thequiltedllama). This [BAT](/bat) appears to have been designed as commentary on the amount of custom content with unbalanced properties and other issues.
