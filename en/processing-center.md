**Processing Center** is a Manufacturing Industrial Machinery Building that grows on Medium and High density Industrial Zones[^1].

Machinery Buildings are grown by SimCity 4's Industrial Zones attached to an Anchor Lot and are designed to expand the base lot with major machinery installations. Machinery buildings are the second type of Industry building to be grown, preceded by [Anchor Lots](/category/i-m-growables/anchors) and followed by [Outbuildings](/category/i-m-growables/outbuildings).
