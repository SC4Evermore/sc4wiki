**Faneuil Hall** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Faneuil Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faneuil_Hall) in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston), [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts), [United States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America) completed in 1742.
