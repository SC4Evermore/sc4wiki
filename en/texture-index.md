The **BSC Texture Index** is an attempt to organize the [Instance ID](/instance-id) numbers of all [overlay-](/overlay-texture) and [base textures](/base-texture) in a single place to avoid duplicate usage of an IID number, which would cause conflicts in-game. Everyone who needs a range of texture IID's, can ask for one at either the [Simtropolis](http://community.simtropolis.com/topic/471-bsc-texture-index/) or [SC4D](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=2101.0) version of the thread. However, the amount of possible IIDs is limited, so do not reserve one if you know you will not be using it. A similar index of Network Textures is maintained by the [NAM](/nam) Team.
