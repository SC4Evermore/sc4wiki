**Adler Planetarium** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 031** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Adler Planetarium & Astronomy Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adler_Planetarium_%26_Astronomy_Museum) in [Northerly Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northerly_Island), [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago), [Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois) as it appeared in 2003.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "#city# Sims Set Sights On Stars At Adler Planetarium".
