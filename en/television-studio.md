The **Television Studio** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that greatly increases your city's Middle-Wealth Residential and Commercial capacities.

It is a requirement in for unlocking the [Movie Studio](/movie-studio), the Middle-Wealth trophy building.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Soaps, Sit-coms And Other Schlock On Tap At New TV Studio".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03340000 TV Station](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03340000-tv-station), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **65,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **50 Points**
- A **[Radio Station](/radio-station) has been constructed in the city**

### U Drive It Missions

- Police Helicopter Mission: Nab the Crook from the Air
