**City View** is where most of the gameplay happens. City View shows only one city, and is entered after you select a city to play in [Region View](/region-view). The [God Mode](/god-mode), [Mayor Mode](/mayor-mode), and [My Sim Mode](/my-sim-mode) toolbars, and the [Options Panel](/options-panel) are all available in City View. City View is where you do all terraforming and developing of a city.
