The **Community Garden** is a group of four [Civic Buildings](/civic-buildings) that provide Beautification and Recreation for your [Sims](/sims). All four variants are identical to the game.

Parks provide a [Park Effect](/park-effect) over a fixed radius, in addition to usually cleaning up air and water pollution and increasing [CAPs](/cap).
