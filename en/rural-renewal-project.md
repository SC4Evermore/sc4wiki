The **Rural Renewal Project**, also known as **RRP**, is a project that wants to increase the diversity of [SimCity 4](/simcity-4)'s agricultural/rural areas. The project is led by [Chrisadams3997](/people/chrisadams3997), and has recently been released to the [LEX](/lex).

### Individual Projects

- Gravel paths
- Wooden fences
- New [floras](/flora)
- Draggable paths
- New highway overpasses
- Rural culverts
- Dams
