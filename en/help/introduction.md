## What is the SC4 Encyclopaedia?

The SC4 Encyclopaedia is an [encyclopaedia](/wikipedia-encyclopedia) written [collaboratively](/wikipedia-collaborative-writing) by [many of its readers](/wikipedia-who-writes-wikipedia). It is a special type of website, called a [wiki](/wikipedia-wiki), that makes collaboration easy. Many people are constantly improving the SC4 Encyclopaedia, making changes which are recorded on [article histories](/help/page-history) and [recent changes](/special-recentchanges). Inappropriate changes are usually removed quickly, and repeat offenders can be [blocked from editing](/wikipedia-wikipedia-blocking-policy). If you add new material to the SC4 Encyclopaedia, [please provide references](/wikipedia-wikipedia-citing-sources). Facts that are unreferenced [may be removed from the encyclopaedia](/wikipedia-wikipedia-verifiability).

## How can I help?

See the "**edit**" tab above? On the SC4 Encyclopaedia, you can **edit articles right now!**

**Don't be afraid to edit** — anyone can edit almost any page, and we encourage you to **[be bold](/wikipedia-wikipedia-be-bold)**! Find something that can be improved, whether content, grammar or formatting, and make it better.

You can't break the SC4 Encyclopaedia. Anything can be fixed or improved later. So go ahead, edit an article and help make the SC4 Encyclopaedia the best information source on the Internet!

<div style="width:100%">

<big>**Make your first edit right now:**</big>

1.  <span class="plainlinks">**[Click here](http://www.wiki.sc4devotion.com/index.php?title=SC4_Encyclopaedia:Sandbox&action=edit)**</span> to edit the sandbox, a place to make test edits. (If there is already some text there, add to the end of the text)
2.  Type some text.
3.  Please, remember that while the SC4 Encyclopaedia is a free encyclopedia, it is [not a forum](/wikipedia-wp-not)! No profanity, libel, or [personal attacks](/wikipedia-wikipedia-npa) will be tolerated.
4.  [Sign](/wikipedia-wikipedia-signatures) your posts on [discussion pages](/wikipedia-help-using-talk-pages) by entering four tildes (\~\~\~~) or by clicking on the signature icon. This will automatically produce your name and the date.
5.  Click **Show preview** to test your changes, or **Save page** when you're happy with the way it looks.

</div>
