<noinclude>Sometimes you will make a mistake and create a page with a bad name, or you will spot a mis-spelled article title. In these situations, you should mark the article with the appropriate template, which will automatically place the article into one of the [ requests categories](/requests). Once there is reasonable certainty that the problem has been alleviated, then the template can be removed.
