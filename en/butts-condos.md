**Butts Condos** is a Stage 6 High Wealth Residential building that grows on Medium and High density Residential Zones[^1].

## Reference Buildings

Butts Condos is likely based on [The Dakota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dakota) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York), notable for being the home of many major creative people and the site of the shooting of [John Lennon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lennon).
