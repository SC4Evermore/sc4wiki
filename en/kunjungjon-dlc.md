**Kunjungjon** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 008** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of Geunjeongjeon, [Gyeongbokgung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyeongbokgung) Palace's Throne Room, in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul), [Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea), as it appeared in 2003.

## Trivia

The way **Kunjungjon** was made by Maxis is different to most of the other landmarks in that its daytime renders take advantage of embedded Alphas, something common to [BAT](/bat) models, however it doesn't utilise dedicated nighttime renders, instead using the Shade then Overlay method used by all Maxis buildings. It shares this trait with [63 Building](/63-building-dlc), [Sungrye-Mun](/sungrye-mun-dlc) and [The Living Mall](/the-living-mall-dlc).
