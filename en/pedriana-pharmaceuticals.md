**Pedriana Pharmaceuticals** is a High-Wealth Commercial Offices Building that grows on High-Density Commercial zones[^1].

It is designed to compete with [Cahalane Communications](/cahalane-communications) from the Houston Tileset, [Lind Entertainment](/lind-entertainment) from the New York Tileset and (Provided [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) is installed) [Hutt Enterprise Headquarters](/hutt-enterprise-headquarters) from the Euro Tileset.

## [Colossus Addon Mod](/colossus-addon-mod)

When the Colossus Addon Mod is installed, all default lots are changed to Stage 11. It is otherwise unchanged.

## Reference Buildings

Pedriana Pharmaceuticals appears to be based on [New York City's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City) [Nelson Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_Tower) in the [Garment District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garment_District%2C_Manhattan).

## Bugs

Pedriana Pharmaceuticals is tall enough that it suffers from the Large Building Light Source Bug present in the original versions of the BAT, where shadows stop being cast on a building above a certain height.
