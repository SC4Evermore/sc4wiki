The **Major Port** is a Transport building that provides Freight Shipping services, designed to remove Industrial Freight traffic from your city.

Ports act as one of the two possible destinations for Freight Trucks.

## Beta

In beta, it appears that the Major Port was to be the Midpoint in an upgradeable chain of buildings, preceded by the [Port](/port) and followed by the [Seaport](/seaport).
