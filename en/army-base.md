The **Army Base** is a [Business Deal](/business-deals) building in SimCity 4.

It has a moderate localised penalty to most desirable properties, however it can be a small tourist attraction and is a much better option that most of the other Business Deals.

As with all Business Deals, demolishing the building is an incredibly expensive action.

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03460000 Army Base](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03460000-army-base), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Population** in excess of **2,000 Sims**
- **Available Cash less than §30,000**
- **Available Cash** now **less than** the formula **0.4 x Funds 36 Months Ago**
- **Monthly Income less than Monthly Expenses**
