The **Hollywood Sign** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Hollywood Sign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Sign) in [Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), completed in 1923 and rebuilt in 1978.
