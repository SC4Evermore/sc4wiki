**The Alamo** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [The Alamo Mission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alamo_Mission_in_San_Antonio) in [San Antonio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Antonio), [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), built in 1744.
