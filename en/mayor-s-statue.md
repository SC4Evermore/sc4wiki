The **Mayor's Statue** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that is very desirable to both residential and commercial sims.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Marble Statue Of Mayor An Uncanny Likeness".

## Unlock Requirements

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **5,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **60 Points**
