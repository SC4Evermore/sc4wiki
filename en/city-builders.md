The **City Builders** Website was founded in early 2012 by [Catty](/people/catty) as a website "for City Planners and Builders whether it's Real Life or a Game."[^1] Early discussion about this website began in [this thread](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=10196) at SC4Devotion, and the first link to the new website was posted there on April 7, 2012. Discussion about City Builders on SC4D was later moved to [its own topic](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=14657.0), which was started April 19, 2012. The City Builders website received an overhaul in early June 2012, including a new appearance, the debut of the CBEX, and further refinement to the site's existing features.

The City Builders website features a forum, discussion area, file exchange (the CBEX), best picture competition, image gallery, and the Custom Content Catalog. The Custom Content Catalog is "a master reference index mainly for SimCity 4 Plugins and their locations, but can be used by any registered user to record any assets, games or websites and their locations on the Internet."[^2]
