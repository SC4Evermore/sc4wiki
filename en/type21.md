A **Type21** (**T21**) [exemplar](/exemplar) is used to add props rendered in [BAT](/bat) (isometric) to [networks](/transit-network), such as traffic lights and lamp posts. These exemplars differ from Lot Exemplars (T10) in that they don't allow for base or overlay textures, and obviously can't be [Transit Enabled](/transit-enabled).
