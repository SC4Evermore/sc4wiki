The **Water Tower** is a utility building that provides water for your [Sims](/sims). Unlike the real world, Water Towers supply water instead of storing it.

Water pumps are designed to be connected to zones and buildings with [water pipes](/transit-network#water-pipes), but do not have to be as they provide water within a three-tile radius.

The Water Tower is the bottom-tier water production utility, producing just over a tenth of the amount of water that the [Water Pump](/water-pump) does.

## Trivia

The version of this building implemented by Maxis draws from the game's Aquifer, which is a bottomless supply of water, however it was originally designed to draw from the game's Fresh Water Table which is not completely implemented in the game's code.
