The **Country Club** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Beautification and Recreation for your [Sims](/sims).

Parks provide a [Park Effect](/park-effect) over a fixed radius, in addition to usually cleaning up air and water pollution and increasing [CAPs](/cap).

The Zoo is the cheapest Park in SimCity 4 Rush Hour/Deluxe, being completely free, however constructing it should be considered akin to taking out a business deal: most of its effects are entirely self-serving (Most of its positive effects barely cover itself) and it has a massive demolition cost.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Gentry Tee Off At New Country Club".
