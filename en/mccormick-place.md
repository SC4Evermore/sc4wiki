**McCormick Place** is a High-Wealth Residential Building that grows on High-Density Residential zones[^1] in [SimCity 4](/simcity-4).

## Reference Buildings

McCormick Place closely resembles The Riviera at 790 Riverside Drive in [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York), built in 1910. The recreation shares most of the building's shape and ornamentation, though it uses a different colour scheme and has a different roof.

## Trivia

The entrance of the Reference Building is on the opposite side of how it appears in-game.
