The **Small Municipal Airport** is a Transport building that provides Air services, designed to provide Commercial CAP Relief.

Airports provide Commercial Office Cap Relief proportional to their Usage.

The Small Municipal Airport is the first lot in the Munincipal-class Airport chain, succeeded by both the [Medium Municipal Airport](/medium-municipal-airport) and [Large Municipal Airport](/large-municipal-airport).
