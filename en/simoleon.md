The **Simoleon** (§) is the unit of currency in [SimCity 4](/simcity-4). It is also the currency used throughout previous versions of SimCity and [The Sims](/wikipedia-the-sims-series). The in-game [budget](/budget) uses Simoleons to report income and expenses, and the cost of items and tools used in [Mayor Mode](/mayor-mode) are listed in Simoleons.
