SimCity 4 (SC4) is the game released in 2003 by [Electronic Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Arts). It was developed by [Maxis](/maxis). It's the best selling game in the [SimCity series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SimCity_%28series%29).

An expansion pack, called [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) was released on September 22, 2003 and is the only expansion pack for SC4 to date.

The [Deluxe Edition](/deluxe-edition) contains both Simcity 4 and Rush Hour.
