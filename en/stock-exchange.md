The **Stock Exchange** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that greatly boosts High-Wealth Commercial Office capacity in your City.

The Stock Exchange is one of the cheapest rewards in SimCity 4 Rush Hour/Deluxe, being completely free, however constructing it should be considered akin to taking out a business deal, mostly because of its' massive demolition cost. However, it gives massive CO§§§ CAP relief and has a large Landmark effect.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Gavel Drops At New Stock Exchange, Fortunes Await".

## Reference Buildings

The entrance of the Stock Exchange appears to be largely based on the [New York Stock Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Stock_Exchange) in [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York), built in 1903. Only the entrance of the building and the railings around the roof share any resemblance, the rest of the recreation is an original work.
