The **SSPTool** is a [tool](/tool), created by [Stefan79](/people/stefan79) and written in [Java](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Java), that allows you to create, edit and index [DAMN](/damn) Menus. It can also be used to scan your [plugins](/plugins) folder for unnecessary files and move those out.
