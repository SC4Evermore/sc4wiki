The **SC4 Organizer** is a tool created by [HandsOn](/people/handson) for organizing your [plugins](/plugins). It has been released, a few of the included features having been made into separate programs, like the [MD/CJ Editor](/md/cj-editor) and the [SC4 Region &amp; City Viewer](/sc4-region-and-amp-city-viewer). You can view the development [here (ST)](http://www.simtropolis.com/forum/messageview.cfm?catid=22&threadid=103888) and download the latest releases [here (STEX)](http://www.simtropolis.com/stex/index.cfm?id=21820).
