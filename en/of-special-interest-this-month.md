The **Of Special Interest This Month** (**OSITM**) board highlights up to five [Mayor Diaries](/mayor-diary) each month, that are seen by the [SC4D](/sc4d) staff for doing particularly good. The selected [MDs](/md) can come from every category in the [Mayor Diaries](/mayor-diary) section, particularly from the [Recently Published](/category/cs-recently-published) and the [Best Sellers](/category/cs-best-sellers).

The first OSITM Awards were given in April 2007, and continue to be given each month to date.

Beginning in April 2012, the OSITM award was broadened to include special recognition for outstanding custom content threads as well as MD's. For the November 2012 SC4Devotion 6th Birthday Celebration, [Threestooges](/people/threestooges) began the [Of Special Interest Interviews](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=15246.0), where various winners of OSITM awards were interviewed on various topics, including of course, SimCity 4. Starting in November 2012, a 4-in-1 (4x1) OSITM award was created, where for the month of November, four winners will get showcased as an OSITM for one week each.
