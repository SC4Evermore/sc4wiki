**Todd & Co.** is a High-Wealth Commercial Officed Building that grows on all Densities of Commercial zones.

## Trivia

As exemplar the name for this building is ChiOfficeBldg, it's highly likely that this building was only ever meant to be available in the Chicago tileset.[^1]

This building uses the same models as the Commercial Services building [Chicago Boutiques 1](/chicago-boutiques-1) and can be considered to be a Commercial Services counterpart.
