SimForum (also known as SimForum.de) is a German The Sims and SimCity community (possibly the largest) that also hosts the [SFBT](/sfbt). It was started by the now defunct [SimsZone](/simszone) in 2001 and features various discussion boards about all Sim games, along with a popular off-topic section. Many regular users know each other since years and have met personally as well. As of September 2008, SimForum has more than 50,000 registered users and more than 3,200,000 postings.
