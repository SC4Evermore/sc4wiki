**Zaidi Apartments** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building that grows on only High Density Residential zones. [^1]

It is designed to primarily compete with [The Knight Building](/the-knight-building), [The Wilkinson](/the-wilkinson) and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Leonid Apartment Complex](/leonid-apartment-complex).
