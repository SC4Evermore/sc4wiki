**Big Ben** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Elizabeth Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Tower) in [Westminster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westminster), [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London), [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom), completed in 1858.

## Trivia

At 12 O'Clock in-game time, it sounds its distinctive twelve strikes.
