The **American Museum Of Natural History** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a rough recreation of the [American Museum Of Natural History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Museum_Of_Natural_History) in [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), the first building of which was completed in 1877.
