The **Landmark Effect** is an effect emitted by [lots](/lot) that increases nearby Commercial desirability. Placing a lot that emits the Landmark Effect (such as a Landmark) will usually result in higher wealth and density Commercial development nearby.

When defining the Landmark Effect for a lot, one specifies the radius over which the effect acts, and the intensity of the effect.

The Landmark Effect is similar to the [Park Effect](/park-effect), which acts on Residential properties.
