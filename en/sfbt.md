<img src="/_files/images/logo-sfbt.png" title=" Logo of the SFBT" width="200" alt=" Logo of the SFBT" /> The **SimForum BAT Team**, short **SFBT**, was founded by a couple of members in February 2005. Back then, [SimsZone](/simszone) was the "home" of the team and this was where its creations were to be found for download. In January 2007, the SFBT made a move to a seperate new page, called [SimCityPlaza](/simcityplaza), giving the German SimCity community a real home (SimsZone used to be a mixed The Sims and SimCity page). Parts of the old SimsZone contents were preserved, new tutorials and articles were added. Furthermore, a new download center was established; the [SimCityKurier](/simcitykurier) was born where all SFBT creations can be downloaded. All the SFBT members are more or less active at [SimForum.de](/simforum), the biggest German community for The Sims and SimCity fans.

## Creations & Projects

The best-known SFBT creations are probably the PreFabs (also [Plattenbauten](/plattenbauten)) by [Ch. P.](/people/ch-p), as well as the [Hamburg W2W](/hamburg-w2w) buildings and residental estates by [stephenhh](/people/stephenhh). ![200\*100px\|right\|Plattenbau with red balconies](/_files/images/sfbt-plattenbau-highrise1.jpg) First blueprints for the [Plattenbau](/plattenbau) buildings in the former GDR can be traced back to the Bauhaus style. In the 70's and 80's, an immense demand for residential buildings had to be satisfied somehow. Highrises were the answer to accommodate as many people as possible on a given lot size. Often poor families lived in these highrises, the lot have been implemented as R-§. With this technique a lot of flats were constructed in a faster way than before. Also a lot of people could live in such a highrise. Normally they stand in one district, and often had a swimming hall, restaurants, parks, childhoods and garages. Each flat looks exactly the same, only the interior was different. An example for this type of building is the [Plattenbau](/plattenbau) *Unité d'habitation* in Berlin, constructed by Le Corbusier. It was an example for most of the Plattenbau buildings in the world, built in 1947. A short form of Plattenbau in German is "Platte", the walls were sticked on the wall before, like LEGO.  
  
The [SFBT Euro Road Texture Mod](/euro-road-texture-mod) by [frimi](/people/frimi) and [Andreas](/people/andreas) is very popular, too, as it changes the American styled road with yellow double-lines into an European styled one with single white dashed line. Other well-known creations are the differently styled park sets by [Andreas](/people/andreas), [ArkenbergeJoe](/people/arkenbergejoe) and [Khiyana](/people/khiyana). [ArkenbergeJoe](/people/arkenbergejoe) also created the [NAM heavy rail viaducts](/nam) in three different colours. Further rail stuff has been made by [Kenworth](/people/kenworth), like his catenaries or stations of which some are to fit the [NAM](/nam) bridges.  
  
Other projects include new (NAM) additions like Tram-in-Road pieces by [Chrisim](/people/chrisim) and [Schleicher68](/people/schleicher68), German rail signals and freight cars by [royal](/people/royal), typical Central European farmhouses by [voltaic](/people/voltaic), and new German locomotives by [Kenworth](/people/kenworth).
