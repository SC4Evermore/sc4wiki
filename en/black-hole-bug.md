The so-called **Black Hole Bug** is a bug that is caused when the [base textures](/base-texture) have been removed from the [lot](/lot) data. It causes the game's background to show through the [lot](/lot). The bug can be fixed on-the-spot by opening and closing the Zone [Data View](/data-view).
