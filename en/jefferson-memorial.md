The **Jefferson Memorial** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Jefferson Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jefferson_Memorial), in [Washington, D.C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington%2C_D.C), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), completed in 1943.
