The **High School** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides [Education](/education) to your Young [Sims](/sims).

Education buildings increase the Education Quotient over a coverage radius. Some buildings have variable coverage radii which can be changed using a School Bus Funding slider, however the majority have a fixed radius that cannot be expanded or contracted without modding the game.

In High School rankings, it is second only to the larger [Large High School](/large-high-school) added in [Rush Hour](/rush-hour).

## Trivia

The original name for this building was probably **Large Grades 9-12 School** before being changed to **High School** as its exemplar still contains this name. This also indicates that there was to be a wider range of Schools in the base game. A [Larger High School](/large-high-school) was eventually added in [SimCity 4 Rush Hour and the Deluxe Edition](/rush-hour).
