The **Brick House** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building with three statistically Identical[^1][^2] Variants that grow on All Residential zones. The third variant is [Sheeza Brick House](/sheeza-brick-house).

Both Variants are designed to compete with [2 Story House](/2-story-house), [Bungalow](/bungalow), [Sheeza Brick House](/sheeza-brick-house) and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Dubois Garden Cottage](/dubois-garden-cottage), [Avery Abode](/avery-abode) and [Handy House](/handy-house).

## Variant Differences

Variant 2 merely had different textures hen rendered. Variant 3 ([Sheeza Brick House](/sheeza-brick-house)) has a flipped model and different textures.
