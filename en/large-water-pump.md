The **Large Water Pump** is a utility building that provides water for your [Sims](/sims).

Large Water Pumps are designed to be connected to zones and buildings with [water pipes](/transit-network#water-pipes), but do not have to be as they provide water within a three-tile radius.

The Large Water Pump is the largest water-supplying building in the game, supplying 200,000 cubic metres of water per month to your city.

It produces ten times the amount of water the [Water Pump](/water-pump) does, for nine times the expense per month. However it does cost 11 times as much to build as 1 pump does and produces 83 times as much water as the [Water Tower](/water-tower) for 63 times the expense.

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Water Usage + Deals** in excess of **40,000 cubic metres**.

or

- **Combined Population** in excess of **10,000**.
