**NDEX** is a custom content creation group, founded in 2004. The name is derived from the three initial members, [N74707](/people/n74707) (N), [DuskTrooper](/people/dusktrooper) (D), and [Equinox](/people/equinox) (Ex).

Around 2006, the team largely became dormant, with DuskTrooper retiring from BATing activity (at least publicly), and Equinox going on hiatus. [Ill Tonkso](/people/ill-tonkso) kept the NDEX name alive after this point. The team expanded again in 2007, with [DragonAnime](/people/dragonanime), [SimRabbit](/people/simrabbit) and [Glenni](/people/glenni) joining.

While NDEX has released a variety of material, it is best known for its large number of skyscrapers, frequently based on Postmodern buildings from cities like Houston or Atlanta.
