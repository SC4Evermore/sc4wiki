**Houston Boutiques 5** (Named in the game as just **[Boutiques](/boutiques)**) is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on only High-Density Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic High-Wealth stores grouped under the Boutiques name.

## Trivia

This building uses the same models as the Commercial Office building [Ajjanagadde Inc.](/ajjanagadde-inc) and can be considered to be a Commercial Services counterpart.
