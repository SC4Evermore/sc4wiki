The **Courthouse** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that serves as an icon of City development.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Justice Served Up Piping Hot At New Courthouse".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03220000 Courthouse](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03220000-courthouse), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Population** in excess of **40,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **33 Points**
- A **[City Hall](/city-hall) has been constructed in the city**

### U Drive It Missions

- Ambulance Mission: Shady Ambulance Driver
