**Gordon Theaters** is a Medium-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on Medium and High Density Commercial zones. [^1]

## Reference Buildings

Gordon Theaters is likely based on the [Civic Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland_Civic_Theatre) in [Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland), [New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand), completed in 1929. Aside from the colour scheme and the tower at the rear, the recreation is accurate.
