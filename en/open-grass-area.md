The **Open Grass Area** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Beautification and Recreation for your [Sims](/sims).

Parks provide a [Park Effect](/park-effect) over a fixed radius, in addition to usually cleaning up air and water pollution and increasing [CAPs](/cap).

The Open Grass area is intended merely as a filler park and has the weakest Park and Landmark Effects of all the basic parks.

## Bugs

The texture used for the grass base of the Open Grass Area doesn't match any of the other parks in the game.

There are multiple mods that fix this problem.
