**RCI** stands for **Residential, Commercial and Industrial**, the main [building](/building) and [zone](/zone) types in [SimCity 4](/simcity-4). The RCI Graph depicts the demand for each type in a particular city at the present time. It can be further expanded to view the actual amount of demand, and the wealth/type of the RCI.

RCI can be a useful tool when expanding your city. It tells you from a general perspective what your [Sims](/sims) want and what they don't. Using a highly demanded zone type typically yields much more development than a undemanded zone type.

For more information, see [RCI Zones](/rci-zones).
