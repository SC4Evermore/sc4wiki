The **63 Building** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 Rush Hour/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 00A** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [63 Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/63_Building) in [Yeouido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeouido), [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul), [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea), completed in 1985.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Massive 63 Building Pokes It's Peak Up Over City", however the code to create the entry is commented out meaning it is never displayed.

## Trivia

The way **63 Building** was made by Maxis is different to most of the other landmarks in that its daytime renders take advantage of embedded Alphas, something common to [BAT](/bat) models. However, it doesn't utilize dedicated nighttime renders; instead it uses the Shade then Overlay method used by all Maxis buildings. It shares this trait with [The Living Mall](/the-living-mall-dlc), [Sungrye-Mun](/sungrye-mun-dlc) and [Kunjungjon](/kunjungjon-dlc).
