The **Taipei Fine Arts Museum** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 030** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Taipei Fine Arts Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei_Fine_Arts_Museum) in [Taipei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei), [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan) as it appeared in 2003.
