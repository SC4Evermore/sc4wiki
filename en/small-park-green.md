The **Small Park Green** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Beautification and Recreation for your [Sims](/sims).

Parks provide a [Park Effect](/park-effect) over a fixed radius, in addition to usually cleaning up air and water pollution and increasing [CAPs](/cap).

Park Greens are the closest thing that SimCity 4 has to a 'balanced' park, with the most equal Park and Landmark effect strengths. However, [equivalent Flower Gardens](/small-flower-garden) feature a stronger Park Effect and [equivalent Plazas](/small-plaza) feature a far stronger Landmark effect.
