**Wall to Wall (W2W)** buildings are a class of [BATs](/bat) that leave no visual space between buildings when plopped or grown next to each other. They literally have the wall of one building against the wall of another building. Wall to wall buildings are generally custom content, and wall to wall sets of buildings have been created in the styles of several real world cities.
