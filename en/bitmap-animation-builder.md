The **Bitmap Animation Builder**, most known as **SC4 BAB**, is a [gmax](/gmax)-addon, that allows the [BATter](/bat) to create animated props with ease. It was created by [OverPoP](/people/overpop) and had undergone rigorous testing, for example by [SimGoober](/people/simgoober), before it's actual release.
