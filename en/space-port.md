The **Space Port** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) [Reward](/rewards) Building.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Space Port Lets \#city# Citizens To Reach Astronomical Heights!".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03840000 Space Port](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03840000-space-port), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### Easy Difficulty

Cannot be unlocked through gameplay.

#### Medium and Hard Difficulties

- **High-Tech Industrial** provides in excess of **25,000 Jobs**
- A **[Advanced Research Center](/advanced-research-center) has been constructed in the city**
