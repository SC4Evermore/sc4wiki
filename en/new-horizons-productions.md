**New Horizons Productions** (**NHP**) is a custom content creation team founded in September 2006 at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis) by several members involved in [Mapping](/mapping), [beskhu3epnm](/people/beskhu3epnm), [blade2k5](/people/blade2k5), [jplumbley](/people/jplumbley), [MallowTheCLoud](/people/mallowthecloud), [Moganite](/people/moganite), [MrFood11](/people/mrfood11), and [Wouanagaine](/people/wouanagaine), coinciding with the release of wouanagaine's mapping tool, [SC4Terraformer](/sc4terraformer). It has since expanded to other aspects of terrain and terrain alteration, in addition to Lots and BATs.
