**Tokyo Tower** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Tokyo Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Tower) in [Minato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minato%2C_Tokyo), [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo), [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan), completed in 1958.
