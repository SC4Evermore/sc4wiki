**Dead Forest Paper** is a Dirty Industrial Anchor Lot that grows on Medium and High density Industrial Zones[^1].

Anchor Lots are the first building grown by SimCity 4's Industrial Zones, designed to provide both a destination and spawn point for the game's traffic simulation. Due to this, they are the only Industrial buildings that require direct [Transit Network](/transit-network) access. [Machinery Lots](/category/i-d-growables/machinery) and [Outbuildings](/category/i-d-growables/outbuildings) are grown adjacent or near to the Anchor lot to expand the Anchor Lot's capabilities, as well as to allow industrial growth away from Transit Networks.

## [Colossus Addon Mod](/colossus-addon-mod)

When the Colossus Addon Mod is installed, all of the default lots are have their stages increased to either Stage 5 or 6 and the number of jobs is increased to 541. It is otherwise unchanged.
