**Casper's Portfolio** is a [Mayor Diary](/mayor-diary) authored by [CasperVg](/people/caspervg). It can be found on [SC4D](/sc4d). The author prefers making regular teasers over making large updates with a lot of time in between them, and that's why he has [beaten the replies written in one week record](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=1300.msg187601#msg187601). However, it has been dormant for the past few weeks, as it's author has been busy working on other things.

This City Showcase had been started on May 31th, [2008](/2008), but then it was radically different than now. [CasperVg](/people/caspervg) was showcasing every step of creating his city, while now he just shows the final effect. He also uses his city to showcase some of his lotting. It has formed part of the [Classics](/classics)-sections of the [Mayor Diaries](/mayor-diary), but is currently inactive.

[CasperVg](/people/caspervg) isn't afraid of trying different city building styles etc. However, he tries to make his city as less '[Vanilla](/vanilla)' as possible. He's also known for his works in regards of transportation. Teasers are very regular within Casper's Portfolio. [CasperVg](/people/caspervg) tries to make one everyday. Some people thus call him the 'Teaser making machine'.
