## UD Productions

**UDProductions** (**UDP**) is a former [BATing](/bat), Lotting and Modding team that was founded on May 29th, [2008](/2008). The letters *U* and *D* are from the two founding members [un1](/people/un1) and [Deion30296](/people/deion30296). Many of its members joined another team, the [SimCity Remodeling Team](#simcity-remodeling-team), which later dissolved as well. There also was a site dedicated to the UDP Team, also home to the [AFDES](/afdes) and the [No Name Maps](/no-name-maps) Team, but it is no longer active.

## SimCity Remodeling Team

**SCRT** was a team that specialized in modding, lotting and BATing. Formerly named *UDPX*, the SCRT formed after a failed merger with [ITC](/itc) and it stuck with the new name. The mission of SCRT was to provide functional, high-quality mods, [lots](/lots) and [BATs](/bat) to all with open application spaces any time. SCRT originally started as UDPX in May/June 2008. The number of members on the site and in the group were both dwindling, so UDPX tried to release new stuff, but hadn't released much. After many attempted mergers and publicity tries, [Travis](/people/travis) from [ITC](/itc), [un1](/people/un1) and [deion30296](/people/deion30296) from UDPX agreed to attempt to merge their two teams into SCRT. After further discussion with other [ITC](/itc) members, the merger was cancelled.

## International Team of Creators (ITC)

**ITC** was a team founded on April 19th, 2008. At first, ITC was named after its founders, [Yoshi](/people/yoshi), [Travis](/people/travis) and [CasperVg](/people/caspervg), but after two of the three founders retired, it was officially changed to *International Team of Creators* on the 25th of December [2008](/2008) to reflect the many nations its team members represent. After that, ITC was led by [Threestooges](/people/threestooges) and [CasperVg](/people/caspervg). ITC produced a wide variety of custom content ranging from network mods, bridges, and general custom content and to have a nurturing, friendly atmosphere within which to conduct such actions while simultaneously providing a source for feedback from those on the cutting edge of development. As of October 9th [2009](/2009), the ITC Team was moved from the Other Teams to the Certified Teams section on the [SC4D](/sc4d) Boards. However, it disbanded in January [2010](/2010)
