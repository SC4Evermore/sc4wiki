The **Lincoln Memorial** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Lincoln Memorial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Memorial) in [Washington, DC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington%2C_DC), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), completed in 1922.
