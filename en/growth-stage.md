The **Growth Stage**, sometimes referred to as just a **Stage**, is a Lot [Exemplar Property](/exemplar-properties) used to limit when a lot can be built. The active growth stage in a city is determined by a combination of the Local and Regional populations. Lots with a Growth Stage set above that calculated value will not be added to the pool of possible options to grow in zones.

SimCity 4 uses Eight stages for Residential and Commercial, and Three stages for all types of Industrial.
