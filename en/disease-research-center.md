The **Disease Research Center** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Healthcare for your [Sims](/sims) in SimCity 4.

Clinics increase your city's Health Quotient and your Sims' Lifespans depending on their funding over a variable radius which can be adjusted using the Vehicle Funding slider in the building's Query Panel up until there are no more free capacity.

The **Disease Research Center** is the most expensive Healthcare building in SimCity 4, with the largest overall effect compared to the the [Large Medical Center](/large-medical-center) and the [Medical Clinic](/medical-clinic).

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Common Colds No Match For New Disease Research Center".

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **56,000 Sims**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **45 Points**
- **At Least 3 [Hospitals](/large-medical-center) or [Clinics](/medical-clinic) built**
