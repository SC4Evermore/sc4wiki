**Holistic Health Center** is a High-Wealth Commercial Services LOT that grows on both Medium Density and High Density Commercial zones. According to the building's Occupant Groups, Holistic Health Center is a Commercial Store.

## Trivia

This building is named '1EuroLuxuryStore2' in the Exemplar, meaning that it is to be considered to be the Euro Tileset equivalent to the [Boutiques](/boutiques) 2 buildings in the other three tilesets.

This building is in a Building Family with [Houston Boutiques 2](/houston-boutiques-2), meaning that they both use the same LOTs and either may appear when a lot made with this building is called by the game.

The Exemplar name contains a typographical error it shares with all Boutiques 2 buildings. The Exemplar name should read '2EuroLuxuryStore2' if it were to be consistent with the other Boutiques stages.
