**SimVision** was a fansite run by [BarbulaM1](/people/barbulam1), opened on April 29, 2004. In addition to a forum, it had an exchange, the **SVEX**, and a **Simcyclopedia** section. The SVEX was an open exchange, like the STEX at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis), and contained files for games other than SC4.

In October 2008, it merged with [The Planning Pages](/the-planning-pages), a now defunct fansite. SimVision closed in April [2013](/2013).
