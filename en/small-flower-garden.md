The **Small Flower Garden** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Beautification and Recreation for your [Sims](/sims).

Parks provide a [Park Effect](/park-effect) over a fixed radius, in addition to usually cleaning up air and water pollution and increasing [CAPs](/cap).

Flower Gardens are similar to [their equivalent Park Greens](/small-park-green), however they are aimed more at residential areas with lowered Landmark Effect strength and raised Park Effect strength.
