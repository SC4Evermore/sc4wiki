**Left Hand Drive** (**LHD**) is the situation when the cars drive on the left side of the road, not when the steering wheel is on the left and therefore cars drive on the right side of the road. LHD is used in the *English UK* version of the game, and a few other versions. It is possible to switch the side cars drive on in SC4, as explained [here](/tutorial/switch-language-and-driving-sides)
