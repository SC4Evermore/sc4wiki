**Chicago Retail Stores 7** (Named in the game as just **[Retail Stores](/retail-stores)**) is a Medium-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on only High Density Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic Medium-Wealth stores grouped under the Retail Stores name.

[Miguel LLC](/miguel-llc) uses the same model sets and can be considered a Commercial Offices variant of this building.

## Reference Buildings

It is likely based on the [Wainwright Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wainwright_Building) in [St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis), [Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri), completed in 1891.
