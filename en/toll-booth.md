The **Toll Booth** is a Transport building that provides Car services, added in the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) expansion pack.

Toll Booth are designed to allow you to charge Sims for use of a stretch of road, however they greatly worsen congestion and generally have a much lower capacity than the network they are on.

In total there are four distinct variants of Toll Booths, each serving one of the different Major Transport Networks ([Road](/road), [Avenue](/avenue), [Highway](/highway) and [Ground Highway](/ground-highway)) in SimCity 4.

## Beta
