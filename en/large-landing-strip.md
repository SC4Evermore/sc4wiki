The **Large Landing Strip** is a Transport building that provides Air services, designed to provide Commercial CAP Relief.

Airports provide Commercial Office Cap Relief proportional to their Usage.

The Large Landing Strip is the final lot in the Landing Strip-class Airport chain, preceded by both the [Small Landing Strip](/small-landing-strip) and [Medium Landing Strip](/medium-landing-strip). To further increase capacity, lots in the [Municipal Airport](/small-municipal-airport) chain must be used.
