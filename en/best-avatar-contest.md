The **Simtropolis Best Avatar Contest** (abbreviates as **BAC**) was an informal forum contest which took place in the Summer of 2006. It was won by Chocolatemax, but it is also noted for a high level of cheating by the now-exiled member [Constantina](/people/constantina), to seize second place: the plot became clear only recently.
