The **Large Municipal Airport** is a Transport building that provides Air services, designed to provide Commercial CAP Relief.

Airports provide Commercial Office Cap Relief proportional to their Usage.

The Large Municipal Airport is the largest lot in the Munincipal-class Airport chain, preceded by both the [Small Municipal Airport](/small-municipal-airport) and the [Medium Municipal Airport](/medium-municipal-airport). To further increase capacity, lots in the [International Airport](/small-international-airport) chain must be used.
