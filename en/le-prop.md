[ILive](/people/ilive)s **LEProp** allows you to easily modify your [lots](/lot-editor). This tool includes the ability to add custom icons and descriptions. It also allows you to change the menus where you want to show your lots, along with other modifying function.

This tool is available at [SC4Devotion](http://sc4devotion.com/csxlex/lex_filedesc.php?lotGET=657) and [Simtropolis](http://www.simtropolis.com/modding/index.cfm?p=details&id=355).
