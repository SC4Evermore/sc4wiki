The **Rural Roads** plugin is a transit modification produced by [dedgren](/people/dedgren) of the [NAM Team](/nam-team). The initial version was released on March 7, 2009.

The Rural Roads Mod includes a wide variety of new puzzle pieces, allowing the inclusion of US/Canada style dashed road lines into rural areas of quads and cities, as well as adding Wide-Radius curves to three [Street Addon Mod](/street-addon-mod) Texture Sets.
