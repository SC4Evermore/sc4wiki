The **Port** is a Transport building that provides Freight Shipping services, designed to remove Industrial Freight traffic from your city.

Ports act as one of the two possible destinations for Freight Trucks.

## Beta

In beta, it appears that the Port was to be the first stage in an upgradeable chain of buildings, followed by the [Major Port](/major-port) and finalised with the [Seaport](/seaport).
