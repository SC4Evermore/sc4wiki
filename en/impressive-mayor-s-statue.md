The **Mayor's Statue** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that is very desirable to both residential and commercial sims.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "New Stone Statue Makes Mayor Permanently Yours".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03210000 Statue 2 - Male](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03210000-statue-2-male), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **30,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **60 Points**
