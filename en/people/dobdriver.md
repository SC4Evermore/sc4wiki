**dobdriver**, also known as **drunkapple** at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis), is an Australian mapper and one of the founding members of [Team Downunder](/team-downunder). He has produced a number of Australian maps, notable for their vast size and the usage of 16-bit mapping techniques on his more recent releases.
