RippleJet is a Finnish modder, who has been around since December 2005. He is a member of [BSC](/bsc). He leads the development of the [Colossus Addon Mod](/cam), a mod which adds several new building stages to the game, further balancing the RCI Simulator. He is an administrator at [SimCity 4 Devotion](/sc4d). He also created several small modifications and the [Census Repository Facility - Version 2](/census-repository-facility-version-2).

Outside the CAM, RippleJet's accomplishments include his involvement in the [X-Ports](/x-ports) project, which has resulted in the release of the fully auto-upgradeable [AC Functional Airports](/ac-functional-airports) and [BSC Functional Seaports](/bsc-functional-seaports). He is widely known for his various experiments with the game, including his recent exploration into the effects of [Transit-Enabled Lots](/transit-enable) on the [Traffic Simulator](/traffic-simulator).
