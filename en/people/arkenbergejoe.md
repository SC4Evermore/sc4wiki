<!-- unconverted template: Teamlabels --> **ArkenbergeJoe** is a German [NAM Team](/nam-team), [SFBT](/sfbt) and [RAM](/ram) member, well known for his (heavy) rail viaduct puzzle pieces that have been added to the [NAM](/nam). He also created several highway onramps, as well as several sets of large lots for [Ch. P.](/people/ch-p)'s famous [Plattenbau](/plattenbau) BATs. At [SimCityKurier](/simcitykurier), ArkenbergeJoe maintains an extensive section with NAM-related information, such as showcasing the NAM items, providing hints for using the content in the game, as well as beta reports for new NAM additions.
