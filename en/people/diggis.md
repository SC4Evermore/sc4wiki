**Diggis** is a Kiwi member of the community living in London, England. He was an active member of SimPeg rising to the rank of Moderator, largly through his work on the [Prop](/prop) pack registy hosted on the old Simpeg. This was lost during the forums upgrade and the list was moved to SC4D [here](http://www.sc4devotion.com/dependencies.html). Due to his work on the [SAM](/sam), totally due to his desire to have it as soon as possible, Diggis became a member of the [NAM](/nam) team.

Diggis then joined the BSC, based largly on his work on [MML](/mml)'s. He was in dispair to learn of the development of the [DAMN](/damn), rendering all his knowledge of [MML](/mml)'s obsolete, so threw himself into learning to create [DAMN](/damn) files. Since joining the [BSC](/bsc) he has learned to [BAT](/bat) and begun to create a series of river and pond lots to compliment [Callagrafx](/people/callagrafx)'s rerendered [SimGoober](/people/simgoober)'s canals. He has also opened a [Prop](/prop) Request Thread [here](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=5149.0), called Diggis's Detail Design, for lotters who need that special prop that isn't available.

Diggis has also worked very closely with the [NUTS](/nuts) team, creating a series of sports based props and textures, and has also helped them learn to [BAT](/bat) and Mod, upto teaching [Paroch](/people/paroch) to [BAT](/bat) in a small dutch pub over a beer or 5. This culminated in an invitation to be a full member of the [NUTS](/nuts) team.
