**ardecila** is an inactive American BATer and transit modder, best known for his work for the BAT team [NDEX](/ndex). He also worked with [Tarkus](/people/tarkus) on Version 3.0 of the [RealHighway](/realhighway) mod, and with [blahdy](/people/blahdy) and [Swamper77](/people/swamper77) on the [Boston Big Dig](/boston-big-dig) project in 2006.
