**Teirusu** is an American member who was one of the pioneers of [Transit Modding](/transit-modding) and [Terrain Modding](/terrain-modding). He is responsible for the Road Turning Lanes and the Road, Street, and One-Way Road Roundabout Plugins in the NAM and is recognized as the discoverer of [Override Networks](/override-network), as a result of his groundbreaking research into [RUL 0x10000002](/rul-0x10000002).

He also released two terrain mods, the Snow/Arctic Terrain Mod and had an unfinished Desert Terrain Mod (later finished by [Thalassicus](/people/thalassicus)), and is also well known for his series of Terrain Tool modifications, including the well known [Rain Mod](/extraterraintools2). He formerly ran a small site, [Teirusu's Niche](/teirusu-and#39-s-niche), but as of 2008, the site has gone down and many of his files have become unavailable.
