**eggman121** is an Australian transit modder, who has been a part of the [NAM Team](/nam-team) since 2013. He became first known for his work designing catenaries for the [RealRailway](/realrailway) project, upon which he was invited to join the team; he later made his name with [RealHighway](/realhighway) interchange designs, as shown in the [SC4 Devotion](/sc4-devotion) edition of the "Show Us Your Interchanges" thread.

During [Swordmaster](/people/swordmaster)'s absence, eggman121 took over as lead developer for the [RealRailway](/realrailway) in 2014. He launched the FlexTrack component for NAM 33, which incorporated and improved most of Swordmaster's initial ideas for draggable curves and switches.
