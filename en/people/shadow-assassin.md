**Shadow Assassin** (often called **SA**) is a long-time member of the community, having authored many [Mayor's Diaries](/mayor-and#39-s-diaries), and since 2007, has been member of the [NAM Team](/nam-team) and [NHP](/nhp).

His most recent MD, [Whitefall, Iridar](/whitefall-iridar) was one of the first three MDs inducted into the [SC4D Mayors' Diary Hall of Fame](/sc4d-mayors-and#39-diary-hall-of-fame). As part of his work with the NAM Team, he is well-known for his [RHW Euro Texture Replacement Mod](/plugin/sa-rhw-euro-texture-replacement-mod), a [texture mod](/texture-mod) which replaces the standard North American textures included in the [Rural Highway Mod](/rural-highway-mod) with more international textures. Under the NHP label, he has released several maps, as well as the [NHP Hole Digging Lots](/plugin/nhp-hole-digging-lots). He also released a series of Cycleway lots as an independent developer, before joining either team.
