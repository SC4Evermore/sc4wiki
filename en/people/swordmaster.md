Became a NAM Team Assiocate on 5 November 2012 after starting a railroad [retexture project](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=15223.0). Promoted to full NAM Team member on 6 January 2013. The name RealRailway was come up with in a skype chat with [noahclem](/people/noahclem) on 8 January. Between then and June, the RRW's design was fleshed out, and the project went public on 14 June 2013.

Swordmaster's MD is [Port St Claire](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=15156.0), started on 8 October 2012. It was OSITM (Of Special Interest This Month) in December of that year.

He is also a member of the NUTs (since November 2012) and a Global Moderator for SC4Devotion (since 28 November 2013).
