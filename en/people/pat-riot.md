**Pat Riot** joined the community first at [ST](/st) in May 2006. His name there is *patriots_1228*. He began [BATing](/bat) and [CJing](/cj) shortly after, and has since released more then 9 [CJs](/cj) and about 50 [BATs](/bat). He first founded a [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston) dedicated team [NEBB](/nebb), and his love of Boston continues to shine through in his work today, whether it is recreations of Boston towers \[One Beacon Street\] or dedications to notable Boston people, such as [Josh Beckett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Beckett) ([Beckett Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beckett_Tower)). Shortly after NEBB, he joined [BLaM](/blam) for a summer, before leaving to form [Team Grater](/team-grater) with old [Simtropolis](/simtropolis) member [Krio](/people/krio). he currently resides at [ST](/st) and is also a [City Journal](/city-journal) Critic.
