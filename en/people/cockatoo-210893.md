**Cockatoo-210893**, better known as Cockatoo, resides in Australia as a BATer for the [Hong Kong / Asian BAT Team](/hkabt). Cockatoo has worked on several highrises from Hong Kong, as well as other projects, released on the [STEX](/stex), and some of it on the [LEX](/lex). Cockatoo also won a [Trixie](/trixie) award in 2007 for "most improved BATter".
