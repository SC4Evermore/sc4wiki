**MandelSoft** (formerly **mrtnrln**) is a Dutch transit modder who has been an extremely prolific member of the [NAM Team](/nam-team), and was also a long-time member of [ITC](/itc) before its closure in January [2010](/2010). He is also presently a moderator at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis).

His first major projects were the [Highway Restyling Mod](/plugin/highway-re-styling-mod), the [Light Replacement Mod](/plugin/light-replacement-mod), and various highway signs. He has also has one MD: [Imaginia](/imaginia).

### Co-operations

Mrtnrln has made two sign packs for [Haljackey](/people/haljackey)'s [Greater Terran Region](/greater-terran-region), both of which are available for all to download on the [STEX](/stex).

He also has a Sign Request Room, where members can request custom signs.
