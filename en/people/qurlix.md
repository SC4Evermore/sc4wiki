**qurlix** is an semi-inactive American member, formerly involved with [NAM Team](/nam-team) until 2007, as well as [BLaM](/blam). He is perhaps best known for co-founding the [Rural Highway Mod](/rural-highway-mod) project in 2005 with [nooneatall](/people/nooneatall), having done all the modding for the Version 1.2 release in November 2005. He also released the well-known Street Cul-De-Sac mod. He planned a couple of other versions for the RHW, Version 1.5 and 1.7, but neither were ever finished. qurlix was also working on a pathing tool, XPath, which also was never released.
