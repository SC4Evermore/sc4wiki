**Swamper77** is an American member of both the [BSC](/bsc) and the [NAM Team](/nam-team). He is well-known for his heavily-detailed automata, which often include a number of animated parts. He is also responsible for contributing numerous puzzle pieces to the NAM, including the diagonal Elevated Heavy Rail viaduct pieces that first appeared in the June 2007 NAM release, and is a pathing specialist.

He also authored an MD, [Hayden Sound](/hayden-sound), which is currently inactive.
