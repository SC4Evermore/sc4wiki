**Tropod** is one of the most prolific modders in the history of SimCity 4 content, whose work was crucial in laying the foundation for the entire area of modding. He founded the [NAM Team](/nam-team), contributing a substantial body of work to the [Network Addon Mod](/network-addon-mod) continuing to this day, and his discoveries on building stages eventually led to the development of the [Colossus Addon Mod](/colossus-addon-mod). He is also a moderator at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis).
