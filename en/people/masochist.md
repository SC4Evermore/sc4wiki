**Masochist** (also known as **Mas**) is a member of the SC4 Community that has been active since December of 2005. He is perhaps most notable as the originator of the [SC4 Picture Competition](/picture-competition) on [SC4 Devotion](/simcity-4-devotion). Masochist is currently an administrator at [SimVision](/simvision), and keeps up a CJ/MD called [Condor Bay](/condor-bay) on both SC4D and [Simtropolis](/simtropolis).
