**BLaM** **TSC** **BATers** **Modders** **NAM**

**fukuda** is a BATer, transit modder and bridge maker from Spain, who was one of the charter members of the former [BLaM](/blam) Team. He is perhaps best known for his series of industrial buildings, and being one of the very first modders to produce a custom bridge in 2006. He was also active for a time as a member of the [NAM Team](/nam-team) and [TSC](/tsc).
