**superhands** (formerly **bighead99** and **bighead99999**) is an Australian member of the [NAM Team](/nam-team), who also does some [BATing](/bat). He is perhaps first known for contributing a number of textures to the [Single Track Rail](/single-track-rail) Plugin, yet more notably texture and design work for the [Network Widening Mod](/network-widening-mod). The year 2011 saw superhands pet project the [Nam Retextures &amp; Cosmetic Mod](/nam-retextures-and-amp-cosmetic-mod) packaged and released to the public. The unofficial Nam team motto "we like to surprise people" is something the member lives by.
