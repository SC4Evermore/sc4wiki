<!-- unconverted template: teamlabels --> **CasperVg** is a Belgian member of the SimCity 4 community. CasperVg is an administrator of [SC4Evermore](https://www.sc4evermore.com/), the successor of [SimCity 4 Devotion](/simcity-4-devotion), and a member of the [NAM](/network-addon-mod) Team and the [BSC](/bsc). CasperVg releases lots, [DLL plugins](/dll-plugins) and tools for [SimCity 4](/simcity-4), mainly on [Simtropolis](/simtropolis), SC4Evermore and GitHub.

## SC4D

CasperVg joined [SC4D](/sc4d) in August 2007. CasperVg wrote a [MD](/md), [Casper's Portfolio](/casper-s-portfolio), that rose to the "rank" of [Classics](/classics). It was most notable for its huge monthly post counts and the large amount of "spoiler" updates. CasperVg was a NAM Associate, a global moderator of SC4D, and in charge of the [Where can I find this?](/where-can-i-find-this) board. In December 2009, CasperVg was invited to join the BSC.

## DLL Plugins

- [SCD3D11](/plugin/scd3d11), a Direct3D 11 renderer that replaces the DirectX 7 renderer of the game.
- [SC4 Plop and Paint](/plugin/sc4-plop-and-paint), an in-game panel to plop lots and paint props and flora.
- [Disable FPS Limits](/plugin/sc4-disable-fps-limits), which removes the frame rate limits at each simulation speed.
- [SC4 Render Services](/plugin/sc4-render-services), a library DLL with rendering services for other DLL plugins.

The [DLL Plugins](/dll-plugins) page lists more, such as the Zoning Extensions, Terrain Extensions and Effects Extensions DLLs. CasperVg also contributes to [gzcom-dll](https://github.com/SC4SDK/gzcom-dll), the library that many DLL plugins use.

## Tools

- [SC4PIM-X 2026](/sc4pim), a community edition of the SC4PIM-X plugin manager and lot editor by [wouanagaine](/people/wouanagaine). CasperVg rebuilt the tool from its compiled files, because the original source code was lost, and has added many new features.
- An LTEXT tool for SimCity 4 (2013).
- The LEX API (2013–2016), which gave developers access to the LEX, the Lot Exchange of SC4 Devotion. It is no longer available, because the LEX no longer exists in that form since SC4 Devotion became SC4Evermore.
