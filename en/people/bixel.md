**bixel** is an American [BATer](/bater), best known for his numerous Hong Kong buildings released under the [Hong Kong/Asian BAT Team](/hong-kong/asian-bat-team) (HKABT). He is a former member of the [Around The Roses](/former-teams#around-the-roses) project, which recreated buildings from Portland, Oregon (his former hometown), and recently joined the revived [Team Grater](/tg).
