Andreharv is an American transit modder who is best known for his efforts in 2008-2009, creating the first [Maxis](/maxis) [Highway](/transit-network#highway-networks) interchanges developed in the previous several years. He was added to the [NAM Associates](/nam/members#associates) on September [2008](/2008) , and then promoted to a full [NAM team member](/nam/members) in January [2009](/2009), though is currently inactive in the community.
