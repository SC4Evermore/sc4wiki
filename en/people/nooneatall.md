**nooneatall** is a Canadian member, best known as the co-founder of the then-[Rural Highway Mod](/rural-highway-mod) (now RealHighway) project with [qurlix](/people/qurlix). He contributed most of the textures to the initial Version 12 release. He was also formerly a tester for the [NAM Team](/nam-team), but has not been active since 2005.
