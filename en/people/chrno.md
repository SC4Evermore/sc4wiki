**Chrno** is an American traffic modder who was active in 2007-2008. He contributed the Avenue Wide-Radius 45-degree and 90-degree puzzle pieces to the [NAM](/nam), and his idea for extendable turn lanes led to the development of [Turning Lane Extension Pieces](/turning-lane-extension-pieces).
