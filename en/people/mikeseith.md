**BSC** **mikeseith** is an American member of the [BSC](/bsc), best known for his car dealership BATs and automata. He also has contributed to several transit-modding projects, among them, the original iteration of Ground Light Rail (before it was incorporated into the NAM) and also provided textures for the OWR-5 network in the [Network Widening Mod](/network-widening-mod).
