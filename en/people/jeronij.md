**Jeronij** is a user who has been active in the SC4 community for a number of years. He first gained prominence as the originator of the award-winning city journal [Sculpting Columbia River](/sculpting-columbia-river), as well as releasing multiple sets of concrete walls for use on slopes and seashores. Jeronij is also the founder/administrator of [SC4 Devotion](/sc4d), and a contributing member of the custom content creation group [BSC](/bsc).
