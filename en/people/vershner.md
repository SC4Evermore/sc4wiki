**vershner** is a British texture artist, who was active at [SC4 Devotion](/sc4-devotion) in [2009](/2009) and [2010](/2010), and is best known for creating a new set of textures for the [High Speed Rail Project](/high-speed-rail-project) during that time. Those textures became the standard for that project when it was integrated into the NAM itself with the Version 31.0 release of March [2013](/2013).
