### ITC Team

**ITC** is a team founded on April 19th, 2008. At first, ITC was named after it's founders, [Yoshi](/people/yoshi), [Travis](/people/travis) and [CasperVg](/people/caspervg), but since two of the three founders retired, it was officially changed to *International Team of Creators* on the 25th of December [2008](/2008) to reflect the many nations it's team members represent. ITC is currently led by [Threestooges](/people/threestooges) and [CasperVg](/people/caspervg).

ITC intends to produce a wide variety of custom content ranging from network mods, bridges, and general custom content and to have a nurturing, friendly atmosphere within which to conduct such actions while simultaneously providing a source for feedback from those on the cutting edge of development. It intends to further those goals through it's development of content as it develops as a team. Theres also inspiration to move ITC to the Certified boards on Devotions from its current Location in the Other Teams section...
