**smoncrie** is a Canadian member, best known for his involvement in the [NAM Team](/nam-team) and his Hole-Digging Lots, which allow for precise raising and lowering of terrain.

He was elected as leader of the NAM Team in 2005 after [Tropod](/people/tropod) went into semi-retirement, but went on extended hiatus shortly thereafter. He returned to the community in 2008, contributing a number of long sought-after new features to the NAM, such as Double Height Monorail and Elevated Light Rail. He also collaborated with [Tarkus](/people/tarkus) on RHW Release 3, contributing several RHW bridges.
