**c.p.** (also known as **cycledogg** and originally **cyclepuppy**) is an American member of the [BSC](/bsc), who is perhaps best known for his popular series of terrain mods and terrain textures, his tree sets and controllers, and his early 19th century American [BATs](/bats). He also produced a series of maps, and his initial Mega Prop Pack is one of the most frequently used Dependencies for Lots.

## List of Works

- Terrain Mods

  - Painted Desert Terrain Mod
  - Olympic Terrain Mod
  - Missouri Breaks Terrain Mod
  - Canyonlands Terrain Mod
  - Grand Canyon Terrain Mod
  - Italia Terrain Mod
  - Meadowshire Terrain Mod
