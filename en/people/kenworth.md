**SFBT** **Kenworth** is a German [SFBT](/sfbt) member, known for his German rail catenary props and S-Bahn stations, as well as a number of railroad items, such as a German RegioRunner (Regionalbahn) (also released as [automata](/automata) by [Swamper77](/people/swamper77)) and several passenger and freight engines.
