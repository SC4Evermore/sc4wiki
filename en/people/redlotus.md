**redlotus** was one of the earliest transit modders in the SC4 Community, and a founding member of what is now the [NAM Team](/nam-team) in 2004. He made substantial contributions to the [NAM](/nam) and his guide "Creating Interchanges for Sim City 4" from 2004 is still a well-utilized modding reference resource for the creation of [Puzzle Pieces](/puzzle-piece). As of this writing, he is not currently active in the community.
