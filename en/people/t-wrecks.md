**T Wrecks** is a German lotter and modder, known for his involvement with the [SFBT](/sfbt) and [BSC](/bsc), as well as collaborations with the [Hong Kong/Asian BAT Team](/hkabt) (HKABT), [Barry Sanders Project](/barry-sanders-project), and several independent BATers. Many of the lots that are available at [SimCityKurier](/simcitykurier) have been modded by him. He is also a moderator at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis).

## Works

Apart from the collaboration works mentioned above, T Wrecks has published a large set of modified [SC4Desc](/sc4desc) files with updated stats, a set of diagonal filler lots, various parks and plazas, as well as updated lots for many [NDEX](/ndex) BATs (available at [SimCityKurier](/simcitykurier)).
