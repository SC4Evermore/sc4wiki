**TheQuiltedLlama**, also known as **mjig_dudy** is the founder of the [SMP](/smp) team, a former member of [BLaM](/blam), and the webmaster of [SimCity Mild Enthusiasm](/simcity-mild-enthusiasm). He is perhaps best known for uploading the "Nuclear-Powered Starbucks Canary Wharf" \[*sic*\], a [Prank BAT](/prank-bat), to the [STEX](/simtropolis). He also produced a popular texture mod for the RHW-2 network in the [Rural Highway Mod](/rural-highway-mod).
