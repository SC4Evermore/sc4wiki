**frimi** is a German [SFBT](/sfbt) member, well known for the creation of the [Euro Road Texture Mod](/euro-road-texture-mod) (ERTM). Picking up [Thorvin](/people/thorvin)'s work, he created European textures for all Maxis road textures, as well as textures for many [NAM](/nam) items. After his retirement from the modding community, [Andreas](/people/andreas) continued the work on the ERTM.
