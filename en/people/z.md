**z** (also known as **z1** at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis)) is an American member, who is the leader of the [RTMT Team](/rtmt-team) and a member of the [NAM Team](/nam-team). He is also well-known for his extensive research into the traffic simulator, resulting in the Simulator Z Traffic Plugin, which was added to the [NAM](/nam) for the January 2009 release. He also released a popular Traffic Volume DataView mod.
