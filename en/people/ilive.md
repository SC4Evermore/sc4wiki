**ILive** is the creator of [Reader](/reader), which is one of the (if not THE) most important tools for modding [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) ever created. It has been critical to the creation of many of the LOTs, BATs and MODs that currently exist.

ILive has also created various other tools, such as the [LE Prop](/le-prop) and [UIeditor](/uieditor).
