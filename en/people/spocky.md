**Spocky** is a German [BATter](/bat), member of the [SFBT](/sfbt). Most of his contributions are filling blanks that were left in the game, such as his rural powerline props, several signalboxes, and a set of highway bridge construction props. He is one of the administrators of [SimCityPlaza](/simcityplaza) and a moderator at [SimForum](/simforum). **SFBT**
