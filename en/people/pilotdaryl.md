**Pilotdaryl** (also **PD** or **Flyin' High**) is the founder and leader of the [Sim City Aviation Group](/scag). He joined the Sim City Community at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis) in January 2006 and joined [SimCity 4 Devotion](/simcity-4-devotion) when it opened later that year. Pilotdaryl is known for his aircraft BATting projects and contributions to the SCAG including the [Boeing 777](/scag#current-projects) and the [MD-80](/scag#current-projects). He was also involved in the [Simtropolis Roadgeek Union](/former-teams#simtropolis-roadgeek-union), which specialized in making transport products for SimCity 4.

Pilotdaryl is currently 21 years old and lives in Burnaby, British Columbia.
