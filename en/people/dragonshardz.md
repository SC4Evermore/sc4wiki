**dragonshardz** is an American and former member of [SC4D](/sc4d), [ST](/simtropolis) and [SimPeg](/simpeg). She was a member of the [3RR](/3rr) Team until 2015 and was responsible for the upkeep of the [3RR Honors List](/3rr-honors-list), in collaboration with [thundercrack83](/people/thundercrack83), and was also a non-[NAM](/nam) affiliated tester for the [High Speed Rail Project](/high-speed-rail-project). She briefly authored two [Mayor Diaries](/mayor-diaries).

She can be reached on the SC4Evermore Discord, Xitter, mastodon.social, and Bluesky under the same username. **3RR**
