He is widely known in the community for various contributions and meaningful posts, as well as helping new members.

Some facts about Haljackey are found below:

- He is a member of the [Simtropolis Help Squad](/simtropolis-help-squad) (SHS).
- He is an associate member of the [NAM Team](/nam-team).
- Simtropolis [Member of the Month](/member-of-the-month) (MOTM) and Trixie winner. [Interview.](http://www.simtropolis.com/forum/messageview.cfm?catid=142&threadid=104100&enterthread=y&STARTPAGE=1)
- His award-winning City Journal/Mayor's Diary, [The Greater Terran Region](/the-greater-terran-region) (GTR for short).
- He is particularly well known for his series of tutorials; many about the [Rural Highway Mod](/rural-highway-mod), [one of which](http://www.simtropolis.com/forum/messageview.cfm?catid=124&threadid=86988&enterthread=y) introduced the popular [Multi-RHW](/rural-highway-mod#multi-rhw-technique) concept. Haljackey continues to make tutorials/guides to this day [1](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?board=235.0)[2](http://www.simtropolis.com/forum/index.cfm?FTVAR_SUBCAT=137&nocookies=y&subcatname=SimCity%20Omnibus%3A%20%20Search%20Here%20First).
- Bringing the highway/interchange showcase to a popular level by starting the ["Show Us Your Interchanges!"](http://www.simtropolis.com/forum/messageview.cfm?catid=23&threadid=89089&enterthread=y) thread and posting many of his custom interchanges there.
- [His videos on Youtube](http://www.youtube.com/view_play_list?p=171E92A82C525152) are quite popular because he showcases his cities from a unique "[U-Drive-It](/u-drive-it)" (UDI) perspective. Most videos are also embedded in the [GTR](/gtr).
- "[Haljackey's SC4 Archives](/haljackey-and#39-s-sc4-archives)" is a place where Haljackey showcases older, sometimes obselete work from the [NAM Team](/nam-team), as well as many of his old, nostalgic creations.

Haljackey is currently active on both [Simtropolis](/simtropolis) and [SC4 Devotion](/sc4-devotion).
