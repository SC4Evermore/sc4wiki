**BarbyW** is one of the most prolific [Lotters](/lot-editor) in the community. A longstanding member of [BSC](/bsc) and an administrator at [SimCity 4 Devotion](/simcity-4-devotion)

## History

BarbyW was initially recruited by [GrampaAl](/people/grampaal) to test [CSX](/csx) lots in November 2003. She started making lots and became part of the [CSX](/csx) Team in December 2003. Lots made by her were modded by [GrampaAl](/people/grampaal) and released under the CSX banner. In June 2004 she joined [BSC](/bsc) as a lotter but was trained to become a Lot Technician - modding of lots to be game assets. Since then she has become an integral part of [BSC](/bsc) and works behind the scenes on projects with [BATers](/bat) as well as make lots on her own. She has worked closely with [D66](/people/d66) and [Mickebear](/people/mickebear) on lotting and modding their work and notably with [ripptide](/people/ripptide) on the [SNM](/simnation-military) project. Recently she has worked with [RippleJet](/people/ripplejet) on the functional air and seaports project and also helped to test the [X-Tool](/x-tool).
