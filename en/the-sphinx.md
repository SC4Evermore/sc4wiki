**The Sphinx** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Great Sphinx of Giza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Sphinx_of_Giza) in [Giza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giza), [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt).
