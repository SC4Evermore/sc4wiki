The **Convention Center** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that greatly increases your city's Commercial Office capacities.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "From UFOs to CEOs, New Convention Center Will Host Them All".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03430000 Convention Center](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03430000-convention-center), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Commercial Population** in excess of **25,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **40 Points**
- At least **One Medium or Large Airport**

### U Drive It Missions

- Bus Mission: Sick Bus Driver
