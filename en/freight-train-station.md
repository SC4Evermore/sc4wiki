The **Freight Train Station** is a Transport building that provides Freight [rail](/rail) services.

Freight Stations are designed to be placed alongside the [Rail Network](/rail), allowing Freight to be transferred from Freight Trucks to Freight Trains in a one-way operation. A side effect is that, in an unmodded game, once Freight boards a train, it must leave the city by train.

## In the NAM

In NAM 36, the Freight Train Station transships freight in both directions, from road to [rail](/rail), and also from [rail](/rail) to road. Thus the NAM Freight Train Station can be used in conjunction with the Maxis [Seaport](/seaport) to transfer freight from rail, to road, then to [Seaport](/seaport).
