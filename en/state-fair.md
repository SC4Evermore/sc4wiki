The **State Fair** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that serves as an icon of good Agricultural management and growth in your city.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "No Bull But A Lot of Beef At State Fair Opening".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03360000 State Fair](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03360000-state-fair), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Population** in excess of **3,500**
- **Agricultural Industry** provides in excess of **1,200 Jobs**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **48 Points**

### U Drive It Missions

- Ice Cream Truck Mission: Ice Cream Delivery

## Reference Buildings

The lot's [Building](/building) is loosely based on the [Hall of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hall_of_State) in [Dallas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas), [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas), built in 1936. At the time, it was the most expensive building per unit of measure built in Texas.  
The game's representation mostly only retains the curved entrance, the building's other intricate details and the surrounding Art Deco sculptures and fountains aren't included.

## Trivia

The State Fair has both of the most elaborate Animated 3D models in the game, the Texan Greeter and Ferris Wheel.

The Greeter removes his hat to greet visitors, then replaces it and The Ferris Wheel turns and will occasionally slow down and stop. Most other animated objects use ATP 2D Animations instead.
