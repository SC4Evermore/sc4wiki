The **Apartments** are a pair of statistically identical Medium-Wealth Residential Buildings [^1][^2] that grows on only Medium and High Density Residential zones.

## Variant Differences

Variant 2 is merely a flipped version of Variant 1. They are otherwise indistinguishable and statistically identical.
