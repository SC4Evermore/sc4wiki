**RW4** is the 3D model format used by [SimCity's](/simcity-2013) [RenderWare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RenderWare) 4 graphics engine.

Its [DBPF](/dbpf) 3.0 [Type ID](/type-id) is **2F4E681B**

## SimCity

Used to store 3D Models, DirectDraw Surfaces, lighting maps, interior maps and the locations of and images used for Model Decals.
