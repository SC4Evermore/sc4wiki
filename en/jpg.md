A **JPG** (**JPEG**) is an image file.

Its [DBPF](/dbpf) 3.0 [Type ID](/type-id) is **3F8662EA**.

## SimCity 4

JPEG is not used by [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) itself, but it is the recommended format for including imagery in a custom content package, or to post pictures on a [website](/wikipedia-website).

## SimCity

[SimCity (2013)](/simcity-2013) uses JPEG to to store various images used on the UI.

More information about the file format in general can be found on Wikipedia: [JPG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JPG)
