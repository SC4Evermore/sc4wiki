**CapitalSimCity** (**CSC**) is a Spanish fansite, that was created on June 6th, [2003](/2003), and has since grown into a major fansite for various simulation games, such as: [CitiesXL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CitiesXL), [The Sims 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sims_2) and [SimCity Societies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SimCity_Societies). It boasts a large exchange and fairly active forums, as well a large tutorial section.
