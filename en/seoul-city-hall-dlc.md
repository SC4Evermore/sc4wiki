The **Seoul City Hall** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 004** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Seoul Metropolitan Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_Metropolitan_Library) (Was Seoul City Hall in 2003) in, [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul), [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea) as it appeared in 2003.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "City Hall Square Destined To Be The Center Of \#city# Life".
