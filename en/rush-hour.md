**Rush Hour** is [SimCity 4](/simcity-4)'s only expansion pack. It was released September 22, 2003.

Rush Hour is exactly the same as the [Deluxe Edition](/deluxe-edition) except that this expansion pack is sold separately from [SimCity 4](/simcity-4).

The expansion pack, among other things, enhances the range of transportation facilities, allows the player to trace traffic flow, [control vehicles](/u-drive-it), and construct larger civic facilities. It also introduces a new range of contemporary Europe-styled buildings.
