The **Major Art Museum** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides [Education](/education) to your [Sims](/sims).

Education buildings increase the Education Quotient over a coverage radius. Some buildings have variable coverage radii which can be changed using a School Bus Funding slider, however the majority have a fixed radius that cannot be expanded or contracted without modding the game.

Museums are designed to provide a School-like effect on an older Sim's life, however they are still treated as Schools by the game's code. The Major Art Museum provides a progressively stronger effect as a household gets older.

In Museum rankings, it is superior only to the [City Museum](/city-museum).

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Concentrated Cultural Collections At New Art Museum Lead To Long Lines".
