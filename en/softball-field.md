The **Softball Field** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Beautification and Recreation for your [Sims](/sims).

Parks provide a [Park Effect](/park-effect) over a fixed radius, in addition to usually cleaning up air and water pollution and increasing [CAPs](/cap).

## Trivia

Properties left over in this building's exemplar indicates that this lot may have been originally intended to be a Reward, possibly as somewhat of a Large Playground.
