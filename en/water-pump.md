The **Water Pump** is a utility building that provides water for your [Sims](/sims).

Water pumps are designed to be connected to zones and buildings with [water pipes](/transit-network#water-pipes), but do not have to be as they provide water within a three-tile radius.

The Water Pump is the middle-tier water production building, supplying 20,000 cubic metres of water a month to your city.

It produces almost ten times the amount of water the [Water Tower](/water-tower) supplies for only seven times the monthly expense but only producing a tenth of the amount the [Large Water Pump](/large-water-pump) supplies at a ninth of the monthly expense.
