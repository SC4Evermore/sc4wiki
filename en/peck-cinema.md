**Peck Cinema** is a High Wealth Commercial Services building that grows on Medium Density (Some variants only) and High Density Commercial zones.

The building appears to be somewhat based on [Castro Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castro_Theatre) in [San francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_francisco), although this has not been confirmed.
