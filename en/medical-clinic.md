The **Medical Clinic** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Healthcare for your [Sims](/sims) in SimCity 4.

Clinics increase your city's Health Quotient and your Sims' Lifespans depending on their funding over a variable radius which can be adjusted using the Vehicle Funding slider in the building's Query Panel up until there are no more free capacity.

The **Medical Clinic** is the cheapest and least effective Healthcare building in SimCity 4, with a weaker overall effect than both the [Large Medical Center](/large-medical-center) and the [Disease Research Center](/disease-research-center).

## Trivia

The original name of this building was **Urgent Care Clinic**.
