**Retail Stores** is the name used by many small CS§§ buildings intended as, utterly surprisingly, Retail Stores. There are many different models and sizes for all of the tilesets in SimCity 4. Strangely, Building Variant 2 was not included in SimCity 4 or the Rush Hour Expansion Pack.

Due to Wiki limitations, they must be linked below. Please click the link according to the model that applies to you.

## Other Variants

The CS§ variants are called [Small Shops](/small-shops) and the CS§§§ variants are called [Boutiques](/boutiques).
