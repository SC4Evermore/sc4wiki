The **TSC Exchange**, part of the [ToutSimCity Fansite](/tsc-website), is the main upload base for creations made by the [TSC Team](/tsc-team). Uploading is free, but uploads are moderated before becoming available to the public for download. The exchange allows for the uploading of [BATs](/bat), [LOTs](/lot), [MODs](/mod) and [MAPs](/map), and is only available in French.
