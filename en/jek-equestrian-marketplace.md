**JEK Equestrian Marketplace** is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on Medium Density and High Density Commercial zones.

JEK Equestrian Marketplace is the Euro Tileset equivalent of [Houston Boutiques 3](/houston-boutiques-3), [Chicago Boutiques 3](/chicago-boutiques-3) and [New York Boutiques 3](/new-york-boutiques-3) as evidenced by its exemplar name, 3EuroLuxuryStore3, following the conventions of the other three Boutiques lots.

## Trivia

As exemplar the name for this building is EuroLuxuryStore, it's highly likely that this building was only ever meant to be available in the Euro tileset.[^1]
