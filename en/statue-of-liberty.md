The **Statue of Liberty** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4 Rush Hour/Deluxe.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Statue of Liberty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statue_of_Liberty) in [Liberty Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberty_Island), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York) in the [United States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America), completed in 1886.
