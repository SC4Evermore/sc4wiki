**Shorty Burder** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 035** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

Unlike most of the other DLC Landmarks, this building is completely fictional.

## Trivia

**Shorty Burger** shares almost all of its building properties with [Lucky's Hang Ten](/lucky-and#39-s-hang-ten), [Sobchak Lanes](/sobchak-lanes) and the [Rockefeller Center](/rockefeller-center), the latter of which was probably a mistake. It's also one of only a few DLC buildings that have no files for any language other than US English, a trait it shares with [Lucky's Hang Ten](/lucky-and#39-s-hang-ten) and [Sobchak Lanes](/sobchak-lanes).
