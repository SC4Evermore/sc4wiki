**Education** is a factor in city development that is needed if you want to attract high-tech [industries](/industry). The intelligence of the inhabitants is measured by the *Education Quotient* (*EQ*). The education [Data View](/data-view) shows you where the lowest (and highest) educated areas are, and may help you in choosing the location of a new school or library.
