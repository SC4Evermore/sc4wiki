**Longfellow Castle** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as past of the **Castle Set** free Downloadable Content, whose content is also included in this page.

Longfellow Castle does not provide any services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a purely fictional creation.

No News Ticker fluff entry is created on its placement.

## Castle Set

This DLC also included props suitable for making a Castle in the lot editor.
