**Brick 2 Story** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building with three Identical Variants that grow on All Residential zones.

It is designed to compete with [2 Story House (Large Variants)](/2-story-house#2-story-house-large), [Cape Cod House (Large Variants)](/cape-cod-house#cape-cod-house-large) and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Mashire Quarters](/mashire-quarters), [Hendawi Place](/hendawi-place) and [Cheez Cottage](/cheez-cottage)

## Variant Differences

Variant 2 is a flipped version of Variant 1.

Variant 3 is exactly the same as Variant 1, except a different brick texture was used and the Chimney was moved.

All three variants have exactly the same properties and grow on the same lots.
