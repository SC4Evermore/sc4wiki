**CSGforums** was a site created by [CSGdesign](/people/csgdesign), author of the City Showcase [Natural Growth](/natural-growth). It started out as a blog, CSGdescussions, where specific CSG content could be found, growing rapidly in popularity with the forum section being set up on March 13, [2010](/2010). Visitor growth of CSGforums nearly doubled each month after launch.

The forum hosted a wide variety of threads, including, but not limited to, Glimpses, where one can post pictures from a city without the need of an active [City Showcase](/city-showcase), Tutorials and Reviews, BAT/Lot Showcases, Forum Games and General Discussion.

The blog portion of the site hosts [Natural Growth](/natural-growth) as well as a spin-off [City Showcase](/city-showcase) called "The Tunnings Chapter". It also contains a downloads section featuring CSGdesign's content, as well as a series of tutorials and custom content reviews, featuring contributors [Rionescu](/people/rionescu) and [Gorbag](/people/gorbag). The site also boasted an exchange, the [CSG File Exchange](/csg-file-exchange).

CSGforums has been defunct since mid-[2014](/2014).
