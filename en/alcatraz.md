**Alcatraz** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect. Unlike most other landmarks, Alcatraz carries a Mayor Rating penalty.

It is a recreation of [Alcatraz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcatraz) in [San Francisco Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Bay), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), completed in 1912.
