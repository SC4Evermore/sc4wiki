The **Double 00 Club** is a group of people who have made posts in [3RR](/3rr) whose number ends with two zeros (100, 200, etc.). You can keep track of it in the [3RR Honors List](/3rr-honors-list) (available [here](http://sc4devotion.com/forums/index.php?topic=2750.0)). The Double 00 Club is not a custom content creation team and can be joined by anyone whose post at [SC4D](/sc4d) in the [3RR](/3rr) topic ends with two zeros.
