**Small Shops** is the name used by many small CS§ buildings intended as, utterly surprisingly, Small Shops. There are many different models and sizes for all of the tilesets in SimCity 4. Strangely,

Due to Wiki limitations, they must be linked below. Please click the link according to the model that applies to you.

## Other Variants

The CS§§ variants are called [Retail Stores](/retail-stores) and the CS§§§ variants are called [Boutiques](/boutiques).
