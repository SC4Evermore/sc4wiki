**Capitol Records** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 012** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Capitol Records Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Records_Building) in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California), built in 1956, as it appeared in 2003.

## Trivia

This landmark has possibly the earliest rendering of written Simlish. Possibly due to trademark concerns, the name on the roof of the building doesn't read 'Capitol Records'.
