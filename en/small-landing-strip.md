The **Small Landing Strip** is a Transport building that provides Air services, designed to provide Commercial CAP Relief.

Airports provide Commercial Cap Relief proportional to their Usage.

The Small Landing Strip is, not surprisingly, the lowest capacity Landing Strip-class Airport in the game, with both the [Medium Landing Strip](/medium-landing-strip) and [Large Landing Strip](/large-landing-strip) having a higher capacity.
