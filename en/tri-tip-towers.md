**Tri-Tip Towers** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building that grows on only High Density Residential zones. [^1]

It is designed to primarily compete with [Copur Apartments](/copur-apartments), [Reed Place](/reed-place) and [Wallace Manor](/wallace-manor).

## Bugs

The compression on this building was particularly aggressive and multiple parts of the renders are cut off. The ones used for the image in this article have had those flaws corrected.
