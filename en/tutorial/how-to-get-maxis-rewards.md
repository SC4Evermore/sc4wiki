> **This article is a [tutorial](/tutorials).** Originally written by [RippleJet](/people/ripplejet), from [SimCity 4 Devotion](/simcity-4-devotion).

This list contains all rewards defined by Maxis, both the YIMBY and NIMBY rewards.

The rewards are in the same order as entered in various LUA scripts dealing with them within simcity_1.dat.

The name given for the reward is not necessarily the same as seen in game. They are the names given in the LUA scripts.

The number in front of a reward is the reward ID and the number in front of a UDI mission is the mission ID (you won't need these numbers).

In order to get a reward you either have to fulfil all listed requirements, or successfully complete the UDI mission(s) listed.
