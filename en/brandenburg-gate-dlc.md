The **Brandenburg Gate** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 00C** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Brandenburg Gate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandenburg_Gate) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), built in 1791, roughly as it appeared in 2003.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "#city# Opens Its Doors to the Brandenburg Gate".
