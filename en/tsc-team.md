The **Equipe BAT TSC** (also known as the **TSC Team** on English sites) is a [BAT](/bat) Team that represents the [ToutSimCity](/toutsimcity) Site, hence the name. The team was administrated by [Sciurus](/user-sciurus). The position of team leader was handed over to [Fukuda](/people/fukuda) too (known as Potsdam on ToutSimcity), and is now occupied by [Sciurus](/user-sciurus). The team has produced several [BATs](/bat) and [Lots](/lot), mostly available on the [TSC Exchange](/tsc-exchange) and the [STEX](/stex).
