**Schloss Neuschwanstein** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Neuschwanstein Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuschwanstein_Castle) near [Schwangau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwangau), [Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavaria), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) and the extant buildings were completed in 1892.
