**Mind Scape** is a [Mayor Diary](/mayor-diary) started on February 27, [2007](/2007) by [rooker1](/people/rooker1) at [SC4D](/sc4d). The MD is a classic mixture of several different approaches to Sc4. Robin likes to involve his readers, using contests, trivia, quotes, and references to many other RL and community things, as well as his views on how a city would be built from his perspective. The cities in Mind Scape are very detailed and mostly consist of dirty industrial areas and low wealth residential neighbourhoods. It is very hard to find high [wealth](/wealth) (\$\$\$) development in the residential or commercial areas, although in Robin's busy, overcrowded downtown cores there are glimpses of \$\$\$ CO and CS every once in a while.

Mind Scape had made it to the [SC4D](/sc4d) [Classics](/classics) section of the [MD](/md) forums and was selected by the site's staff for [Of Special Interest this Month](/of-special-interest-this-month) recognition three times over the course of about a year and a half. In November [2008](/2008), Mind Scape was selected by a votes at SC4D as one of the seven members of that site's [MD Hall of Fame](/md-hall-of-fame). Robin's [MD](/md) is currently active.
