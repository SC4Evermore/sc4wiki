The **Chicago Retail Stores 4** (Part of the **[Retail Stores](/retail-stores)** family of Growable buildings) is a Medium-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on all densities of Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic Medium-Wealth stores grouped under the Retail Stores name.

[King Design](/king-design) uses the same model sets and can be considered a Commercial Offices variant of this building.
