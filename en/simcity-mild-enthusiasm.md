**SimCity Mild Enthusiasm** was a SimCity 4 Modsite operated by [mjig_dudy](/people/mjig-dudy). It appears to have been active during 2007, though it is unknown when it went defunct. As the site was hosted on free Bytehost hosting behind the discontinued Nauru domain 'simcitymildenthusiasm(dot)co(dot)nr', nothing of the site has been preserved.
