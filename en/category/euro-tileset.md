This category is to contain all buildings in the Euro Tileset by Maxis. Custom lots should be in [:Category:Euro Tileset/Custom](/category/euro-tileset/custom).

The Euro Tileset contains primarily modern buildings in an International Style, mostly from Europe and ranges from the 1960s to the late 1990s. The tileset and all buildings in it were introduced in the [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack.

It is balanced to be playable with all of the other tilesets, but it is disabled by default when starting a new city. Additionally, parts of the set (Most notably [Small Shops](/small-shops), [Retail Stores](/retail-stores) and [Boutiques](/boutiques)) have had more detail put into them than the default sets, with more detailed lots and less generic naming.
