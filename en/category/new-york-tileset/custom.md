This category is to contain all Custom buildings in the New York Tileset. The Maxis equivalent of this category is [:Category:New York Tileset](/category/new-york-tileset).

The New York Tileset is designed to hold buildings resembling New York City in the 1940s. However like the other two default tilesets, it contains buildings from all across the world.

The stylistic focus of this tileset is on Art Deco and is really the only unifying aspect of all the buildings.
