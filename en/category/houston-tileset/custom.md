This category is to contain all Custom buildings in the Houston Tileset. The Maxis equivalent of this category is [:Category:Houston Tileset](/category/houston-tileset).

The Houston Tileset is designed to hold buildings resembling Houston in the 1990s and 2000s. However like the other two default tilesets, it contains buildings from all across the world.

The stylistic focus of this tileset is on the Contemporary style and is really the only unifying aspect of all the buildings.
