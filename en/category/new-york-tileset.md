This category is to contain all buildings in the New York Tileset by Maxis. For Custom lots, add them to [:Category:New York Tileset/Custom](/category/new-york-tileset/custom).

The New York Tileset is designed to hold buildings resembling New York City in the 1940s. However like the other two default tilesets, it contains buildings from all across the world.

The stylistic focus of this tileset is on Art Deco and is really the only unifying aspect of all the buildings.

## Trivia

The New York tileset is considered to be the most unsightly tileset due to odd choices when it comes to building colours.
