This category contains all categories and articles related to *[SimCity 4](/simcity-4), The Game*, as originally released by Maxis in June 2003, and also the features released in the *Rush Hour* / *[Deluxe Edition](/deluxe-edition)* in September 2003, including information about the simulation and transit networks. SimCity 4 players often refer to the original unmodded Maxis features as "vanilla", or "Maxis vanilla".

One of the most comprehensive guides to [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) is the **Prima Guide**, which can be downloaded at:

  
  
<font color="brown"> **https://ia600202.us.archive.org/25/items/SimCity_4_Rush_Hour_Prima_Official_eGuide/SimCity_4_Rush_Hour_Prima_Official_eGuide.pdf**</font>

For games related to SimCity 4, see [:Category:Other Games](/category/other-games). For information about playing the game, see [:Category:Gameplay](/category/gameplay).
