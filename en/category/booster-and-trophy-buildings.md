Booster and Trophy buildings are [Buildings](/buildings) that either are designed to simultaneously provide a boost to an Industry (R§, CO§§, I-HT, etc.) as well as serve as a requirement for a building given as a reward for good management (For example, the [Space Port](/space-port)), or be that reward in and of itself.
