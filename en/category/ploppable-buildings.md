This category is to contain all *Maxis* ploppable buildings, to distinguish them from Custom content.

Ploppable Buildings are those directly placed by the player, named because the user can just 'plop' them down.

For the Custom equivalent to this category, see [:Category:Ploppable Buildings/Custom](/category/ploppable-buildings/custom).
