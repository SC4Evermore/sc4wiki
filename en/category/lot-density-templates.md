This category is designed to hold templates used to show the Zone Types and Densities that a Building grows on and which Sizes of Lot that Building is on will grow on which.

These templates are intended for use with [Template:Infobox grow2](/template-infobox-grow2), but will work wherever a zone type or density needs to be referred to by an image. Due to how they are implemented, they will appear in inline elements incorrectly and may break out of text areas. Use line breaks to force correct line spacing.
