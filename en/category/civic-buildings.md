This is a category to contain all [Civic Buildings](/civic-buildings) created by [Maxis](/maxis) for [SimCity 4](/simcity-4). Do not place any custom buildings into this category, put them in [:Category:Civic Buildings/Custom](/category/civic-buildings/custom) instead.

[Civic Buildings](/civic-buildings) are buildings in the game designed to provide a passive service to a lot or the city, unlike [Utility Buildings](/utility-buildings) which provide a consumable service to a lot or [:Category:Transport Buildings](/category/transport-buildings) which provide a consumable service to a Sim.

Civic buildings can provide one of seven functions, all of which serve to improve the [Mayor Rating](/mayor-rating), [Land Value](/land-value) and [Desirability](/desirability) of the areas they cover. The [Police Department](/crime), [Fire Department](/flammability), [Education Services](/education), [Health Services](/health), [Landmarks](/landmark), [Business Deals](/business-deals) or [Parks](/parks), all of which provide passive bonuses to their coverage area depending on their usage.

The Custom equivalent of this category is [Civic Buildings/Custom](/civic-buildings/custom)

Do not place individual buildings in this category, place them in one of the subcategories below.
