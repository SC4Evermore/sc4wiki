**SimCity Polska** is a Polish [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) Fansite. It was founded in November [2007](/2007), and has since become well known in the SimCity 4 custom content world. It has many [W2W](/w2w) buildings in the modern style, which are quite hard to find anywhere outside the [STEX](/stex).

On May 18, [2018](/2018), SimCity Polska administrator [kbieniu7](/people/kbieniu7) announced that the site would be closing by the end of the year, due to rising hosting costs and diminished site activity, with the forums closing in the coming weeks, and the exchange remaining active until the final closure. [Simtropolis](/simtropolis) has since stepped in to serve as a new home for the SimCity Polska downloads, which, much like the files from [SimPeg](/simpeg), exist in their own STEX category.
