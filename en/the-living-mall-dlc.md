**The Living Mall** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 002** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Core Pacific City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Core_Pacific_City) in [Taipei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei), [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan) as it appeared in 2003.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Core Pacific City Living Mall Has A Life Of Its Own". Due to a commenting error in the original LUA file, it may or may not appear in gameplay.

## Trivia

The way **The Living Mall** was made by Maxis is different to most of the other landmarks in that its daytime renders take advantage of embedded Alphas, something common to [BAT](/bat) models, however it doesn't utilise dedicated nighttime renders, instead using the Shade then Overlay method used by all Maxis buildings. It shares this trait with [63 Building](/63-building-dlc), [Sungrye-Mun](/sungrye-mun-dlc) and [Kunjungjon](/kunjungjon-dlc).

The Living Mall also features in-render semi-transparency, allowing objects to appear behind the model. It is utilised on the high awning seen in the above image. This is the only Maxis object known to have this.
