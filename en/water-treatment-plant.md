The **Water Treatment Plant** is a utility building that removes pollution from the water table in a fixed radius.

Water Treatment Plants are designed to be connected to zones and buildings with [water pipes](/transit-network#water-pipes), but do not have to be as they provide no water to your city at all, they only serve to clean pollution from the water table.
