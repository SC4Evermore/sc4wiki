The **Cruise Ship Building** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Come Aboard! Cruise Ship Port Christened".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03BC0000 Cruise Ship Building](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03bc0000-cruise-ship-building), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Commercial Population** in excess of **15,000**
- **At least 25 other [Park Department Buildings](/category/park-buildings) built**
- **At least 35% or more of the city map is water**
