**Cities: Skylines** is a city-simulation game developed by Colossal Order, and published by Paradox Interactive, which received its release on 10 March 2015. Like [SimCity (2013)](/simcity-2013) and the [Cities XL](/cities-xl) franchise before it, Cities: Skylines uses full three-dimensional graphics.
