**Reed Place** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building that grows on only High Density Residential zones. [^1]

It is designed to primarily compete with [Copur Apartments](/copur-apartments), [Wallace Manor](/wallace-manor) and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Tri-Tip Towers](/tri-tip-towers).
