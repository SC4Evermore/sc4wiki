The **Radio Station** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that greatly increases your city's Middle-Wealth Residential and Manufacturing Industrial capacities.

It is a requirement in for unlocking the [Television Studio](/television-studio), the Middle-Wealth trophy building.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "#city# Tuned Into New KSIM Radio Station".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03330000_Radio_Station](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03330000-radio-station), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **18,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **30 Points**
