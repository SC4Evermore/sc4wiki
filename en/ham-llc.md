**Ham LLC** is a Medium-Wealth Commercial Offices Building that grows on all densities of Commercial zones[^1].

This building is part of the group of generic Medium-Wealth stores grouped under the Retail Stores name.

## Reference Buildings

Ham LLC is quite clearly a recreation of [Chicago's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago) [Marquette Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marquette_Building_%28Chicago%29), modified to be a standalone building. This makes it one of the few [Chicago Tileset](/category/chicago-tileset) buildings that is actually from Chicago.
