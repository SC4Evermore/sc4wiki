**California Plaza** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect.

It is a recreation of California Plaza, Maxis' former Corporate Headquarters at [2121 North California Boulevard](https://maps.google.com/?ll=37.908507,-122.065857&spn=0.002526,0.004128&t=h&z=19&layer=c&cbll=37.908394,-122.065927&panoid=8xNtCJAgehwzPs9W0E59OQ&cbp=12,288.1,,0,-7.95) in [Walnut Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut_Creek), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States).
