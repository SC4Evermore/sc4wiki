A **growable** [lot](/lot) is one that "grows" on [RCI Zones](/rci-zones). Depending on the zone and the type of building, a growable building may require road access, [power](/power), [water](/water), or other amenities in order to grow.

Unlike a [ploppable](/ploppable) lot, which requires the mayor to specifically place the lot in the city, a growable will grow on its own when the necessary conditions for its growth are met.
