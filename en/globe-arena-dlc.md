The **Globe Arena** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 006** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the Globe Arena, known as the [Ericsson Globe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ericsson_Globe) since 2008, in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden), completed in 1989, as it appeared in 2003.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Globe Arena Rolls Into \#city#".
