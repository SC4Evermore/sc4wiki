The **City Museum** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides [Education](/education) to your [Sims](/sims).

Education buildings increase the Education Quotient over a coverage radius. Some buildings have variable coverage radii which can be changed using a School Bus Funding slider, however the majority have a fixed radius that cannot be expanded or contracted without modding the game.

Museums are designed to provide a School-like effect on an older Sim's life, however they are still treated as Schools by the game's code.

In Museum rankings, it is second only to the larger [Major Art Museum](/major-art-museum).

## Trivia

The original name for this building was probably **Science, History and Art Museum** before being changed to **City Museum** as its exemplar still contains this name. This may indicate that other types of museum were planned.
