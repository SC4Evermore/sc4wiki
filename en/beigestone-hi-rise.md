The **Beigestone Hi-Rise** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building that grows on only High Density Residential zones, added in the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack. [^1]

It is designed to primarily compete with [Jolly Manor](/jolly-manor), [Simon Manor](/simon-manor), [The Pratt](/the-pratt), [Grossman Apartments](/grossman-apartments) and [Graham Apartments](/graham-apartments).
