The **Coal Power Plant** is a utility building that produces Power for your [Sims](/sims).

Power Plants are designed to be connected to zones and buildings with [Power Lines](/transit-network#powerlines), but do not have to be as they provide power to other buildings within a three-tile radius and those buildings may relay that power to other buildings in another three-tile radius and so on.

The Coal Power Plant is the cheapest power solution in SimCity 4, edging out the [Natural Gas Power Plant](/natural-gas-power-plant) by having a lower monthly cost and producing more power. Of all the power plants in SimCity 4, only the [Waste to Energy Plant](/waste-to-energy-plant) is capable of producing more pollution.

It is positioned between the [Waste to Energy Plant](/waste-to-energy-plant), [Solar Power Plant](/solar-power-plant) and [Oil Power Plant](/oil-power-plant) in power production, however it produces more pollution than the Oil Power Plant, much more pollution than the Solar Power Plant and slightly less than the Waste to Energy plant.
