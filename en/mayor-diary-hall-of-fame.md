The **SC4Devotion Mayors' Diary Hall of Fame**, sometimes abbreviated **SC4D MD HoF**, features those [Mayor Diaries](/mayor-diaries) that have been chosen by the [SimCity 4 Devotion](/simcity-4-devotion) community to be of such quality that they have been inducted into this institution. Being entered into the Mayor Diary HOF is subject to a few rules, that can be found [here](/mayor-diary-hall-of-fame/rules). Currently, seven [Mayor Diaries](/mayor-diaries) form part of this group, namely [Ennedi Plays Shosaloza](/ennedi-plays-shosaloza), [AntigOne](/antigone), [Whitefall, Iridar](/whitefall-iridar), [Blackwater](/blackwater), [Mind Scape](/mind-scape), [Tales at Teatime](/tales-at-teatime) and the [Commonwealth of Marathon](/commonwealth-of-marathon).
