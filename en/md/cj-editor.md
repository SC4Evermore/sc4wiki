The **MD/CJ Editor** is a tool, created by [HandsOn](/people/handson), that allows [City Showcase](/city-showcase) authors to create an [off-line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/off-line) version of their posts. It supports [Simtropolis v 6.0](/simtropolis), [SC4 Devotion](/sc4-devotion), [SimPeg](/simpeg) and [CapitalSimCity](/capitalsimcity).
