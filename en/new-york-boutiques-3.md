**New York Boutiques 3** (Named in the game as just **[Boutiques](/boutiques)**) is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on Medium Density and High Density Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic High-Wealth stores grouped under the Boutiques name.

[Curtin Fabrics](/curtin-fabrics) uses the same model sets and can be considered a Commercial Offices variant of this building.

## Trivia

From the Exemplar name, it's likely that this building was only intended to grow in the New York tileset.
