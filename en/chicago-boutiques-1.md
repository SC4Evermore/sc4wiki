**Chicago Boutiques 1** (Named in the game as just **[Boutiques](/boutiques)**) is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on all Densities of Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic High-Wealth stores grouped under the Boutiques name.

## Trivia

As exemplar the name for this building is ChiLuxuryStore, it's highly likely that this building was only ever meant to be available in the Chicago tileset.[^1]

This building uses the same models as the Commercial Office building [Todd &amp; Co.](/todd-and-amp-co) and can be considered to be a Commercial Services counterpart.
