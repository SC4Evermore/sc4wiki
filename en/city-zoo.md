The **City Zoo** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Beautification and Recreation for your [Sims](/sims).

Parks provide a [Park Effect](/park-effect) over a fixed radius, in addition to usually cleaning up air and water pollution and increasing [CAPs](/cap). The **City Zoo** also provides an additional Landmark Effect over a small radius.

The Zoo is the second most expensive Park in SimCity 4 Rush Hour/Deluxe, behind the [Major League Stadium](/major-league-stadium) and above the [Minor League Stadium](/minor-league-stadium) in Costs.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Every Sight (And Smell) In Animal Kingdom Seen At New \#City name# Zoo".

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **80,000 Sims**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **68 Points**
- **At least 25 other [Park Department Buildings](/category/park-buildings) built**
