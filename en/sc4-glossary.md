## Acronyms with SC4 Encyclopaedia Articles

## Glossary

### Other

- [3RR](/3rr): Three Rivers Region.
- [6U](/6u): 6underground

### A

- [AFDES](/afdes): Alliance of Fuel Developing and Exporting States
- [ANT](/ant): Additional Network Tool.
- [ARD](/network-widening-mod): Asymmetrical Road
- [ATL](/plugin/atl): Avenue Turning Lane Mod.
- [ATR](/former-teams#around-the-roses): Around the Roses (Former BAT Team).
- [Automata](/automata): Any type of vehicle or pedestrian/animal that moves by itself in SimCity 4. Usually grouped into four categories--aircraft, pedestrians/animals, land vehicles, and watercraft.
- [AVE](/ave): Avenue.

### B

- [Bobbo662](/people/bobbo662): Bobbo662 (BATter).
- [BAT](/bat): Building Architect Tool, program used to create 3D structures for SimCity 4; any structure created with Building Architect Tool program.
- [BBD](/plugin/bbd): Boston Big Dig.
- [BLaM](/blam): BATters, Lotters, and Modders Team, defunct.
- [BLS](/bsc/houses#bls): Barby Lot Studio
- [BMD](/bsc/houses#brt): [BarbyW](/people/barbyw), [MickeBear](/people/mickebear), [D66](/people/d66)
- [Brown Boxes](/brown-box): Structure shown in-game when all dependencies for a BAT are not present in Plugins folder.
- [BRT](/bsc/houses#brt): [BarbyW](/people/barbyw) [Ripptide](/people/ripptide)
- [BSC](/bsc): BAT Squad Certification.
- [BSP](/former-teams#barry-sanders-project): Barry Sanders Project (Former BAT Team).
- [BTE](/bte): [BSC](/bsc) Tracking Enabled.
- [BTM](/btm): [Bullet Train Mod](/bullet-train-mod).

### C

- [Callagrafx](/people/callagrafx) [BSC](/bsc) BATer and [LEX](/lex) moderator.
- [CAM](/cam): Colossus Add-on Mod, a mod affecting the [RCI](/rci) simulation and adding new growth stages to the game. ([Plugin:CAM](/plugin/cam))
- [Cerulean](/people/cerulean): Cerulean Architecture and Design.
- [CasperVg](/people/caspervg): CasperVg's Lots
- [CDK](/cdk): Coastal Development Kit
- [CML](/cml): Custom Member Label.
- [Citynut](/people/citynut) BSC BATer
- [COFR](/cofr): [Confederacy of Free Regions](/confederacy-of-free-regions), A SC4 Union
- [c.p](/people/c-p): Cyclepuppy a propmaker and terrain modder. (also known as Cycledogg or c.p.)
- [CSG](/csg): [CSGdesign](/people/csgdesign) and his site, [CSGforums](/csgforums).
- [CSK](/csk): Channel Stream Kit
- [Colyn](/people/colyn): Colyn Serfontein Expanded.
- [CTD](/ctd): Crash to Desktop.
- [Custom Content](/custom-content): Anything added to SimCity 4 that modifies the game (excludes official Maxis releases).

### D

- [D/L](/d/l): Download.
- [D66](/people/d66): Dolphin66.
- [DragonAnime](/people/dragonanime): DragonAnime, NDEX batter
- [Deadwoods](/people/deadwoods): Deadwoods.
- [Dependency](/dependency): Any object that a lot needs to work correctly.
- [DIP](/dip): Distilled Intersection Path.
- [DuskTrooper](/people/dusktrooper): DuskTrooper.
- [Deathtopumpkins](/people/deathtopumpkins): Deathtopumpkins.

### E

- [ELR](/transit-network#elevated-rail): Elevated Light Rail; also El-Rail.
- [Exemplar](/exemplar): A file that defines the properties of the game engine or a specific in-game element.

### F

- [FA](/fa): Fractional Angle, a type of [NAM](/nam) piece [David (dedgren)](/people/dedgren). Consists (currently) of the [FAR](/far) for Roads, [FAA](/faa) for Avenues, [FARR](/farr) for Railroads, and FARHW for RealHighways.
- [FAA](/faa): Fractional Angle Avenue.
- [FAR](/far): Fractional Angle Road. Part of the [FA](/fa).
- [FARR](/farr): Fractional Angle Railroad
- [FARHW](/farhw): Fractional Angle RealHighway.

### G

- [GrampaAl](/people/grampaal): GrampaAl, a founder of [BSC](/bsc)
- [Gary_Reggae](/people/gary-reggae): Gary Raggae (BATter).
- [Gascooker](/people/gascooker): Gascooker ([BSC](/bsc) Batter)
- [GHSR](/hsr): Ground High Speed Rail
- [GLR](/glr): Ground Light Rail.
- [GMax](/gmax): A free version of 3DS Max, a program used for modelling and creating 3D objects for SimCity 4.
- [GRV](/grv): Grand River Valley
- [GRV II](/grv-ii): Grand River Valley II
- [Growable](/growable): A lot that grows when zoned in-game.
- [GTR](/gtr): The [Greater Terran Region](/greater-terran-region), a CJ/MD by [Haljackey](/people/haljackey)
- GUI: Graphical User Interface

### H

- [HD](/hd): "High Definition", a term used to refer to textures or props that have a higher than standard resolution.
- [HDR](/hdr): Half Diagonal Road. Part of the [Fractional Angle Project](/fa).
- [HKABT](/hkabt): Hong Kong / Asia BAT Team.
- [HSRP](/hsrp):High Speed Rail Project (Also known as HSR)

### I

- [IDK](/idk): Item Description Key.
- [IID](/iid): Instance ID.
- [ITC](/itc): Team founded by [Yoshi (Ian)](/people/yoshi), [Travis](/people/travis) and [CasperVg](/people/caspervg). Later stood for International Team of Creators (former team).
- [ILive Reader](/reader): Tool created by for editing SimCity 4 files.
- [ISAT](/former-teams#insuns-architecture-team): InSims Architecture Team (former BAT team).
- [IllTonkso](/people/illtonkso): Ill Tonkso.

### J

- [Xannepan](/people/xannepan): Xannepan.
- [jeronij](/people/jeronij): Jeronij.

### L

- [LBT](/lbt): Latin American BAT Team.
- [LE Prop](/le-prop): A tool used by ILive, used for changing key properties of custom lots.
- [LEX](/lex): Lot Exchange; custom content exchange at [SC4Devotion](/sc4devotion).
- [LHD](/lhd): Left-hand drive.
- [LM](/lm): Landmark.
- [Lot Editor](/lot-editor): Official Maxis release that allows for th customization of lots in SimCity 4.
- [Lot](/lot): The entire structure of something in SimCity 4, including the building, props, and ground tiles.
- [LTEXT](/ltext): LText file (for in-game text).

### M

- [MAVE](/mave): Medianless Avenue. See Road, Network Widening Mod.
- [MD](/md): Mayor Diary. (another name for City Journal)
- [MIS](/mis): Modular Interchange System.
- [Couchpotato](/people/couchpotato): Couchpotato (prop BATter).
- [MML](/mml): Menu Management Lot.
- [Mod](/mod): A file that somehow changes that game, including textures, ordinances, and tools.
- [Modder](/mod): One who creates a Mod.
- [Modding](/mod): The act of creating a Mod.

### N

- [NAM](/nam): Network Add-On Mod. ([Plugin:NAM](/plugin/nam), [NAM/members](/nam/members))
- [North_Country_Dude](/people/north-country-dude) North Country Dude
- [NDEX](/ndex): N74704, DuskTrooper, Equinox (BAT Team). ([Plugin:NDEX Essesntials](/plugin/ndex-essesntials))
- [NHP](/nhp): New Horizon Productions.
- [NJZ](/zot#no-job-zot): No Job Zot.
- [NUTS](/nuts): Nobody Under Thirty Squad. A team of "middle-aged" batters.
- [NWM](/nwm): Network Widening Mod. ([Plugin:NWMWALL](/plugin/nwmwall))

### O

- Off-Topic: Any post, conversation, etc., in the forums that does not pertain to the subject matter where it has been posted. Generally frowned upon.
- [OG](/og): Occupant Group.an
- [Override Network](/override-network): A draggable transit item, which uses a [starter piece](/starter-piece) and [RUL](/rul) overrides to act upon a default game network, causing it to take on new properties and/or behaviors.
- [OWR](/owr): One Way Road.
- <!-- unconverted template: plugin -->: Old Wooden Waterfront

### P

- [PAM](/pam): Persistent Automata Mod, an automata controller file package developed by [Jeiman2008](/people/jeiman2008)
- [Pathfinding](/pathfinding): The process by which automata find the shortest route to their destination.
- [Pathing](/pathing): The creation of paths for automata on transit-enabled lots.
- [PDA](/former-teams#pool-diamond-and-amp-associates): Poole, Diamond & Associates (Former BAT Team).
- [Pegasus](/people/pegasus): Pegasus.
- [PIM-X](/pim-x): Extended Plugin Manager (part of X-Tool).
- [PIM](/pim): Plugin Manager.
- [PLEX](/plex): Pegasus Lot Exchange.
- [Ploppable](/ploppable): Any lot that can be placed directly in the game without having to zone for it.
- PM: Private Message.
- [Prop Pack](/prop#prop-pack): A file containing models and prop descriptors that can be used to make lots.
- [Prop](/prop): Anything used to aesthetically improve a lot, excluding the building itself and ground tiles.
- [PP](/pp): Prop Pox, a bug that causes props to go missing.

### R

- [RABET](/rabet): Roundabout Addon Tile, proposed NAM item.
- [RAM](/ram): Rail Add-On Mod. ([Plugin:RAM](/plugin/ram))
- [RCI Ploppable](/rci-ploppable): Ploppable building that provides jobs.
- [RCI](/rci): Residential, Commercial, Industrial.
- [RD](/rd): Road
- [RH](/rh): Rush Hour.
- [RHW](/rhw): RealHighway Mod (formerly Rural Highway Mod). ([Plugin:RHW](/plugin/rhw))
- [RKT](/rkt): Resource Key Type.
- [RLS](/rls): Real Life Syndrome; or BATs created by [SimGoober](/people/simgoober).
- [RNP](/former-teams#rabbit903-nealos101-productions): Rabbit903 Nealos101 Productions, former bat team kept alive by tag_one.
- [Tag One](/people/tag-one): Tag One Productions.
- [RPL](/rpl): River Park Kit
- [RTL](/rtl): Road Turning Lane Mod. ([Plugin:RTL](/plugin/rtl))
- [RTMT](/rtmt): Road Top Mass Transit. ([Plugin:RTMT](/plugin/rtmt))
- [RUL](/rul): A file that sets various rules and algorithms on the placement of transit networks.

### S

- [SAM](/sam): Street Add-On Mod. ([Plugin:SAM](/plugin/sam))
- SC: SimCity, SimCities (defunct home of the BLaM found \[<http://simcities.net/>\| Here\]).
- [SC3X](/sc3x): SimCities Exchange.
- [SC4](/sc4): SimCity 4.
- [SC4D](/sc4d): SimCity 4 Devotion, a SC4 community found \[<http://sc4devotion.com/>\| Here\].
- [SC4RH](/sc4rh): SimCity 4 Rush Hour.
- [SC4TF](/sc4tf): SC4Terraformer.
- [SC4Tool](/sc4tool): A tool for purposes related to custom content in SimCity 4.
- [SCAG](/scag): SimCity Airport Group
- [SCC](/scc): SimCity Central
- [SCK](/sck): SimCityKurier.
- [SCP](/scp): SimCity Plaza.
- [SCRT](/former-teams): SimCity Remodeling Team
- [SCS](/scs): SimCity Societies. SC4's sequel, generally frowned up on by the SC4 community for its simplicity among other reasons.
- SDM: Super demand mod.
- [SF](/sf): Slope-friendly.
- [SFBT](/sfbt): SimForum BAT Team.
- [SimGoober](/people/simgoober): SimGoober.
- [SIP](/sip): Signalized Intersection Piece
- [SLR](/slr): Single Lane Road. (now [ARD](/sc4-glossary#a))
- [SMP](/smp): Simtropia Productions Team.
- [SNM](/snm): SimNation Military.
- [SPAM](/spam): SimPeg Agricultural Mod.
- [SPM](/spm): Signage Piece Mod.
- [SimRabbit123](/people/simrabbit123): [NDEX](/ndex) batter.
- [ST](/st): Simtropolis, arguably the biggest SimCity fansite, found \[<http://www.simtropolis.com/>\| here\].
- [STEX](/stex): Simtropolis Exchange.
- [STR](/str): Single Track Rail, part of the [RAM](/ram). ([Plugin:STR](/plugin/str))
- [SUM](/sum): Start-Up Manager.
- [SZ](/sz): SimsZone.

### T

- [TE](/te): Transit-enabled. Usually refers to Lot-based items.
- [Terraform](/terraform): The process of modifying the terrain in SimCity 4.
- [TG](/tg): Team Grater, defunct.
- [TLA](/tla): Turning Lane Avenues, three letter acronym. ([Plugin:TLA](/plugin/tla)
- TM: Typing monkeys, referring those who ask questions that they can find the answer in the readme.
- [TPP](/the-planning-pages): The Planning Pages. A SC4 community found [Here](http://www.theplanningpages.com/)
- [TPW](/plugin/transparent-ploppable-water): Transparent Ploppable Water by JRJ.
- [TSC](/tsc): ToutSimCity, a French community. Also the name of the resident BAT/Lot team at that site.
- [TheTeaCat](/people/theteacat): TheTeaCat. Likes tea (but not Earl Grey) and has an MD called Tales at TeaTime.
- [TuLEP](/tulep): Turn Lane Extension Piece, a NAM item.

### U - Z

- [UDI](/udi): U-Drive-It.
- [UI](/ui): User Interface
- [UVNK](/uvnk): User Visible Name Key.
- UDPX: Now [SCRT](/former-teams), defunct
