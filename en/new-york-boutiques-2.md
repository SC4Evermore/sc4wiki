**New York Boutiques 2** (Part of the **[Boutiques](/boutiques)** family of Growable buildings) is a High Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on all densities of Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic High-Wealth stores grouped under the Botiques name.

[Chalmers Co.](/chalmers-co) uses the same model sets and can be considered a Commercial Offices variant of this building.

## Reference Buildings

This building is likely based on the Main Entrance of the Graybar Building in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York), with fictional sides and rear.

## Trivia

From this building's Exemplar Name, it can be assumed that this building was originally only meant to grow when the Chicago tileset was active.
