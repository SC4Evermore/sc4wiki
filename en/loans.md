[SimCity 4](/simcity-4) allows taking out **Loans**, in the Budget Control menu. They have an [interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/interest) rate of 8.5% with payback on a monthly basis for 10 game years, with the minimum and maximum §5.000 and §200.000, respectively.
