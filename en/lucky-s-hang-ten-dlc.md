**Lucky's Hang Ten** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 036** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

Unlike most of the other DLC Landmarks, this building is completely fictional.

## Trivia

**Lucky's Hang Ten** shares almost all of its building properties with the [Rockefeller Center](/rockefeller-center), [Shorty Burger](/shorty-burger) and [Sobchak Lanes](/sobchak-lanes), the first of which was probably a mistake. It's also one of only a few DLC buildings that have no files for a language other than US English, a trait it shares with [Shorty Burger](/shorty-burger) and [Sobchak Lanes](/sobchak-lanes).
