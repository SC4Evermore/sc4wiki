**Plowed Dirt (with Sprinkler)** is an Agricultural Industry Field building that grows on Low Density (Agricultural) Industrial Zones[^1].

Field buildings are grown when construction on an [Agricultural Anchor](/category/i-a-growables/anchors) begins and greedily fill all connected and unoccupied Agricultural zones.

This particular lot has a randomly spawning Sprinkler prop that will disappear at certain times in the game year.
