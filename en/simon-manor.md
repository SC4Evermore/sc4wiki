**Simon Manor** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building that grows on only High Density Residential zones. [^1]

It is designed to primarily compete with [Jolly Manor](/jolly-manor), [The Pratt](/the-pratt), [Grossman Apartments](/grossman-apartments), [Graham Apartments](/graham-apartments) and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Beigestone Hi-Rise](/beigestone-hi-rise).

## Reference Buildings

Simon Manor was clearly based on 895 Park Avenue in [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan), [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City). The only real difference in the Recreation is that the two towers' heights differ.
