There are three **utilities** in [SimCity 4](/simcity-4): water, power, and [garbage](/garbage) (waste disposal). All three of these can be found in the Utilities Menu in [Mayor Mode](/mayor-mode). [Power](/power) is required for nearly every building in the game, and zones will not develop unless they are supplied with power. [Water](/water) is required for higher-stage and higher-[wealth](/wealth) development to occur, however low density development can occur without water. Waste disposal is not required for growth to occur, however without facilities or [Neighbor Deals](/neighbor-deals) in place to dispose of garbage, it will accumulate in your city, reducing [desirability](/desirability). All three utilities can be monitored in [Data View](/data-view).
