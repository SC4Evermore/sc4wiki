The **Bungalow** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building with three statistically Identical[^1][^2][^3] Variants that grow on All Residential zones.

All Variants are designed to compete with [2 Story House](/2-story-house), [Brick House](/brick-house), [Sheeza Brick House](/sheeza-brick-house) and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Dubois Garden Cottage](/dubois-garden-cottage), [Avery Abode](/avery-abode) and [Handy House](/handy-house).

## Variant Differences

Variants 2 and 3 are mirrored versions of Variant 1 and all variants had different textures when rendered. All three variants have exactly the same properties and grow on the same lots.
