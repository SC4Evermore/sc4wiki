The **Elevated Rail Station** is a Transport building that provides [Elevated Rail](/elevated-rail) services added in the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack.

It is designed to be placed on top of a stretch of Elevated Rail, allowing your sims to board and alight Elevated Rail trains.

Elevated Rail is designed as an extension of the Subway network in SimCity 4, allowing you to provide subway service to suburbs very cheaply.
