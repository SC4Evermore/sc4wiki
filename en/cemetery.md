The **Cemetery** are multiple [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Buildings that serve as icons of good Residential growth.

All three variants have identical statistics, however their lots and unlock requirements vary.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Dead Hush Falls Over Crowd At New Cemetery Opening".

## Unlock Requirements

### Variant 1

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03470000 Cemetery 1](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03470000-cemetery-1), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

#### Gameplay

##### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **2,500**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **42 Points**
- A **[House of Worship](/house-of-worship) has been constructed in the city**

#### U Drive It Missions

- [Toxic Waste Truck](/toxic-waste-dump) Mission: Spread the Mutation

### Variant 2

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#037B0000 Cemetery 2](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#037b0000-cemetery-2), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

#### Gameplay

##### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **15,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **46 Points**
- **Variant 1 has been constructed in the city**

### Variant 3

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#037C0000 Cemetery 3](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#037c0000-cemetery-3), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

#### Gameplay

##### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **60,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **50 Points**
- **Variant 2 has been constructed in the city**
