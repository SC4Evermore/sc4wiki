The **Sheeza Brick House** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building with three statistically Identical[^1] Variants that grow on All Residential zones. The other variants are both called [Brick House](/brick-house).

It is designed to compete with [2 Story House](/2-story-house), [Bungalow](/bungalow), [Brick House](/brick-house) and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Dubois Garden Cottage](/dubois-garden-cottage), [Avery Abode](/avery-abode) and [Handy House](/handy-house).
