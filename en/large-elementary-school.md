The **Large Elementary School** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) [Reward](/rewards) Building that provides [Education](/education) to your Young [Sims](/sims) that was added in the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack.

Education buildings increase the Education Quotient over a coverage radius. Some buildings have variable coverage radii which can be changed using a School Bus Funding slider, however the majority have a fixed radius that cannot be expanded or contracted without modding the game.

In Elementary School rankings, only the [Elementary School](/elementary-school) is inferior.
