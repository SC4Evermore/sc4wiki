The **Warehouse** is a Dirty Industrial Outbuilding that grows on all densities of Industrial Zones[^1].

Outbuildings are grown by SimCity 4's Industrial Zones attached to an Anchor Lot, designed to fill out the entire zone. Outbuildings are grown last and are designed to fill the spaces between [Anchor Lots](/category/i-m-growables/anchors) and [Machinery](/category/i-m-growables/machinery) lots.

## [Colossus Addon Mod](/colossus-addon-mod)

When the Colossus Addon Mod is installed, the number of jobs is increased to 40 and its lots are changed to Stages 2 and 3.
