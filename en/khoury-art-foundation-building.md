The **Khoury Art Foundation Building** is a High-Wealth Commercial Offices Building that grows on High-Density Commercial zones[^1], added in [SimCity 4's](/simcity-4) [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack.

It is designed to compete with [Reamon Plaza](/reamon-plaza) from the Chicago Tileset, [Trottier Trading](/trottier-trading) from the Houston Tileset and [Cameron Cameras](/cameron-cameras) from the New York Tileset.

## Reference Buildings

The Khoury Art Foundation Building is clearly based on [Frankfurt's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt) [Westendstrasse 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westendstrasse_1), however the semi-cylindrical tower and elevator core are much smaller, the base of the building is significantly different and the building overall is shorter.
