**Houston Boutiques 1** (Named in the game as just **[Boutiques](/boutiques)**) is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on all Densities of Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic High-Wealth stores grouped under the Boutiques name.

## Trivia

As exemplar the name for this building is HouLuxuryStore, it's highly likely that this building was only ever meant to be available in the Houston tileset.[^1]
