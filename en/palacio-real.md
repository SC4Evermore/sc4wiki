**Palacio Real** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Royal Palace of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Palace_of_Madrid) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid), [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain), completed in 1755.
