**The Commonwealth of Eleutherian States**, usually referred to as **Eleutheria** or the **E.S.**, is a constitutional federal imperial republic comprising thirteen states and a Federal Capital Territory, as well as a number of islands in the Central Atlantic Ocean. The country is located in West Africa and shares land borders with the Republic of Benin in the west, Chad and Cameroon in the east, and Niger in the north. Its coast lies on the Gulf of Guinea, part of the Atlantic Ocean, in the south. The capital city is Eleutheria. It is an [MD](/md), authored by [M4346](/people/m4346), but it is currently inactive.
