The **Mayor's House** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that is very desireable to both residential and commercial sims.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Mayor Digs New Digs; Residence Open To City Eminences".

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **500**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **20 Points**
