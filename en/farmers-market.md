The **Farmer's Market** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that greatly increases your city's Middle-Wealth Residential capacities.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "It's Corny But Fresh: New Farmer's Market Opens".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#032C0000 Farmers Market](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#032c0000-farmers-market), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **3,000**
- **Agricultural Industry** provides in excess of **600 Jobs**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **34 Points**
