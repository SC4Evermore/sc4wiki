The **Major League Stadium** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Beautification and Recreation for your [Sims](/sims).

Parks provide a [Park Effect](/park-effect) over a fixed radius, in addition to usually cleaning up air and water pollution and increasing [CAPs](/cap). The Major League Stadium however carries a Landmark Effect, a Park Effect penalty and a CAP relief.

The Major League Stadium is the most expensive Park in SimCity 4 Rush Hour/Deluxe, above the [City Zoo](/city-zoo) in Costs.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Cleats Cleaned And Fans Ready: Major League Ball Playing At New Stadium".

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Population** in excess of **85,000 Sims**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **30 Points**
- **[Minor League Stadium](/minor-league-stadium) has been built** in that city.
