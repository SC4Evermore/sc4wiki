The **Lincoln Center** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Lincoln Center for the Performing Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Center_for_the_Performing_Arts) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), completed in 1969.
