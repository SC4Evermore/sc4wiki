**Tilesets** are a way of organizing buildings by architectural style. You can choose which tilesets you want to grow in your city through the [Building Style Control](/building-style-control).

The four tilesets are:

- [Chicago](/category/chicago-tileset) 1890
- [New York](/category/new-york-tileset) 1940
- [Houston](/category/houston-tileset) 1990
- [European](/category/euro-tileset) Contemporary
