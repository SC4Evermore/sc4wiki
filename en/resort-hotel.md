The **Resort Hotel** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that increases your city's Middle-Wealth and high Wealth Residential capacities.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Hot Nights and Lazy Days At New Resort Hotel".

## Unlock Requirements

> Main article: [Tutorial:How_to_get_Maxis_Rewards#03810000 Resort Hotel](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards#03810000-resort-hotel), [Tutorial:How to get Maxis Rewards](/tutorial/how-to-get-maxis-rewards)

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Regional R§§ and R§§§ Population:** in excess of **50,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **52 Points**
- At least **2 or more cities connected to by the city**
- At least **3 or more cities connected to indirectly through others**
- **Average Pollution** being less than **20 points**

## Bugs

Due to a bug in the exemplar, the building actually provides R§ jobs not R§§ jobs. This is likely since the accompanying Amenities provided is for R§§ jobs.
