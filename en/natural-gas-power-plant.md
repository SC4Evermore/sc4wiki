The **Natural Gas Power Plant** is a utility building that produces Power for your [Sims](/sims).

Power Plants are designed to be connected to zones and buildings with [Power Lines](/transit-network#powerlines), but do not have to be as they provide power to other buildings within a three-tile radius and those buildings may relay that power to other buildings in another three-tile radius and so on.

The Natural Gas Power Plant is the more expensive power solutions in SimCity 4, dearer than the [Wind Power Plant](/wind-power-plant) in the short term, but becoming cheaper in the long term.

It is positioned between the [Wind Power Plant](/wind-power-plant), [Waste to Energy Plant](/waste-to-energy-plant) and [Solar Power Plant](/solar-power-plant) in power production, producing less pollution than the Waste to Energy Plant but more than the Solar and Wind plants.

The Natural Gas Power Plant can be seen as a clean alternative to the [Coal Power Plant](/coal-power-plant) or [Oil Power Plant](/oil-power-plant) as it costs about the same, however it produces half of the power of the Coal Power Plant.
