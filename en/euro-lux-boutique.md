**Euro Lux Boutique** is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on all Densities of Commercial zones.

Euro Lux Boutique is the Euro Tileset equivalent of [Houston Boutiques 1](/houston-boutiques-1), [Chicago Boutiques 1](/chicago-boutiques-1) and [New York Boutiques 1](/new-york-boutiques-1) as evidenced by its exemplar name, 1EuroLuxuryStore1, following the conventions of the other three Boutiques lots.
