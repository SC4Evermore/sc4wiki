The **SC4 Region & City Viewer** is a [spin-off](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/spin-off) from the [SC4 Organizer](/sc4-organizer), created by [HandsOn](/people/handson), that allows you to view your [cities](/quad) and [regions](/region) outside the game. If the Region & City Viewer is used together with the [Region Census](/region-census), you can also view population data. Since the release of [SC4 Organizer v 2.0](/sc4-organizer), this tool has been superseeded by the version included in the latter.
