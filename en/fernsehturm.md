**Fernsehturm** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Fernsehturm Belin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernsehturm_Belin) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), completed in 1969.
