**Chicago Boutiques 2** (Named in the game as just **[Boutiques](/boutiques)**) is a High Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on all densities of Commercial zones.

## Trivia

From this building's Exemplar Name, it can be assumed that this building was originally only meant to grow when the Chicago tileset was active.

[McCord Printing](/mccord-printing) uses the same model sets and can be considered a Commercial Offices variant of this building.
