The **Sobchak Lanes** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 034** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

## Reference Buildings

The building's signage is largely based on that of [Los Angeles'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles) [Hollywood Star Lanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Star_Lanes), used as a Filming location in the 1998 film [The Big Lebowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Lebowski). The game references one of the film's characters, Walter Sobchak, in its name.

It was closed and demolished in 2002 to allow construction of the Kingsley Elementary School.

## Trivia

**Sobchak Lanes** shares almost all of its building properties with [Lucky's Hang Ten](/lucky-and#39-s-hang-ten), the [Rockefeller Center](/rockefeller-center) and [Shorty Burger](/shorty-burger), the second of which was probably a mistake. It's also one of only a few DLC buildings that have no files for any language other than US English, a trait it shares with [Lucky's Hang Ten](/lucky-and#39-s-hang-ten) and [Shorty Burger](/shorty-burger).
