Starting in March 2007, **SimCityPlaza** became the successor of the now defunct [SimsZone](/simszone), one of the largest German sites about the Sim games. All SimCity related content from SimsZone was transferred to SimCityPlaza, and the new [SimCityKurier](/simcitykurier) became the download area for all [SimCity 4](/simcity-4)-related downloads. Apart from reviews and general information about the game, SimCityPlaza also features a wide variety of tutorials for the game, as well as custom content creation tools like the [BAT](/bat). A special section about the [NAM](/nam) explains the mod in detail and shows many hints and suggestions how to use it in the game. Other sections include a comprehensive SimCity 4 FAQ, a comparison of the four major SimCity versions, funny screenshots, a brief overview about SimCity: Societies, and the old SimCity 3000-related content that was transferred from [SimsZone](/simszone). The site has since been merged into [SimForum](/simforum).
