The **Medium Landing Strip** is a Transport building that provides Air services, designed to provide Commercial CAP Relief.

Airports provide Commercial Office Cap Relief proportional to their Usage.

The Medium Landing Strip is, surprisingly, the midpoint of the Landing Strip-class Airport chain in the game, preceded by the [Small Landing Strip](/small-landing-strip) and succeeded by the [Large Landing Strip](/large-landing-strip).
