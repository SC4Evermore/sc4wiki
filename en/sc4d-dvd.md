The **SC4D DVD**, also known as the **LEX DVD**, is a [DVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DVD) that [SimCity 4 Devotion](/simcity-4-devotion) Members receive as a present, after they have donated € 25 (\$ 35) to the site. It first shipped on November 17th, [2008](/2008) and includes all kinds of [custom content](/category/custom-content), ranging from [Cycledogg](/people/cycledogg)'s [terrain mods](/terrain-mod) to [iLive](/people/ilive)'s tools. It also contains some exclusive content, for example the [Petronas Towers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petronas_Towers) and the <!-- unconverted template: plugin --> by [JBSimio](/people/jbsimio). It utilizes a custom-scripted application, created by [Daeley](/people/daeley) to install the files and view the readme's.

A successor to the LEX DVD, the LEX CD, is currently being worked on. It will contain some additional content that was released after burning the LEX DVD, and also a few patches.
