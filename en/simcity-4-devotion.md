## The Site

### General

**Sim City 4 Devotion**, most commonly called **SC4D**, is a member supported fansite dedicated solely to the [(EA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Arts) game [SimCity 4](/simcity-4). The site caters to the "serious player" and is the home of a number of some premier SC4 custom content development teams, such as the [BSC](/bsc), [Network Addon Mod](/network-addon-mod) Team, and the [AC](/ac) Team. The site also hosts [dedgren](/people/dedgren)'s [Three Rivers Region](/three-rivers-region) and [ChrisAdams3997](/people/chrisadams3997)'s [Rural Renewal Project](/rural-renewal-project), along with many popular [Mayor Diaries](/mayor-diaries) and a [Picture Competition](/picture-competition). SC4D is also the portal and co-host with [BSC](/bsc) of the [LEX](/lex), a custom content download site.

**SC4D** was founded on November 4 [2006](/2006), by [jeronij](/people/jeronij), a member of the SC4 community from [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain), who had prior to that time authored the [City Journals](/city-journal) [Sculpting Columbia River](/sculpting-columbia-river) and [Simtropia](/simtropia-cj) at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis). [jeronij](/people/jeronij) initially privately sponsored the webspace for the site and did all of its formatting and design. A core of many of the community's most distinguished members had joined by the end of 2005, and 3RR moved over from [Simtropolis](/simtropolis) and set up as one of the site's first [Mayor Diaries](/mayor-diaries) in December of that year. [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa)n member [Colyn](/people/colyn), also known in the community as [Vlakhaas](/people/vlakhaas), and [dedgren](/people/dedgren) became the site's assistant administrators early in [2007](/2007).

By it's first birthday in [2007](/2007), the site had grown to just under 5000 members. Its forum sections were very busy, with a fairly consistent daily average since that time of about 300 new posts. [jeronij](/people/jeronij) and the assistant admins created a staff from the most involved and knowledgeable members, and the site has received many accolades for being well organized and efficiently run. During 2007, the [NAM Team](/nam-team) and [AC Team](/ac-team) relocated from [Simtropolis](/simtropolis).

In June [2008](/2008), [Tarkus](/people/tarkus) and [RippleJet](/people/ripplejet), were appointed by [jeronij](/people/jeronij) to fill two newly created administrator positions. The growth and activity on the site during its second year increased substantially, with SC4D's membership growing to well over 15.000 members by its second birthday. Around the Summer of 2008, the site experienced its first real growing pains, as its servers became overwhelmed by all the activity and the demands of hosting the [LEX](/lex). This caused [jeronij](/people/jeronij) to move SC4D onto dedicated server space, at a substantial increase in monthly cost. Rather than require members to contribute to defray expenses, [jeronij](/people/jeronij) opened the site up to limited [advertising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/advertising), which a member has the option to eliminate by "subscribing" for a nominal amount. SC4D also worked with [BSC](/bsc) to produce the [SC4D DVD](/sc4d-dvd), which was released in November [2008](/2008), and is sent to any member who contributes €25 or more to the site.

SC4D entered it third year true to its original purpose. Over the years, its relations with other SC4 fansites, notably [Simtropolis](/simtropolis) and [SimPeg](/simpeg), have mellowed, and there is a notable absence of the controversies between sites that have marked the past. In late November 2008, [dedgren](/people/dedgren) and [Colyn](/people/colyn) left their admin positions and [jeronij](/people/jeronij) selected [BarbyW](/people/barbyw), and [thundercrack83](/people/thundercrack83) to replace them. [dedgren](/people/dedgren), who had asked to step down based on his desire to focus his time on [3RR](/3rr), remains on in the capacity of an adviser to [jeronij](/people/jeronij) and the staff as "SC4D Counsellor."

As of May 22nd, [2009](/2009), [Diggis](/people/diggis), [Jmouse](/people/jmouse), [rooker1](/people/rooker1) and [threestooges](/people/threestooges) have been appointed as new Global Moderators. Various other staff members have since been appointed as Global Moderator or Admin.

Since August [2019](/2019), the site has been owned and operated by [Tarkus](/people/tarkus).
