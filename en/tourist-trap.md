The **Tourist Trap** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that greatly increases your city's Low-Wealth Residential capacities.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "New Roadside Attraction Offers Attractive Junk For Bored Travellers".

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Residential Regional Population** in excess of **40,000**
- **4 or more Number of cities connected in the local city**
- **5 or more Number of cities indirectly in the Region**
