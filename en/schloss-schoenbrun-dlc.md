**Schloss Schoenbrun** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 00E** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Schönbrunn Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sch%C3%B6nbrunn_Palace) in [Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna), [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria), Originally constructed as a Mansion in 1548, roughly as it appeared in 2003.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Schloss Schoenbrun Give*(sic)* \#city# Rooms to Spare".
