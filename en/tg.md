Team Grater is a [BATing](/bat) team formed by [Krio](/people/krio) and [Pat Riot](/people/pat-riot), which has made several dozen releases.

## History

Team Grater was founded in late 2007 by [Krio](/people/krio) and [Pat Riot](/people/pat-riot). The first recruits for the newborn team were [JosefBrisko](/people/josefbrisko), [scotty222](/people/scotty222), a former team-mate of [Pat Riot](/people/pat-riot) from the [NEBB Team](/former-teams#nebb), and [Travis](/people/travis), who was brought in as a modder. [jayo](/people/jayo) was first recruited after the founding, but the starting [Lotter](/lot) was eventually removed from the team. [dogfight](/people/dogfight) joined the team shortly thereafter and during this time TG was given a team room at [SC4D](/sc4d). He has since disappeared from [SC4 Devotion](/sc4-devotion) and is sorely missed. [RebaLynnTS](/people/rebalynnts) and [Yoshi](/people/yoshi) joined to team during winter [2008](/2008) and have remained members since.

In the Spring of 2008, [CasperVg](/people/caspervg) was recruited by [Travis](/people/travis), but disagreements within the team led to a split. [CasperVg](/people/caspervg) followed [Travis](/people/travis) and [Yoshi](/people/yoshi) to form [ITC](/itc), and [Krio](/people/krio) then disbanded the team, after [Pat Riot](/people/pat-riot) wanted to take time off.

Team Grater was reunited in July [2008](/2008) when [Krio](/people/krio) and [Pat Riot](/people/pat-riot) settled their disagreements about the [BAT](/bat) Team. The revived team invited [bixel](/people/bixel) and former [BLaM](/blam)-member [delija21](/people/delija21), and [Yoshi](/people/yoshi) rejoined after he left [ITC](/itc). Former members such as [scotty222](/people/scotty222) and [JosefBrisko](/people/josefbrisko) rejoined as well.

Since reuniting, TG has been able to establish itself, which it had struggled to do in its first run. It now has 60 [STEX](/stex) uploads and has recently recruited a new BATter, [tomdapom](/people/tomdapom).The largest projects that TG has made are the [Modular Mall Series](/modular-mall-series) and [Lee Roy Selmon Project](/lee-roy-selmon-project). They are now working on a project called the Suburban Set.
