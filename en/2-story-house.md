The **2 Story House** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building with four statistically Identical Variants that grow on All Residential zones.

Variants 1, 2 and 3 are designed to compete with [Bungalow](/bungalow), [Brick House](/brick-house), [Sheeza Brick House](/sheeza-brick-house) and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Dubois Garden Cottage](/dubois-garden-cottage), [Avery Abode](/avery-abode) and [Handy House](/handy-house).

Variant 4 is designed to compete with [Shingle House](/shingle-house), [Cape Cod House (Small)](/cape-cod-house#cape-cod-house-small) and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Devonshire Cottage](/devonshire-cottage).

## Variant Differences (Small Version)

All variants have exactly the same properties, except Variant 3 which provides one less resident (Likely an error), and grow on the same lots.

Variants 1, 2 and 3 have the same base Model, however each used a distinct texture when rendered.

Variant 4 has a completely different model which is unrelated to the other variants.

It is designed to compete with [Brick 2 Story](/brick-2-story), [Cape Cod House (Large Variants)](/cape-cod-house#cape-cod-house-large) and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Mashire Quarters](/mashire-quarters), [Hendawi Place](/hendawi-place) and [Cheez Cottage](/cheez-cottage)

## Variant Differences (Large Version)

All variants have exactly the same properties and grow on the same lots.

All variants use different exterior finishes, however Variants 2 and 3 used a mirrored model of Variant 1.
