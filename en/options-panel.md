The **Options Panel** is one of the main parts of the [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) user interface in [City View](/city-view). It contains buttons for saving your city, exiting to [Region View](/region-view), and quitting the game. It also provides access to the in-game camera and photo album, as well as graphics, sound, and general settings.
