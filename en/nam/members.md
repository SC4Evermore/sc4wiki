The **NAM Team** is a group that produces the popular [Network Addon Mod](/network-addon-mod) (NAM), along with producing other interrelated plugins, such as the [RealHighway](/realhighway) (RHW), [High Speed Rail](/high-speed-rail) (HSR) and [Street Addon Mod](/street-addon-mod) (SAM). It also manages the central [RUL Files](/rul-file) for the benefit of the community.

It was originally started as **Modd Squad Transport** back in [2003](/2003) at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis), with its members releasing a number of transit bugfixes. The first official version of the NAM was released on April 24, [2004](/2004), and 26 NAM releases have been made since then. The team currently houses developmental functions at [SimCity 4 Devotion](/simcity-4-devotion), and has since [2007](/2007).
