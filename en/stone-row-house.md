The **Stone Row House** is a Medium-Wealth Residential Building that grows on only Medium and High Density Residential zones.[^1]

It is designed to compete with [2 Story Row House](/2-story-row-house), [Modern Apartments (Small Variant)](/modern-apartments#modern-apartments-small), and, if the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) Expansion Pack is installed, [Languish Lofts](/languish-lofts)

## Trivia

The 1x1 lot variants were originally intended to be Stage 6 and the 1x2 Variants were originally intended to be Stage 5.
