**Farly Audio** is a High-Wealth Commercial Office Building that grows on Medium and High Density Commercial zones. [^1]

## Reference Buildings

Farly Audio appears to be largely based on the Union Trust Company Building in [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York), completed in 1890. Most of the architectural flairs like the Chimneys and most of the arched windows were stripped from the recreation. The building was also shallowed and the central light shaft is missing as a result.

It was demolished in 1929.
