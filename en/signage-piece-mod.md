The **Signage Piece Mod** (**SPM**) was a mod by the [NAM Team](/nam), which is unreleased and abandoned. It consists of a series of 1x1 [Puzzle Pieces](/puzzle-piece) for the various networks that have signs in [Prop Families](/prop) attached via [Type21](/type21) Exemplar. It is designed to solve the issue that the Lot-based solutions for signal implementation resulted in, such as extra space consumption and [CTDs](/ctd) from [TE Lot](/transit-enabled)-based sets, while allowing the user to control signage placement, unlike a standard T21-based mod.
