**Unreleased DLC** is a term used to identify **Plugin Packs 001, 00B, 011, 013 - 015** and **018 - 02F**.

The names for these plugin packs are determined by gaps in the names of released DLC landmarks.

The fates and subjects of these unreleased landmarks are unknown and no research has been done into them.

It is possible that they were dropped when in conceptualisation or production due to reproduction difficulties, they were denied release because of legal concerns (Copyright or Trademark for example) or that they were not started or completed by the time development for SimCity 4 Rush Hour was dropped by EA.
