**Small House** is the name given to multiple Statistically Identical Low-Wealth Residential Buildings that grow on All Densities of Residential zone. [^1][^2][^3]

## Variant Differences

The roof of each variant is subtly different, but most of Variant 2's and 3's models are a mirrored version of Variant 1.
