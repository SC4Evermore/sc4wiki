**ToutSimCities** (often abbreviated as **TSC**) is a large French-language SimCity fansite. The site is divided into the Forums and the Exchange (Télécharger), and is possibly the largest online French-language SimCity community. The site is administered by [laurent](/people/laurent), who started the site in December of 2002. The site is home to almost 10 000 members, and is very active.

## Management

ToutSimcities is managed an administrator and two global moderators:

- [dvchronique](/people/dvchronique)
- [GER](/people/ger)
- [laurent](/people/laurent) (administrator)
