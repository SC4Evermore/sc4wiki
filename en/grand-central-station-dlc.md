**Grand Central Station** is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 010** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost. This building has no mass transit features — for the train station reward with a similar name, see [Grand Railroad Station](/grand-railroad-station).

It is a recreation of [Grand Central Terminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Central_Terminal) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York), [United States of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America) built in 1903 and roughly represented as it appeared before the [Park Avenue Viaduct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Avenue_Viaduct) began construction in 1918.

## Trivia

**Grand Central Station** is probably the most unusual of the game's landmarks based on a real place with Maxis depicting the building in close to a state it had not been in for nearly 90 years by the time the game was published.
