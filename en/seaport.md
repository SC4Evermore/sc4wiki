The **Seaport** is a Transport building that provides Freight Shipping services, designed to remove Industrial Freight traffic from your city.

Ports are one of the three possible destinations for Freight Trucks:

- Seaport for export.
- A [Neighbor Connection](/neighbor-connections) for export, which might be road, avenue, highway, [RealHighway](/realhighway) or [rail](/rail) [Neighbor Connections](/neighbor-connections).
- [Freight Train Station](/freight-train-station) for transshipment from road to [rail](/rail).

## Beta

In beta, it appears that the Seaport was to be the final stage in an upgradeable chain of buildings, begun by the [Port](/port) and preceded by the [Major Port](/major-port).

## Bugs

Its actual capacity, 3000, is below its catalogue capacity of 4000.
