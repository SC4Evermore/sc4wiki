**Andrews Fashion Center** is a High-Wealth Commercial Services LOT that has variants that grow on all density Commercial zones. According to the building's Occupant Groups, Andrews Fashion Center is a Commercial Shop (more accurately being a mall).

## Re-Lots

\[<http://www.simtropolis.com/forum/files/file/2781-additional-5x5-and-6x6-mall-lots-v10/>\| There is an upload on the STEX\] by Toroca that allows this building and the [Lee Shopping Mall](/lee-shopping-mall) to grow on 5x5 size LOTs and the other Shopping Malls to grow on 5x5 and 6x6 size LOTs.

## Trivia

The name of the Building's exemplar is ChiFashionCentre[^1], implying that this building was originally only meant to appear in the Chicago tileset.
