The **Public Parking Garage** is a Transport building that provides Car services, added in the [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) expansion pack.

Parking Garages are designed to allow Sims to park their cars to board Mass Transit. They are required for Sims looking to drive to a [Bus Stop](/bus-stop), [Elevated Rail Station](/elevated-rail-station), [Subway Station](/subway-station) or [Monorail Station](/monorail-station) to be able to transfer correctly. The transfer from Car to Pedestrian is one-way only, so it is not unusual to see Sims using Parking Garages taking a different, All-Car route home from work in the evening.

## Bugs

The parking capacity stated in the catalogue is far lower than the actual capacity.
