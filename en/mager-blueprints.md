The **Mager Blueprints** is a Medium-Wealth Commercial Offices Building[^1] that grows on all densities of Commercial zones.

[Chicago Boutiques 3](/chicago-boutiques-3) uses the same model sets and can be considered a Commercial Services variant of this building.

Mager Blueprints is, aside from the number of jobs, functionally identical to [Mellon Inc.](/mellon-inc) and [Zilber Communications](/zilber-communications).
