The **Open Paved Area** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Beautification and Recreation for your [Sims](/sims).

Parks provide a [Park Effect](/park-effect) over a fixed radius, in addition to usually cleaning up air and water pollution and increasing [CAPs](/cap).

Plazas in particular are designed for commercially-dominated areas. Instead of reducing pollution, they carry a hefty [Landmark effect](/landmark-effect-2), usually on-par with a much larger and more expensive landmark.

The Open Paved Area is designed to fill holes in commercial areas or in larger plazas.
