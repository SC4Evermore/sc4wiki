**Ng Place** is a High-Wealth Residential Building that grows on All Residential zones[^1].

## Reference Buildings

**Ng Place** is likely based on [Joseph Raphael De Lamar House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Raphael_De_Lamar_House) in [Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York), completed in 1905. Aside from the front of the building being copied to form the back, the recreation is very accurate.
