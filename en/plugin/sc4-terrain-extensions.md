The **SC4 Terrain Extensions** is a DLL plugin for [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) that adds custom terrain tools. You use the tools through cheat codes.

## Features

- The `earthbender` cheat gives command-line tools: a Constant Grade tool, a Flatten tool and a Bridge Approach tool.
- The Constant Grade tool makes a path with a constant slope between two points.
- The `bridgebuilder` cheat gives a drag tool for bridge approaches. You change its settings with the scroll wheel and modifier keys.

## Requirements

- SimCity 4 version 641, on Windows 10 or later.
