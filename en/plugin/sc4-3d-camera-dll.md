The **3D Camera DLL** is a DLL plugin for [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) that lets you change the camera angles of the game freely. It does this through two cheat codes.

## Features

- The `CameraYaw` cheat sets the azimuth angle. The default is 22.5 degrees.
- The `CameraPitch` cheat sets the altitude angle, for all zoom levels or for each zoom level separately.
- Allows views such as a top-down view or an isometric view like SimCity 3000.
- Graphical glitches may occur, especially at extreme angles.

## Requirements

- SimCity 4 version 1.1.641 (the digital release), on Windows 10 or later, or Linux.
