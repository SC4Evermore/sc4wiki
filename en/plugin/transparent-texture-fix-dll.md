The **Transparent Texture Fix DLL** is a DLL plugin for [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) that fixes the underground view bug, also called the water bug.

## Features

- Fixes the bug on lots that use overlay textures without a base texture.
- The bug appears after you leave the underground view.

## Requirements

- SimCity 4 version 1.1.641 (the digital release), on Windows 10 or later, or Linux.
- The Microsoft Visual C++ 2022 or later x86 Redistributable.

The DLL is also available in [sc4pac](https://memo33.github.io/sc4pac/channel/?pkg=memo:transparent-texture-fix-dll) as `memo:transparent-texture-fix-dll`.
