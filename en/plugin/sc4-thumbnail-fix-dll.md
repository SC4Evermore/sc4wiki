The **Region Thumbnail Fix DLL** is a DLL plugin for [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) that fixes a rendering bug in the region view thumbnail of a city. The bug affects large screen sizes.

## Features

- Fixes the bug that occurs with a vertical screen resolution larger than 1024 pixels.
- Without the fix, saving a large city tile renders only part of the terrain. This leaves a visible gap in the region view.

## Requirements

- SimCity 4 version 1.1.641 (the digital release), on Windows 10 or later, or Linux.
- The Microsoft Visual C++ 2022 or later x86 Redistributable.

The DLL is also available in [sc4pac](https://memo33.github.io/sc4pac/channel/?pkg=memo:region-thumbnail-fix-dll) as `memo:region-thumbnail-fix-dll`.
