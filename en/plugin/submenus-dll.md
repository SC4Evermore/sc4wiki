The **Submenus DLL** is a DLL plugin for [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) that adds submenus to the building menus of the game. Other plugins that are compatible with submenus need this DLL.

## Features

- Enables submenus. Plugins can put their buildings and flora into a submenu.
- Adds more than 50 standard submenus, such as Plazas, Green Spaces and Sports.
- Fixes menu rendering problems that duplicate or missing menu icons cause.

## Requirements

- SimCity 4 version 1.1.641 (the digital release), on Windows or Linux.
- The [Resource Loading Hooks DLL](/plugin/sc4-resource-loading-hooks) version 2.0.1 or later.

The DLL is also available in [sc4pac](https://memo33.github.io/sc4pac/channel/?pkg=memo:submenus-dll) as `memo:submenus-dll`.
