The **SC4 Plop and Paint** is a DLL plugin for [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) that adds an in-game panel to browse and plop lots and to paint props and flora. You open the panel with the `O` key in a loaded city.

## Features

- Has five tabs: Buildings & Lots, Props, Prop Families, Flora and Flora Collections.
- Paints props and flora one at a time, in a line or to fill a polygon. Paint mode has grid snapping and undo.
- Builds weighted prop families to paint a random mix of props.
- Removes placed city, lot and street props with a strip mode.
- A separate cache builder program scans your plugins. Run it again when your plugins change.

## Requirements

- [SC4 Render Services](/plugin/sc4-render-services) version 0.8.1 or later.
- The Microsoft Visual C++ 2015-2022 x86 Redistributable.
- The cache builder needs 64-bit Windows. A native Linux build of it is also available.
- The DLL also runs under Wine or Proton on Linux.
