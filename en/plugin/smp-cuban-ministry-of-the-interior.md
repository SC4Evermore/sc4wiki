The Cuban Ministry of the Interior is located in Havana, at the Plaza de la Revolución. The ministry shows the large mural, based of Alberto Korda's famous photo of Ché Guevara. The text reads 'Hasta la Victoria Siempre' (Forever Onwards Towards Victory).
