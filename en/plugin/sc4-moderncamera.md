The **SC4-ModernCamera** is a DLL plugin for [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) that changes the game camera into a mouse-controlled camera, like in modern city builders.

## Features

- Pans the camera with WASD. Hold Shift to pan faster.
- Rotates and tilts the camera when you drag with the middle mouse button.
- Zooms smoothly with the mouse wheel.
- Hold ALT to snap to the nearest classic camera angle. This is still experimental.
- A settings button in the game switches between Modern and Classic camera mode.

## Requirements

- SimCity 4 Deluxe (Rush Hour) version 641, on Windows. This is the Steam, GOG and EA App release. Disc and retail versions are not supported.
