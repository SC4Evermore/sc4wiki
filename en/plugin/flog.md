FLOG (Freakish Links Of Gophers) is a modular golf course country club developed by [NHP](/nhp) founder [Jplumbley](/people/jplumbley). The set was released on the [LEX](/lex) in April 2007.

Included in the set:

- NHP FLOG Clubhouse Vol 1
- NHP FLOG Holes Vol 1
- NHP FLOG Fillers Vol 1
- NHP FLOG Paths Vol 1
- NHP FLOG Clubhouses Vol 2 (released May 2007)
