The **Small Fire Station** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides [Fire Protection](/flammability) for your [Sims](/sims).

Fire Stations reduce flammability by a variable amount dependent on their funding over a variable radius which can be adjusted using the Vehicle Funding slider in the building's Query Panel.

The Small Fire Station is the cheapest Firefighting solution in SimCity 4, with a monthly cost manageable for most towns, some dispatchable engines and a reasonable coverage area.

It is positioned below both the [Large Fire Station](/large-fire-station) and [Fire Department Landing Strip](/fire-department-landing-strip) in Firefighting power and facilities. The Large Fire Station is a direct upgrade.

## Bugs

It produces electricity. It probably should not do that.

## Trivia

The original name for this building was probably the **2-Engine Station House** before being changed to **Small Fire Station** as its exemplar still contains this name.
