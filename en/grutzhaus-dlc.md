The **Grutzehaus**, also known as **Gtutz House** and **House of Grutz**, is a downloadable [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a purely fictional building.

No News Ticker fluff entry is created on its placement.

## Trivia

This was one of two buildings made by Maxis that was made entirely in the [BAT](/bat) as part of its testing process. The other is [Grutz Industries](/grutz-industries-dlc).
