**The Bank of China Tower** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [Bank of China Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_China_Tower_%28Hong_Kong%29) in [Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central%2C_Hong_Kong), [Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong), completed in 1990.
