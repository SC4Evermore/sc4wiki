The **Medium City Hall** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides a Landmark Effect and some Civil jobs to your [Sims](/sims).

Government Buildings are markers of your city-building prowess and usually supply Civil Service jobs and a Landmark Effect.

The **Medium City Hall** was probably intended to be an automatic direct upgrade of the [Small City Hall](/small-city-hall) at an arbitrary population point and upgraded to the [Large City Hall](/large-city-hall-unimplemented) itself at another arbitrary point.

## Unlock Requirements

### All Difficulties

- With MrTruesage's Fix
  - **Combined Population** in excess of **31,000 Sims**
  - **Mayor Rating** above **45**

## Bugs

Only the Small City Hall was ever given a reward script by Maxis. The Medium and Large city halls are both functionally complete. It appears that it was originally intended to automatically upgrade in the way that Airports and Seaports can. MrTruesage at Simtropolis fixed this by implementing them as three separate lots.

With custom props installed, a single badly-made tree on the lot can become any of of the custom props installed. This bug was fixed by Andreas Roth at Simtropolis.

Links for both fixes can be found in the Infobox.
