**The White House** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of The [Executive Residence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Residence) in [Washington, DC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington%2C_DC), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), as it appears after 1829.
