The **Latin American BAT Team**, known as **LBT**, is a team of mainly bilingual speaking (Spanish and English) people who try to create realistic Latin-American and American custom content. An associate of American Bats by Victor Gonzales.

### Team History

LBT started as a big challenge project in November 2006, a project that was initially started by [Heblem](/people/heblem) and [Berethor](/people/berethor), The project consists of recreating Mexican cities building styles, major [landmarks](/landmarks) and well know commerces etc, and ending the project by making everything proposed, this project was temporally until we decided to make a team that formerly was know as [MBT](/mbt) (Mexican BAT team).

Due the incorporate of American BATS and many other well know members as [Victor Gonzales](/people/victor-gonzales) and [tcx](/people/tcx), in December, 2006 we had to look for a new design and name for the team, thats where in January we asked [Jeronij](/people/jeronij) for a new home in [SC4Devotion](/sc4devotion) and of course the logo design by [callagrafx](/people/callagrafx). But not until March, 2007 when LBT section was created.

A discussion started in some threads at [CSC](/csc) talking about our new home and of course the name, for a moment we were thinking to keep the name of MTB, but later it changed to LBT by a public poll.

The team started developing many [BATs](/bats) such as apartments, hospitals, fire stations, police stations, hotels, homes, well know landmarks and much more, mainly bats from [Mexico](/wikipedia-mexico), [Venezuela](/wikipedia-venezuela), [United States](/wikipedia-united-states) and [Panama](/wikipedia-panama).

### American BATs

American BATs was created by [Victor Gonzales](/people/victor-gonzales) in 2005, this team is an essential key of LBT, under this team we can recreate American and Canadian buildings, principal collaborators are [Victor Gonzales](/people/victor-gonzales) and [Heblem](/people/heblem).

### Team goal

Our team goal is to give the best custom content, specially to the people of the Americas.

### Expansion Pack Project

The main goal as like LBT was formed in the beginning is to create a replica of the latin american cities, as one of our main goals is to create a FREE download expansion pack, specially designed for build a 100% latin american city. It proposed to be completed until 2010.

### LBT Files sites

Mainly the main sites where LBT creations are located is at [Simtropolis](/simtropolis) [STEX](/stex), [CSC](/csc) [Zona de descargas](/zona-de-descargas) and at [BSC LEX](/bsc-lex). There are some exclusive creations at the mentioned sites, such in [CSC](/csc) many hotels buildings and apartments by [Heblem](/people/heblem), and at [LEX](/lex) the pyramid pack by [Berethor](/people/berethor), but the preferred site by the LBT is the [STEX](/stex) due many members personal reasons.
