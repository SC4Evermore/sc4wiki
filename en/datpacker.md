The **DatPacker** is a a freeware program created by to compress the files in the My Documents\Plugins folder, to help speed up load times and reduce [Crash To Desktops](/ctd), a new version, conveniently named *DatPacker 2008* was released recently, and uses new ways of compressing, to allow for even bigger increase in loading time.
