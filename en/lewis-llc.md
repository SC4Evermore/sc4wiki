**Lewis LLC** is a Medium-Wealth Commercial Office Building that grows on only High Density Commercial zones. [^1]

## Reference Buildings

Lewis LLC's tower is likely based on that of the [Saint Paul City Hall and Ramsey County Courthouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Paul_City_Hall_and_Ramsey_County_Courthouse) in [Saint Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Paul%2C_Minnesota), [Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota), completed in 1932.

It is unknown what building the base was derived from.
