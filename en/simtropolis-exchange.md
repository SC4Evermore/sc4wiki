The **Simtropolis Exchange**, most commonly referred to as **STEX**, is a open custom content exchange on [Simtropolis](/simtropolis). This exchange is open to all talents and allows a custom content creator to upload a map, prop, custom building, modification or a tool. A Rating System was installed to enable users to give a rating on their favourite uploads. You can receive as an uploader up to ten "stars" with the rating system. Currently, the STEX Top Contributors are [SimGoober](/people/simgoober), [Pegasus](/people/pegasus) and [ill tonkso](/people/ill-tonkso), with 277, 242 and 226 uploads, respectively.
