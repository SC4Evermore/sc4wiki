The **Grassy Pasture** is an Agricultural Industry Field building that grows on Low Density (Agricultural) Industrial Zones[^1].

Field buildings are grown when construction on an [Agricultural Anchor](/category/i-a-growables/anchors) begins and greedily fill all connected and unoccupied Agricultural zones.
