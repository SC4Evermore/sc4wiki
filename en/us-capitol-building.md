The **US Capitol Building** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of the [United States Capitol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Capitol) in [Washington, DC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington%2C_DC), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), constructed in 1800, roughly as it appeared in 2003.
