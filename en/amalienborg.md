**Amalienborg** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Amalienborg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amalienborg), the winter home of the Danish Royal Family, in [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen), [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark), completed in 1760.

## Bugs

Only the base building on the lot (Brockdorff's Palace/Frederick VIII's Palace; top-left in the infobox image) will respond to the query tool. The cause is probably that the Query As property on the props used for the other three palaces and the Frederick V statue are set incorrectly.
