**Walker's Island** (French: Îles de Walker) is an affluent island nation in the southern Pacific Ocean.It concists of several groups of islands, which all are famous for beaches with white sand surrounded by cerulean blue ocean waters. The islands were first sighted by Christian Frederikson on May 13, 1526. Frederikson was a mariner and explorer of the mighty and powerful Frederikson Family, a wealthy trading dynasty from the [Zollernmark](/lars-and#39-md). The nation is ruled monarchisticly by the Walker Family.
