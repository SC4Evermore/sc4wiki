**Ajjanagadde Inc.** is a Stage 6 High-Wealth Commercial Office Building that grows on both Medium Density and High Density Commercial zones.

## Trivia

This building is likely one of the many in the game named after a member of SimCity 4's production team. It is probably named for Venkat Ajjanagadde, SimCity 4's Co-Architect and Engineer (Basically the second Programming Lead).[^1]

This building uses the same models as [Houston Boutiques 5](/houston-boutiques-5), basically acting as a Commercial Office version of that building.

This building is present on two different versions of 2x3 lots, one of which is only able to grow where there is a road running along the right hand side of the LOT. [^2]

This building is one of the many game buildings that feature advertising, generally for The Sims.
