There is no game with the title **SimCity 5** (SC5), although the name has been used for years to denote the potential sequel to [SimCity 4](/simcity-4).

Two games in the series have been released since SimCity 4, however neither is considered a sequel to that game, nor is named SimCity 5. The first post-SimCity 4 release was [SimCity:Societies](/simcity-societies), which was the 5th PC game released under the SimCity brand. [SimCity (2013)](/simcity-2013) is scheduled for release in 2013, and is billed as a reboot to the series, not an extension or sequel to SimCity 4.
