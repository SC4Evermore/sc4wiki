The **Medium Municipal Airport** is a Transport building that provides Air services, designed to provide Commercial CAP Relief.

Airports provide Commercial Office Cap Relief proportional to their Usage.

The Medium Municipal Airport is the second lot in the Munincipal-class Airport chain, preceded by the [Small Municipal Airport](/small-municipal-airport) and succeeded by the [Large Municipal Airport](/large-municipal-airport).
