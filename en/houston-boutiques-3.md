**Houston Boutiques 3** (Named in the game as just **[Boutiques](/boutiques)**) is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that only grows on High Density Commercial zones.

## Trivia

Houston Boutiques 3's Exemplar Name indicates that it was meant to only be available in the Houston Tileset.
