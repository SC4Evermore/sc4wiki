The **budget** screen is where you manage your city's money. It is a flyout screen in the lower right corner of the user interface, that can be minimized to show a summary, or expanded for a detailed view of the city's finances. The budget screen allows you to:

- Raise or lower taxes,
- Set funding for city services, such as schools or hospitals, either individually or as a group,
- Turn on and off [ordinances](/ordinance),
- Manage [Business Deals](/business-deals) and [Neighbor Deals](/neighbor-deals),
- Take out a [loan](/loans).
