**Larva Breeding** is a High-Tech Industrial Outbuilding that grows on Medium and High density Industrial Zones[^1].

Outbuildings are grown by SimCity 4's Industrial Zones attached to an Anchor Lot, designed to fill out the entire zone. Outbuildings are grown last and are designed to fill the spaces between [Anchor Lots](/category/i-ht-growables/anchors) and [Machinery](/category/i-ht-growables/machinery) lots.

### Reference Building

**Larva Breeding** may be based on a Bag-Style [Anaerobic Digester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaerobic_Digester), though a Bag-style digester would not be used on the scale depicted by Larva Breeding in SimCity 4.
