**Houston Boutiques 2** (Named in the game as just **[Boutiques](/boutiques)**) is a High-Wealth Commercial Services Building that grows on all Densities of Commercial zones.

This building is part of the group of generic High-Wealth stores grouped under the Boutiques name.

## Trivia

This building is in a Building Family with the [Holistic Health Center](/holistic-health-center), meaning that they both use the same LOTs and either may appear when a lot made with this building is called by the game.
