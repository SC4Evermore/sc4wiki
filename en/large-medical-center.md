The **Large Medical Center** is a [Civic Building](/civic-buildings) that provides Healthcare for your [Sims](/sims) in SimCity 4.

Clinics increase your city's Health Quotient and your Sims' Lifespans depending on their funding over a variable radius which can be adjusted using the Vehicle Funding slider in the building's Query Panel up until there are no more free capacity.

The **Large Medical Center** is the second most expensive and effective Healthcare building in SimCity 4, with a stronger effect than the [Medical Clinic](/medical-clinic), but a weaker overall effect than the [Disease Research Center](/disease-research-center).

## Trivia

This building has a [Christmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas) easter egg associated with it, where Christmas-themed decorations appear on the lot when the system clock is set to December 25th.
