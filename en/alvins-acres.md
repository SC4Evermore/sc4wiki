**Alvin's Acres** is an Agricultural Industry Anchor building that grows on Low Density (Agricultural) Industrial Zones[^1].

Anchor Buildings are the first buildings grown by SimCity 4's Industrial Zones, designed as a hub for all connected Fields, Machinery Buildings and Outbuildings. They are the only Industrial buildings that must be directly adjacent to a transferable [Transit Network](/transit-network) (eg. Road or Avenue).

## [Colossus Addon Mod](/colossus-addon-mod)

With the Colossus Addon Mod installed, the number of jobs provided by this building is increased to 38. It is otherwise unchanged.
