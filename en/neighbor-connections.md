**Neighbor Connections** are connections that you make within a city tile to an adjoining city tile. Usually, these connections show a yellow arrow pointing towards the neighboring city. Neighbor connections are required to initiate [Neighbor Deals](/neighbor-deals). Neighbor connections also allow [Sims](/sims) to commute to other cities, and are one way to export freight and provide [cap relief](/tutorial/demand-supply-and-caps#demand-satisfied).

Many types of network can be used to create a neighbor connection, including:

- Road
- Avenue
- Highway
- [RealHighway](/realhighway)
- Rail
- Subway
- Monorail
- Elevated rail
- Water pipe
- Transmission line
