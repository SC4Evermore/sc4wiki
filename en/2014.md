## January

- 14th - [NAM Team](/nam-team) releases [NAM](/nam) Version 32, coinciding with [SimCity 4](/simcity-4)'s 11th birthday.

## February

- 5th - [haljackey](/people/haljackey)'s [Building A City from Scratch](/building-a-city-from-scratch) ends with Part 100.

## March

- 18th - EA [Maxis](/maxis) releases Patch 10.0 for [SimCity (2013)](/simcity-2013), allowing the game to be played fully offline.

## April

- 10th - [Aspyr](/aspyr) re-releases their port of [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) Deluxe for Mac OS X, with Universal Binary support.

## May

- 6th - [Aspyr](/aspyr) launches re-released [SimCity 4](/simcity-4) Deluxe port for Mac OS X on Steam.

## July

- 28th - [NAM Team](/nam-team) releases [NAM](/nam) Version 32.1, a Mac-only update, designed to address crash-to-desktop issues with newer versions of OS X and the [Aspyr](/aspyr) port.
