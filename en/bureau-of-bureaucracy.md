The **Bureau of Bureaucracy** is a [Civic](/civic-buildings) Trophy [Reward](/rewards) Building that serves as an icon of bad City Services management.

The facade of the building appears to be based on that of [Chicago City Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_City_Hall) in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago), [Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois), although this has not been confirmed.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Line Forms To The Left (And Right) At New Bureau Of Bureaucracy".

### Gameplay

#### All Difficulties

- **Combined Population** in excess of **39,000**
- **Average Mayor Rating** in excess of **30 Points**
- **Overall Education Department Funding** at or above **101%**
- **Overall Health Department Funding** at or above **101%**
- A **[Courthouse](/courthouse) has been constructed in the city**
