**Zilber Communications** is a Medium-Wealth Commercial Offices Building[^1] that grows on all densities of Commercial zones.

If it existed, Chicago [Retail Stores](/retail-stores) 9 would be the Commercial Services equivalent of this building.

Zilber Communications is, aside from the number of jobs, functionally identical to [Mager Blueprints](/mager-blueprints) and [Mellon Inc.](/mellon-inc).
