**Smith Tower** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) in SimCity 4.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Smith Tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_Tower) in [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle), [Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_%28State%29), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), completed in 1914.
