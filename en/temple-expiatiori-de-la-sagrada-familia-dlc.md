The **Temple Expiatiori de la Sagrada Familia** is a [Landmark Building](/landmark) for SimCity 4 [Rush Hour](/rush-hour)/Deluxe, released as the **Plugin Pack 00F** free Downloadable Content.

It provides no services aside from a Landmark Effect and Mayor Rating boost.

It is a recreation of [Sagrada Familia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagrada_Familia) (full name **Basílica i Temple Expiatori de la Sagrada Família**) in [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona), [Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalonia), [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain) as it appeared in the 1970s, 1980s or 1990s.

The full name in real life and that used in the game differs as it was consecrated as a Minor Basilica in 2010, seven years after the plugin was released.

The News Ticker fluff entry created on its placement is "Sagrada Familia Strikes Locals as "Very Gaudi"".

## Trivia

The building was originally going to be a 12x12 tile lot according to the exemplars. The reason it was scaled down to only the main building is not known.
