**Lind Entertainment** is a High-Wealth Commercial Office Building that grows on High Density Commercial zones.

It is designed to compete with [Pedriana Pharmaceuticals](/pedriana-pharmaceuticals) from the Chicago Tileset, [Cahalane Communications](/cahalane-communications) from the Houston Tileset and (Provided [Rush Hour](/rush-hour) is installed) [Hutt Enterprise Headquarters](/hutt-enterprise-headquarters) from the Euro Tileset.

## [Colossus Addon Mod](/colossus-addon-mod)

When the Colossus Addon Mod is installed, All lot variants are changed to Stage 10. It is otherwise unchanged.

## Reference Buildings

Lind Entertainment appears to be mostly based on [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago)'s [One North LaSalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_North_LaSalle), however the structure of the building is closer to that of [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City)'s [Woolworth Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woolworth_Building) .
