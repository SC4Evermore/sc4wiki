**Go Gyro Plant** is a Manufacturing Industrial Anchor Lot that grows on Medium and High density Industrial Zones[^1].

Anchor Lots are the first building grown by SimCity 4's Industrial Zones, designed to provide both a destination and spawn point for the game's traffic simulation. Due to this, they are the only Industrial buildings that require direct [Transit Network](/transit-network) access. [Machinery Lots](/category/i-m-growables/machinery) and [Outbuildings](/category/i-m-growables/outbuildings) are grown adjacent or near to the Anchor lot to expand the Anchor Lot's capabilities, as well as to allow industrial growth away from Transit Networks.

## Colossus Addon Mod

When the Colossus Addon Mod is installed, the number of Jobs is increased to 33 and the small lot variants are changed to Stage 3.
